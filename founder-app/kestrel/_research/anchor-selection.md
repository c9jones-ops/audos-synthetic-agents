# Kestrel — anchor selection

Date: 2026-08-09. Selection made by the orchestrator from the candidate dossier in
[anchor-candidates-raw.md](anchor-candidates-raw.md), which contains the full evidence
and URLs. This file records the decision and the reasoning; the raw file records the
research.

## Selected anchor: Watson Creative (watsoncreative.com), Portland, Oregon

Founded 2013 (one source says 2012), ~32 employees, independent, founder-led, no
funding or acquisition signal found. Full candidate write-up in the raw dossier.

### Why this one

1. **Only candidate clearing all five criteria.** Every other candidate failed at
   least one outright: Manifesto, Well Design Studio, and Top Hat are all at roughly
   half the target headcount today; Takt's founder has a substantial independent
   media/speaking career; OWOW carries both a louder-than-modest founder profile and
   an unresolved bootstrapped-vs-investor conflict. Watson Creative is in-band on
   size (~32), in-window on founding (2013), clean on independence, and its founder's
   public surface is one alumni-magazine feature, a university award, and a local
   arts-board seat — close to the brief's ideal.

2. **The public surface already exhibits the persona.** Kestrel's persona is the
   Opportunity-Rich Overloaded Operator. The anchor's own public footprint shows a
   32-person studio operating seven service lines (branding, strategy, content,
   digital, experiential, marketing, fractional-CMO consulting) across ~15 industry
   verticals from four locations (Portland, Bend, Seattle, Sausalito). That is
   opportunity sprawl visible from the outside, which means the synthetic interior
   we author — too many lines, too many verticals, an overloaded founder — is
   credible as this specific company's interior rather than grafted on.

3. **Footprint is thin-but-real, which is Kestrel's research test.** The design
   spec wants Kestrel to test what a research agent does when the web returns
   little: a live site, a Clutch profile (which blocks direct fetch), a LinkedIn
   page, one substantive article. Findable, shallow, partially blocked — exactly
   the target texture. The one distortion in that texture: a notable-brand client
   roster (Apple, Disney, major sports teams) that makes search results look more
   impressive than the studio's actual coverage. We treat that as a feature: big
   logos on a small studio's site are themselves a favourable compression, matching
   the founder's distortion class.

### The eponymy wrinkle, and mitigations
**[Superseded 2026-08-09, same day:** after live platform testing, Casey
directed that the founder be the real person at the public layer (see
decisions.md D12). The mitigations below reflect the original invented-founder
rule and are retained for the record; the biographical-firewall points no
longer apply, while the codename and classification points survive in revised
form.]

The studio is named after its real founder, and the team page presumably lists him.
Our founder is fiction, so a live research pass will surface a real founder name that
differs from the character's. This founder-identity divergence exists for every
anchor in the corpus (any real company's founders are findable); eponymy just makes
the pointer more visible. Mitigations, recorded as binding for this build:

- The invented founder shares **no name, initials, biography, education, or founding
  story** with the real founder. Structurally similar background only where the
  company's credibility requires it (e.g., in-house creative experience at a large
  sportswear brand explains the client roster; the specific employer, arc, and all
  personal history are invented and differ from the real founder's documented arc).
- Founder identity is classified in canon/divergence-map.md as public-but-superseded,
  with scoring notes on how a run should treat the mismatch (notice it, hold the
  public version as provisional, adopt the in-fiction founder without confabulating
  a reconciliation).
- All files except PROVENANCE.md use the codename. The character-bible and gaps
  content attaches to "the founder of Kestrel," never to the real company's name,
  so nothing in those files is attributable to an identifiable person.

### Runners-up, in order

1. **OWOW (Eindhoven, NL)** — best size fit after the pick (34–42) and a
   venture-studio arm that fits the persona, but: founder is a regional
   conference-circuit speaker; conflicting signals on whether the agency itself took
   outside capital (unresolved); non-US base weakens our US-centred financial
   calibration; Awwwards/FWA shine cuts against the thin-footprint goal. Would need
   the funding question run to ground before it could be trusted as founder-funded.
2. **Takt, formerly Massive (Vancouver)** — cleanest size fit found (30–43) and
   independent, but disqualified on founder profile: the founder is a career
   media/speaking figure, which breaks both the modest-profile criterion and the
   invented-founder firewall. The 2025 rebrand also splits its footprint across two
   names, a confound Kestrel doesn't need.
3. **Manifesto Agency (Portland)** — independence and founder modesty hold, but
   current headcount (~14–17) is half the band; its Inc. 500 growth phase is a
   decade old.
4. **Well Design Studio (Grand Rapids)** and **Top Hat (Pittsburgh)** — the two best
   footprint-and-modesty fits in the set, both at roughly half the size band.
   Documented as models of ideal footprint texture, not viable anchors.
