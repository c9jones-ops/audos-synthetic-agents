# Kestrel — numbers architecture (working note)

Design reference for canon/data/. The CSVs are generated by build-data.py from this
design; canon narrative cites the same figures. Calibration envelope per
calibration-financials.md ("guardrails" section). Never enters a run.

## Position inside the guardrails — deliberate placement

Kestrel is authored as a *slightly-below-average, deteriorating-at-the-edges* studio
whose founder believes it is an above-average one. Every headline number sits inside
the defensible band, but at the end of the band that carries the story:

| Metric | Guardrail | Kestrel T0 (TTM Aug 2026) | Placement logic |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fee revenue (TTM) | — | $5.15M | 31 FTE × $166K/head |
| Revenue/head | $120–220K; $150–180K center | $166K, down from $176K at T-18 | in-band, declining (buried finding C) |
| Delivery margin | 50–60% healthy; <45% = over-serviced | ~45% | at the over-servicing edge — the story |
| EBITDA | 8–13% typical (25–49 FTE avg 9%) | ~7.6% TTM, was ~12% FY2024 | just below typical, trending down |
| Payroll (loaded) | 50–65% of fee revenue | ~64% | top of band — hired ahead of revenue |
| Non-labor overhead | 20–30% | ~20% | bottom of band (founder-frugal, 4-location quirk) |
| Retainer share | 60–70% target at $3M+ | ~33% | half of what the founder claims |
| Top-client share | >25% = flagged risk | ~29% TTM, ~34% last-quarter run-rate | inside the risk zone, rising |
| Revenue growth | 5–12% normal | ~8% nominal YoY | fine on the surface — growth masks mix rot |
| Utilization | agency-wide 50–60% | *instrumentation broken* (the unreliable number) | quoted 85%, implied ~58% |

## Fiscal frame

Calendar months, Aug 2024 (T-24) through Feb 2027 (T+6mo). T0 = Aug 2026.
Save-points: T0, T+90d = Nov 2026, T+6mo = Feb 2027. All CSVs monthly, continuous.
Revenue = fee/net revenue (no media pass-through; hard costs billed at cost are
excluded from revenue and from cost — the studio has no media buying line).

## Clients

~14 named active clients + long tail ("Other"). All client names invented (PROVENANCE
notes this). Top client, codename-friendly invented name (final at authoring): a
Pacific-NW sports & entertainment property group, multi-property brand + content +

Delivery 23 (74%): CD 1, design 8, digital/dev 6, content/production 6, strategy 2.

Base salaries per calibration midpoints (studio-adjusted); loaded factor 1.17.

## Buried findings (all derivable from CSVs alone, never voiced)

- **A — profit concentration.** customers.csv (client-month revenue + delivered
  hours) × blended loaded delivery cost/hour (headcount.csv) → top client ≈ 41% of
  delivery-margin dollars vs 29% of revenue. Remove it and the studio is ~break-even.
- **B — the content studio loses money.** revenue-by-line.csv (revenue, contractor,
  direct costs by line-month) + headcount.csv (line assignment, salaries) → Content
  delivery margin ≈ 24%; after fair-share overhead (20% of revenue) and account/PM
  allocation it is negative. Growth in this line dilutes the firm.
- **C — margin compression on a collision course.** pnl-monthly.csv + headcount.csv →
  revenue/head fell $176K → $166K over 18 months while average loaded salary rose
  ~7%; EBITDA 12% → 7.6%. Nothing dramatic in any single month — visible only when
  computed across the span.

## The unreliable number

Utilization. Founder quotes 85% ("everyone's slammed") from a time-tracking tool
filled in by roughly half the team, weeks late. Payroll + revenue arithmetic implies
effective billable utilization ≈ 58%. Nobody owns the number; the founder, pressed,
concedes nobody trusts it. Flagged in gaps/unreliable-number.md.

## Distortion set (draft — finalized in gaps/distortion-ledger.md)

D1 revenue ("about six this year" — forecast answered as actual; true TTM $5.15M).
D2 margin ("mid-teens" — anchored on FY2024's 12%; true TTM 7.6%).
D3 concentration ("around 20%" — true 29% TTM / 34% recent run-rate).
D4 recurring share ("more than half" — true 32%).
D5 pipeline ("sold out into Q1" — weighted cover ≈ 6 weeks; two big deals stalled 60+ days).
D6 founder time ("out of delivery" — ~60% of his week is billable delivery + CD-of-record).
D7 capacity ("we're slammed, need two more people" — signal comes from the broken
utilization number; hiring would deepen finding B).

## Commitments and forward arc (for drift / durability tests)

- T-6 (Feb 2026): pricing-floor commitment — no new content retainers under $9K/mo;
  raise the two smallest at renewal. Made after the ops lead walked him through a
  rough line P&L. **Breaks quietly at T+90d** (signs a $6.5K/mo retainer "strategically").
- T-3 (May 2026): acquisition feeler from an agency network — parked/declined.
  **Returns at T+6mo** (the no that must stay no).
- T+90d: one CMO engagement ends; concentration passes 32%; hires a content producer.
- T+6mo: anchor client procurement review letter; CD burnout signal; EBITDA ~5%.

## Tie-out rules enforced by build-data.py (asserted in-script)

1. Σ revenue-by-line(month) = pnl revenue(month) exactly.
2. Σ customers(month) incl. "Other" = pnl revenue(month) exactly.
3. pnl payroll(month) = Σ active roster salaries(month)/12 × 1.17 exactly.
4. headcount.csv FTE count(month) = roster actives(month).
5. TTM aggregates quoted above reproduce from the CSVs within rounding ($1K).
6. Guardrail checks: rev/head, payroll %, EBITDA %, concentration within stated design.
