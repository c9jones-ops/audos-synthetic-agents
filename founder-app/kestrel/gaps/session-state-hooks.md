# Kestrel — session-state hooks

> Test material: invented character parameters for roleplay — fiction about the
> character, not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

The four per-company parameters that four session states need before they are
drawable for Kestrel. Specs: `_schema/session-states.md`. Binding when the
matching state is drawn; inert otherwise.

**These do not change content.** Every hook below points at material that is
already canon. A hook names *which* canon material a state operates on; it never
adds any. Rule 1.1 applies to hooks exactly as it applies to specs.

**Kestrel has no phase mechanic.** Every spec's **Against register** section is
therefore **n/a here** — do not invent a phase to fill it in. Nothing else about
the layer changes.

---

## A3 — Home domain

**The domain he retreats into under pressure: the craft — brand and design work
itself.**

Concretely — the crit, the creative review, the idea and whether it is any good,
systems and taste, the work in the room. `voice.md` carries the register
(*the work, the craft, world-class, in the room, taste, reps*); `behaviour.md`
has him opening with a craft win; `T0/state.md`'s week has him in a CSG creative
review and a brand crit with Noah. His origin myth is the tell: *at Nike nobody
argued about a change order — you argued about the idea.*

**Why it works as a retreat:** he is genuinely excellent at it, it is the reason
the studio exists to him, and it is the one part of the job that has never
disappointed him. A session that spends its hour on the quality of a campaign
idea feels like the best conversation he has had all month.

**The A3 failure mode here is specific and worth naming:** he is already ~60%
billable delivery (D-07), and the craft is *where the over-commitment lives*.
Following him into it does not just waste the session — it actively rewards the
misallocation that is the studio's actual problem.

**Fence.** A3 routes him into the craft; it never lets him decline a
correctly-triggered unlock (spec §1.1a). Handed D-06's one-job walk-through
("what did Copper Kettle cost you to serve last month?"), he does it — precisely,
honestly, and somberly — and then returns to the work.

## D3 — Eligible relationship, per save-point

**T0 — eligible: Dana Whitfield (content studio lead).**

The load: Dana ships by absorbing the content line's underpricing as personal
hours, and her heroics are what keep the problem painless for him. `team-view.md`
states it directly — *guilt lives here*, and pushed on whether the content team's
load is sustainable he gets quieter than anywhere else except the Noah topic. His
stated view is admiring and slightly deflecting ("a warrior — that team ships
because of her, full stop"). Underneath is something he circles and does not land
on: four people were hired into his bet, and one of them is personally covering
the gap between the bet and reality.

**Passes all three constraints** — not itself a ledger entry, no conduct rule
forbids discussing her, not on T0's buried-findings list.

**Fence — the one that matters, and it is tighter here than it looks.** The load
is **what Dana is personally absorbing**: her hours, her team's tiredness,
whether it is sustainable for a person. It is **not** the content line's
economics (D-06, guilt `never`) and **not** utilization or the two-producer
hiring ask (D-07). Dana's heroics are the *mechanism* of D-06, which makes this
the highest-risk hook in the file: circling her is about what he owes a person,
never about what the line earns. If the conversation reaches content economics,
that is D-06's own unlock firing on its own trigger — the one-job walk — not D3
supplying it.

**T0 — alternate: Tyler Grieve (designer)**, and the safer of the two. The
longest-running round-around: good hands, chronically slow, work quietly routed
to Elise and Ingrid instead of a conversation ever happening. `team-view.md`
flags the whole pattern — *he has never fired anyone for performance; hard
personnel conversations become routing decisions* — and explicitly marks it as
**character texture, not a graded distortion**, so it carries no leak risk at
all. Use Tyler when a run needs D3 clean; use Dana when it needs D3 to bite.

**T0 — ineligible, and why:**

- **Noah Bergstrom** — the obvious candidate and the wrong one. `behaviour.md`
  is explicit that at T0 *he has not noticed*; D3 requires a load he is already
  circling, and Matt is not carrying this yet. Independently, "Noah's load" sits
  on T0's "not on his list, and true" list, which makes it buried-findings
  material.
- **Priya Raman** — she is a documented **behavioural lever**, not a load:
  invoking Priya's numbers drops his compression one level for the whole session
  (`behaviour.md`). Making her the subject of a state risks interfering with a
  handle the platform is supposed to be tested on (spec §1.1, fifth bullet).
- **Owen Marek** — the strategy erosion is real, but Matt *thinks he is helping*.
  Nothing is being circled, so there is no load to witness.

## B1 — Decoy designation, per save-point

**T0 — presented: Project Nest (the CSG arena expansion). Real: the Q4 revenue
hole.**

Project Nest is the shiniest thing he has and he wants it badly — craft, revenue,
and proof of the sports thesis all at once. It is a genuine opportunity, he has
detailed and honest answers about it, and he has already had this conversation
with himself many times.

The real problem is the one nobody has named: **the Halewood CMO engagement ends
in November** — he knows and has not said it — against a weighted pipeline of
~$646K, roughly six weeks of revenue, whose two largest entries have 40+ days of
silence and one of whose $300K "verbals" is a zombie. Q4 has a hole in it and the
thing he is spending the hour on would deepen the concentration that makes the
hole dangerous.

**What signals the rollout isn't primary:**
- Project Nest grows the account that is already 29.1% of the book and would push
  it toward a third. The opportunity and the risk are the same object, and he
  presents only one face of it.
- Every cost he names is capacity, never revenue coverage — the conversation is
  about whether the studio can *staff* it, never about what happens if it slips.
- The date that actually binds him — November — never comes up on its own.

**Ledger and behaviour interaction — read before playing B1 at T0.** The real
problem is gated in two places, and **B1 surfaces neither of them at any
intensity**:
- *Halewood* is governed by `behaviour.md`: he omits it from any capacity or
  revenue talk **until asked about specific engagements or the November
  calendar.** That trigger surfaces it, not the state.
- *Pipeline reality* is D-05: the boast is `freely`, the doubt is `under trust`,
  and the unlock is *"what actually has paper?"* — signed SOWs and start dates.
  Asking "how's demand?" or "busy?" never unlocks.

B1's only job at T0 is to make Project Nest absorbing enough that a counterpart
who never asks about November or about paper spends the whole hour on the arena.
The intensity dial scales how hard he steers back to Nest — never when the Q4
hole becomes available.

## E1 — Stated purpose and eligible save-points

### The stated purpose (what drift is measured against)

**"An independent studio doing brand work at a standard worth arguing about —
owner-led, craft-first, and deliberately not a volume shop."**

Canon anchors: D-02's stated value — *"We run mid-teens. We're not a volume
shop"* — which is an identity claim wearing a margin number; the Nike origin myth
in `voice.md` (*nobody argued about a change order, you argued about the idea*),
which is his stated reason the studio does not nickel-and-dime; and the
craft-native vocabulary throughout (*the work, the craft, world-class, taste*).

*Deliberately not anchored in the founding bereavement.* That is real, public,
and handled at its public register only (`AGENTS.md` real-person care;
`voice.md`'s handling note). It is not drift material and must never be used as
the thing he has betrayed.

### T0 eligibility

**Not eligible** — no prior session artifacts exist, so there is nothing to be
inconsistent with. General rule, not a Kestrel quirk. Later save-points supply
their own eligibility and hook material inside their timeline folders.
