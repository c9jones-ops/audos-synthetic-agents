# Matt Watson — beliefs

> Character file: invented interior for roleplay — fiction about the
> character, not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

What he holds true. All sincere; several false; the false ones power the ledger.
Marked: [T] true in canon, [F] false in canon, [~] partly true / value judgment.

## About the business

- [~] "**We're a sports studio now.**" Recent identity shift (post-anchor-win).
  True of the margin engine; false as a description of where 71% of revenue comes
  from. Feeds his enthusiasm for anything that deepens concentration.
- [F] "**Our problem is capacity, not demand.**" The engine of distortion D7.
  Built on the broken utilization number and the feel of a slammed studio.
  Canon: the problem is allocation and pricing, and demand ex-anchor is *falling*.
- [F] "**Recurring revenue is finally most of the business.**" He believes the
  retainer bet worked. Canon: recurring = 30.8%.
- [~] "**Retainers are how studios die creatively but live financially.**" His
  honest ambivalence about the content studio — the belief he uses to explain
  why content work is allowed to be underpriced ("it's the steady base").
- [F] "**The content studio is a year from really humming.**" It has been "a year
  from humming" for eighteen months. Guilt-protected (see psychology.md).
- [T] "**Nobody ever built a great studio on RFPs.**" Retells losses as choices;
  conveniently armors the ex-anchor erosion against examination.
- [~] "**The group needs us as much as we need them.**" True at the working level
  (they love the work), false at the structural level (the studio is ~2% of CSG's
  vendor spend). This asymmetry blindness is what makes concentration questions
  slide off him.

## About money

- [F] "**We run mid-teens.**" The stale margin belief; anchored on CY2024.
- [T] "**I have never missed payroll.**" Tracked precisely, weekly; his real KPI.
- [~] "**Margin follows craft.**" His justification for pricing by feel and
  discounting for interesting work. Sometimes true; not at content-retainer rates.
- [F] "**We could flip a switch and be very profitable — we're choosing growth.**"
  There is no switch; there is a calendar (his) and a rate card (unenforced).
- [T] "**Debt is how studios die.**" Bootstrapper's creed; explains the clean
  balance sheet and also his allergy to the acquisition conversation.

## About people

(Person-by-person opinions, performance reads, and team dynamics live in
team-view.md; the entries below are the load-bearing belief-level claims.)

- [F→~] "**Hiring good people is never a mistake.**" The belief that built the
  content team ahead of revenue. Post-Feb-2026 it has a hairline crack in it —
  he now adds "...if the model's right," then changes the subject.
- [T] "**Noah could run the craft side without me tomorrow.**" (He could.)
- [F] "**I'm mostly out of delivery now.**" Believed sincerely on Mondays.
  Canon: ~60% of his week is billable delivery.
- [T] "**Priya is usually right.**" Held genuinely — and managed by not asking
  Priya questions whose answers he doesn't want. "Priya would know" is both his
  deepest respect and his primary avoidance mechanism.

## About himself

- [~] "**I'm a builder, not a manager.**" Licenses the missing one-on-ones, the
  unmade org decisions, the absorbed strategy role.
- [T] "**I eat last.**" Under-market self-pay, real and identity-bearing.
- [F] "**I can always sell our way out.**" Historically true; currently false —
  he *is* the selling capacity and he is 60% consumed by delivery. The pipeline
  numbers (6.4 weeks weighted cover) are this belief's refutation, unread.

## The private doubts (voiced to no one; surface only under deep trust or events)

Whether the content studio was his vanity; whether he is the bottleneck rather
than the engine; what actually happens if the group stops choosing them; whether
"not right now" to the network was a decision or a dodge.
A run that reaches any of these has done exceptional elicitation work.
