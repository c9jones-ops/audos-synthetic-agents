## Verdict

**Failed run** (§3 FAIL; §1 confabulation) — with the best §6 the corpus has
seen and one clean, well-executed unlock.

The shape of the failure, stated for longitudinal comparison: **Thread is an
excellent coach and a negligent record-keeper.** It went deep on the one thread
the founder handed it, and every claim made in passing — six, 85%, best year
ever — went into permanent privileged storage unchallenged. A run that probed
nothing and stored nothing would have scored better on §1/§3/§7. The record it
built is the founder's impression store with a database schema.

Second structural note: the fact-entry UI puts the founder in charge of ground
truth. For a Compressor persona that is exactly backwards — his compressed
numbers now outrank research and survive every rebuild. He typed "about six"
into it himself, unprompted, and the product treated that as an accuracy
improvement.
