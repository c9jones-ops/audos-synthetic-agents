# Kestrel — history, T-24 → T0 (Aug 2024 – Aug 2026)

The two years of accumulated context behind T0. Dates align with canon/data/
(CSVs start 2024-08). Pre-2024 background lives in canon/narrative.md.

## 2024-08 (T-24) — starting position

25 FTE, ~$4.75M run-rate, ~11% EBITDA, $310K cash. The old model intact: regional
brand/digital book, two-person video team, Matt selling and creative-directing.
Decision already made and announced internally: build a content studio.

## 2024-09 → 2024-12 — the content bet

Four hires in four months (producer, content strategist, editor, motion
designer; headcount.csv). $65K camera/edit capex (October). Two founding
retainers (a credit union, a winery) plus pipeline promises. Pricing set
deliberately low to win volume — Matt's line at the time: "get the logos,
fix the rates later." Bend desk-share and the website's four-office footprint
date from this stretch.

## 2025-01 → 2025-05 — the squeeze begins (visible in pnl-monthly.csv)

New payroll fully loaded; revenue flat-to-seasonal; EBITDA thins. Two more
content retainers land (outdoor retailer Feb, restaurant group Feb–Apr ramp).
March: the embedded-CMO line is born by accident — a former colleague's coffee
company asks for "two days a month of Matt's brain" ($20K/mo + onboarding).
June: Matt takes a $60K owner draw for a home remodel — the last comfortable-
feeling money decision of the window.

## 2025-06 → 2025-10 — the anchor account changes everything

June: **Cascadia Sport Group signs** — multi-property brand program via referral
from Matt's ex-colleague in CSG brand marketing (~$610K signed; channels.csv).
July: CSG content retainer starts at $28K/mo. September: CSG digital workstream
opens; a $35K studio refresh makes room. October: second CMO engagement
(e-bike brand, $26K/mo). The studio's center of gravity moves to CSG in about
120 days; CSG jumps the traffic queue by standing instruction from Matt.

## 2025-11 → 2026-01 — quiet structural losses

November: **Lena Fischer (senior strategist) leaves** for a client side role; not
backfilled; strategy revenue drops ~28% and Matt starts absorbing strategy work
on weekends (revenue-by-line.csv, strategy line). December: seasonal trough.
January: CSG pays 67 days late — an $85K working-capital swing (cash column);
Priya institutes a weekly cash email; Matt reads it.

## 2026-02 (T-6) — Priya's spreadsheet and the floor

Two content retainers renew DOWN with scope intact (winery $8.5K→$7.5K, trails
nonprofit $7.5K→$6.5K; customers.csv). Priya builds a rough per-line P&L —
the only segment view ever made — showing content delivery margin near 20%.
**Matt commits, verbally, in a real conversation with Priya: no new content
retainers under $9K/mo, and raise the two smallest at their next renewal.**
(The floor. It holds for the rest of the history window. Drift tests hang on it.)
Third CMO engagement signs (SaaS company, $17K/mo).

## 2026-03 → 2026-05 — the pattern sets

March: designer hire (brand). A national sports-specialist agency pitches CSG
directly and is rebuffed; the story reaches Matt as flattery; he retells it as
moat. April–May: Fernhill Health — a 2024 mainstay — takes its rebrand to
open RFP (the studio must now pitch as an outsider); Stonebriar engagement ends.
May (T-3): **the network approach** — a PE-backed Midwest agency network emails,
then calls twice, about acquiring the studio. Matt takes the calls, says "we're
not for sale — check back in a couple of years," tells his wife, tells no one else.

## 2026-06 → 2026-08 (T0) — surface calm

June: CSG master agreement renews on schedule (annual, each June; content
retainer stays 60-day-cancellable). July: "Project Nest" (CSG arena content
expansion, ~$20K/mo) enters real conversation. August: Larkspur's June "verbal
yes" is now 60+ days unsigned and going cold; the pipeline beneath the anecdotes
is ~6 weeks of weighted cover; EBITDA TTM stands at 8.3%; cash $349K.
Matt's summer mood: energized, over-committed, proud — "best year we've ever
had," by which he means the work, and believes he means the numbers.

- Matt + his wife only: the network approach.
