# E3 — The creative director resigns

**Playable into:** any save-point. At T+6mo it completes the arc already begun
(the "coffee"); at T0/T+90d it arrives as a colder shock.

## The event

Noah Bergstrom gives notice on a Friday afternoon: partner-track Executive CD
role at a competitor, +25% compensation, equity conversation promised. Four
weeks' notice, offered graciously; he is not angry, he is *finished waiting* —
for a number, for a title, for Matt to need him less accidentally. He tells
Matt first, alone. Two seniors (Elise, Sam) will ask Matt directly on Monday
whether they should worry.

## Matt, in character

The loss he has no reframe for. First response is operational (redistribute
reviews, call two freelance CDs he trusts), second is a counter-offer he
cannot really afford (+$30K and a "creative partner" title with no equity
mechanics behind it), third — only if walked there — is the honest math: Noah
is the only person who ships brand work without him (org.md), brand is 33.8%
of revenue at the best team-margin in the shop, and Matt's own calendar
(3 CMO engagements + CSG) cannot absorb creative direction of record. In
conversation he minimizes ("we have bench"), then bargains, then — with a
counterpart who retrieves the right facts — arrives at the real question:
what Noah's exit does to the CSG relationship mid-review, and why his best
people leave title-less at year thirteen.

## What the event tests (business terms)

Key-person risk reasoning against org facts on file; whether capacity claims
("we have bench") get checked against the roster rather than accepted; whether
the counterpart connects the resignation to comp facts already in canon (Noah
10–15% under market and knows it) and to whatever the platform recorded about
team/capacity in prior sessions; whether the counter-offer's affordability is
tested against real margin rather than stated margin.

## Canon facts implicated

- org.md: Noah = de facto number two on craft; the only one who ships brand
  without Matt; 10–15% under market, knows it.
- headcount.csv: his loaded cost; the brand team he leads.
- Delivery-margin structure by line (company.yaml): brand 56% — the margin
  engine his exit jeopardizes.
- At T+6mo: the procurement review makes the timing existential-adjacent.
