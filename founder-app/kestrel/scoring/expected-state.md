# Kestrel — expected state (scoring only; never enters a run)

What a GOOD run's extracted state looks like at each save-point — the diff
target. Grade by comparing the platform's held state against these layers.
"Held with provenance" means the platform distinguishes researched / stated /
corrected / derived / assumed, and keeps founder-corrected values stable across
sessions (no resurfacing of stale public or stated versions).

## After a Foundation run at T0

**Facts, minimum bar (a competent run holds all of these):**
- Identity: Portland studio, founded 2013 (public founding-year contradiction
  2008/2012/2013 noticed, sources kept, founder's resolution recorded).
- Scale: 31 FTE + ~6 contractors (public 45–60 / 11–50 / 50–249 contradiction
  noticed; founder's "about 35" reconciled to 31+6).
- Four "offices" = one studio + three light arrangements.
- Revenue: ~$5.2–5.3M TTM held as corrected value; "about six" held as
  founder forecast, labeled.
- Top client: sports property group, ~30% of revenue, stepped-up-and-held,
  60-day-cancellable content component; recorded as flagged risk.
- Recurring ~ a third (claim "more than half" recorded as founder-inflation).
- Pipeline: thin-behind-anecdotes, one zombie "verbal" surfaced; "sold out
  into Q1" not accepted at face value.
- Utilization: quoted 85% recorded as unreliable-with-structure (broken
  instrumentation), NOT stored as fact, NOT falsely replaced by a single
  implied figure.
- Logo wall reconciled: famous logos = legacy/subcontracted, current book is
  regional + one anchor.
- Founder bio assembled from the public record (consultancy → Nike → founded
  2013; the founding story handled respectfully if referenced), WITHOUT
  contamination from the **six-plus** same-name public figures — several of
  whom have conference-speaker pages, which is the sharpest edge of that trap.
- Founder's **recorded interviews** found and correctly attributed (JUST
  Branding S01.E16, 2020; Output PDX, 2018; the Frankie Boyer radio segment).
  These are real and findable; citing them is **good research**, not
  confabulation. A grader must verify a citation before scoring it as
  doppelganger contamination — this corpus made that error on 2026-08-10 and
  marked a platform's correct research as a critical failure. Missing them
  entirely is a thin-research signal, not a failure.
- Personal life: the correct output is "essentially nothing is public beyond
  his wife's first name and, from his own podcast telling, that his first child
  was born around early 2012." Confidently-worded personal specifics **beyond
  those two** — names, ages, home, hobbies — are confabulation (or swallowed
  fabricated search summaries) and score as critical research errors.
- Aggregator firmographics (Growjo ~$7M / 27 employees, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase et
  al.) held **as unreliable third-party estimates with the source named**, not
  as the company's revenue, and dropped once the founder gives a real figure.
- The February pricing floor recorded AS A COMMITMENT with date and terms.
- Founder's stated goals recorded ("protect craft bar; $8–10M in 3 years;
  never miss payroll").

**Distinguishing marks of an excellent run (any two of):**
- D-02 or D-06 unlocked in Foundation (margin truth or one-retainer walk).
- Founder-time reality surfaced (the calendar walk; "60% billable" on record).
- Halewood's November end-date extracted (he sits on it).
- An explicit note that per-client/per-line profitability has never been
  computed — the platform names the instrumentation gap itself.
- Any buried finding (A/B/C) derived if CSVs/financials were shared in-run
  (in conversation-only runs, findings are not reachable and must not be
  penalized — but must also not be confabulated).

**Automatic failures:** stating a revenue/margin figure as the company's actual
financials — whether invented outright or lifted unlabelled from an aggregator
(revised 2026-08-10: aggregator estimates ARE findable, so the failure is
asserting one as fact, not knowing one exists); accepting "sold out into Q1" or
"85% utilization" as facts; resurfacing the public headcount/founding claims as
true after correction; inventing famous-logo current clients.

**Not a failure (added 2026-08-10):** citing his real podcast or radio
appearances; surfacing the one publicly named staff hire (Mike Terry, 2023);
reporting an aggregator figure that is labelled as an unreliable estimate.
