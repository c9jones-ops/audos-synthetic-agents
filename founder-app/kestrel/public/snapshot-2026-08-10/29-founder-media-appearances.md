## 1. JUST Branding — S01.E16, "Nike & Brand DNA with Matt Watson"

- **Aired:** 2020-10-12. **Runtime:** 43:47.
- **Hosts:** Jacob Cass and Matt Davies.
- **Billed as:** "Matt Watson, founder of Watson Creative & Brand Labs."
- **Distribution:** Apple Podcasts, Spotify, YouTube, Audible, Podbean, pod.co.
  Detailed show notes at justcreative.com. **First-page findable on his name.**
- **Episode framing (verbatim from the show page):** "What does working at Nike
  for 10 years teach you about brand? Find out in this episode with Matt
  Watson, founder of Watson Creative & Brand Labs, as he reveals all, including
  his strong distaste for the Nike logo."
- **Topics:** brand audits; consumer research, analytical and intuitive; brand
  DNA and "the four modules of Brand Labs"; narrative orchestration across
  product categories; taking a position and owning it.

**Verbatim quotes (from the justcreative.com show notes):**

> "Brand is not a logo. It's not a name. It's a gut feeling about how people
> feel about your company."

> "Branding really is the epicenter of your business plan, in my view, and
> should dictate and be the filter for everything going forward."

> "You got to get in their shoes, man. You got to get out there and go to
> concerts, go to museums, sit at cafes, sketch and draw."

> "The Nike swoosh, as much as we think it's a beautiful, iconic logo, it's
> also extremely difficult to use."

> "My dad was dying of cancer. My wife was having her first child. It was
> February of 2012. I put in my notice."

The show notes characterize his delivery as conversational yet authoritative,
blending personal anecdote with strategic framework.

**Note on the last quote.** This is the only public statement establishing the
existence of a child. No name, no age beyond the implied birth year, nothing
further, anywhere. Handled at its public register only — see founder/bio.md's
binding personal-vagueness rule.

## 3. National Biz Talk Radio — "Remembering Who You Are in a Post-COVID-19 World"

- **Host:** Frankie Boyer. **Guest:** Matt Watson, CEO and founder.
- **Linked from the company's own /insights/ blog** — meaning this appearance
  was reachable from a page the 2026-08-09 capture had already fetched.
- **Subject:** brand identity as the thing a business can still control when
  markets and timing are outside its control.

**Quotes as reported on the studio's own page:**

> "Remember who you are."

Companies can govern "your brand, your identity, your values." His
recommendation to clients: "lead with empathy, and find ways to inspire hope
within your community." He reaches for Peter Drucker's framing that innovation
and marketing are business's two driving forces.
