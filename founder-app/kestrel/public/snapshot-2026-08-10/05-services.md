## The contradiction

`/studio/` (file 04) simultaneously presents a **Strategy / Creative /
Campaigns** staged framework — a different scheme, also labelled with the
"Systems of Meaning" phrase. Both are live. A research pass that reports a
single confident service taxonomy has silently picked one.

## Against canon

strategy/research, and embedded CMO. The last is **absent from the site
entirely** — it is the founder-delivered line and exists nowhere publicly.
Neither public scheme maps cleanly onto the real five.
