   Growth" (2023). Canon's org (canon/org.md) has no such person — the roster
   is fictional by design. Handling rule for the player is in
   founder/behaviour.md: he's someone who moved on, mentioned briefly and
   without a departure narrative.
2. **Real outside press exists**, self-reported but pointing at real outlets:
   The Oregonian profile (2017), an Adobe global feature (2019), George Fox
   University coverage (2016). Thin, mostly a decade old, and not remotely
   equivalent to trade-press attention — but "no press coverage of substance"
   overstated it.

   his MBA is from George Fox University (see file 02).

   and a Quality Business Awards 2025 listing for Portland advertising agencies.
