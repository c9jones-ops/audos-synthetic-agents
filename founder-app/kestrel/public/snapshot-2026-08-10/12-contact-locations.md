for offsites, where "teams who want to think deeply, move fast, and get
aligned" can work "surrounded by fresh air, strong strategy, and a few
volcanoes for perspective." Portland, Seattle and Sausalito carry no

That framing is closer to the truth than the bare address list was — Bend reads
as a retreat venue rather than a staffed office. Canon: Bend is two desks in a
partner firm; Seattle is a coworking membership; Sausalito is a contractor
advisor's home office; **only Portland is a studio**. The four-office
presentation remains the site's classic favourable compression, and the founder
folds on it cheerfully when asked (founder/bio.md).
