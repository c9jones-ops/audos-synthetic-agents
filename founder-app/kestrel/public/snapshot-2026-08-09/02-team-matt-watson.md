## Bio
Watson started his creative career at Lippincott designing for Fortune 50 rebrandings before joining Nike's creative team for over a decade. He shaped product strategy, led evolution of the NIKEiD customization platform, and contributed to Olympic and athlete partnerships. After leaving Nike in 2012 to care for his father during cancer treatment, he transitioned to teaching and freelancing from a Portland attic.

By 2013, this evolved into Watson Creative — described as a multidisciplinary West Coast studio serving brands including Apple, EA Sports, PlayStation, Disney Marvel, and LEGO, alongside nonprofits and government agencies.
