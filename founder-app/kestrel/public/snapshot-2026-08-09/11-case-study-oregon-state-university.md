# Case Study: Oregon State University Athletics

**Client:** Oregon State University Athletics
**Project Title:** College Football Marketing / Oregon State University Athletics Campaign

## Challenge
OSU Athletics needed a comprehensive strategy to secure its future across multiple fronts: increasing season ticket sales, driving donor contributions, repositioning the brand, and amplifying student-athlete programs. A key initiative was the "Victory Through Valley" campaign to expand the Valley Football Center — a 55,000-square-foot project that would double the locker room size and enhance training facilities.

## Solution
Watson Creative conducted comprehensive workshops, brand audits, and persona development to understand OSU's diverse audiences, leading to a unifying messaging framework centered on "One Opportunity." Two primary campaigns emerged:

1. **"Everyday Champions"** — celebrated student-athlete grit, resilience, and academic excellence through authentic storytelling
2. **"Victory Through Valley"** — promoted the facilities expansion project

Creative execution involved filming and photographing athletes at night under Reser Stadium lights to capture raw energy and determination.

## Services Provided
Strategy, Content, Digital, Experiential, Marketing, Consulting

## Results / Metrics
- $3 million raised in the first month
- 25% of contributors increased their support from previous years
- Significant boost in season ticket holder numbers
- Enhanced student-athlete programs through leadership initiatives
- Strengthened connections between fans, donors, alumni, and OSU Athletics
