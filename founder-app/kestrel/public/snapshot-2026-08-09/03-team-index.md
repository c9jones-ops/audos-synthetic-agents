# Watson Creative — Team Page

Note: unlike a typical multi-person team roster, this page (at time of capture) featured only the founder in depth. No roster of additional named employees/team members was rendered on this page.

## Primary Team Member Featured

**Matt Watson — CEO & Founder**

### Bio summary (as presented on this page)
- **Background:** Previously worked at Lippincott (Brand Designer) and Nike (Sr. Creative, 11+ years)
- **Experience:** Shaped product across 43 retail seasons, 5 Olympic Games, and work with 52 professional athletes; led NIKEiD platform development
- **Education:** MBA from George Fox University; Design degree from Oregon State University (page states he turned down a President's Scholarship to RISD)
- **Current roles:** CEO of Watson Creative; Advisory Board member, Oregon State University School of Design; Vice Chair, Oregon Ballet Theatre; Secretary, Regional Arts & Culture Council
- **Teaching:** Lectures at Yale, NYU, University of Washington, Pacific Northwest College of Art, and Oregon State University

### Personal narrative (quoted)
"Watson didn't begin with a business plan, investor pitch, or perfect name. It started in a moment of real life—my father was dying, and I had just left NIKE."

Additional framing: leadership happens "in the room," not behind the curtain, with accountability signaled by having his name on the door.
