# Case Study: Umpqua Bank

**Client:** Umpqua Bank
**Project Title:** Business Banking Marketing

## Challenge
Umpqua Bank sought to elevate its B2C and B2B marketing offerings while reinforcing its identity as a community-focused financial institution. The bank needed tailored campaigns that would resonate with diverse customer segments while strengthening community partnerships.

Quoted framing: "Umpqua Bank isn't your average financial institution. Known for its customer-centric approach and deep community ties, the bank sought Watson's expertise to create tailored campaigns that would elevate its B2C and B2B offerings."

## Solution
Watson conducted extensive research including workshops, surveys, and stakeholder interviews to develop three core customer personas: small business owners, personal banking customers, and community members. This persona-based approach informed all campaign messaging and design elements to ensure relevance across audiences.

Campaign elements included: customer stories emphasizing financial harmony, localized messaging celebrating community involvement, business owner testimonials, educational resources, and geotargeted digital advertising.

## Services Provided
Strategy, Content, Digital, Experiential Design, Marketing, Consulting

## Results / Metrics
- 31% increase in small business banking inquiries
- 23% boost in digital engagement
- Increased community participation at Umpqua-sponsored events
