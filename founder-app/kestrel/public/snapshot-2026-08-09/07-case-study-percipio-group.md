# Case Study: Percipio Group

**Client:** Percipio Group
**Project Title:** Strengthening a Consulting Brand Rooted in the Pacific Northwest

## Challenge
Percipio Group, a Portland-based consulting firm, faced two interconnected challenges. First, their existing branding lacked a cohesive narrative, making it difficult to convey core strengths consistently. Second, their website, while functional, didn't fully reflect their mission or provide an engaging user experience. The firm needed distinct positioning in a crowded consulting industry, with messaging that resonated with business leaders seeking strategic partnership rather than service provision. Internally, they needed reinforcement of company culture to attract top talent.

## Solution
Watson Creative developed a modernized visual identity incorporating regional themes reinforcing Percipio Group's Pacific Northwest commitment. The website underwent a complete transformation with improved navigation, clearer messaging, and a streamlined user experience. The firm created a unified brand voice aligned with company values — "grounded, intentional, and collaborative" — for consistency across presentations, marketing materials, and internal communications.

## Services Provided
Strategy, Content, Digital, Experiential Design, Marketing, Consulting

## Results
The refreshed brand positioned Percipio Group as a go-to consulting partner for strategic business scaling. The updated website increased engagement with improved user interactions and stronger lead generation. Clients responded positively to the refined messaging. Internally, the brand identity reinforced company culture, strengthening employee engagement and talent attraction.
