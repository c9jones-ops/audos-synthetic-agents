# Case Study: Courtesy Lincoln (Lincoln Motor Company)

**Client:** Courtesy Lincoln
**Project Title:** A New Era for Lincoln in Oregon

## Challenge
Courtesy Lincoln, opening Fall 2024 in Portland, needed more than a standard dealership announcement. The dealership required "a strategic brand introduction that aligned with Lincoln's elevated, design-forward philosophy while setting a new standard for automotive retail in the Pacific Northwest."

## Solution
Watson developed a multi-channel launch strategy combining digital and traditional media. The campaign centered on sensory-driven messaging: "Not everyone sees it… The way you see it. The shape. The light. The feel. Experience true, Pacific Northwest Luxury… At Courtesy Lincoln of Portland." This positioned the dealership as embodying Lincoln's philosophy of "sanctuary, seamless technology, and curated design."

## Services Provided
Strategy, Content, Digital, Experiential Design, Marketing, Consulting

## Marketing Tactics
- Cable and OTT (over-the-top) streaming advertising targeting $150K+ household income earners on networks including CNN, ESPN, and HGTV
- Pre-roll video and YouTube TrueView ads
- Polygonal Targeting Attribution to reach luxury car shoppers at competing dealerships (Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Audi)
- Behavioral retargeting and paid social advertising

## Results / Metrics
- 62,000+ impressions through OTT advertising
- 98.14% video completion rate
- "Lincoln Now Open" campaign delivered 45,517 impressions, with nearly all resulting in full video views

## Quote (Matt Watson, CEO, Watson Creative)
"Lincoln is a brand that embodies effortless power and luxury. Our approach was to ensure that Courtesy Lincoln's launch reflected that same level of refinement."
