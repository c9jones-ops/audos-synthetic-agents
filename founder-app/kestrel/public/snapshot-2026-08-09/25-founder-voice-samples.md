## Voice/register observations
Across these samples, a consistent register emerges: short declarative sentences, frequent tricolon/parallel-structure phrasing ("Ethics. Composure. Belief.," "Points and perks aren't loyalty. Signage isn't experience...The bar is coherence."), plain-spoken self-deprecation mixed with confidence ("It's weird stuff. But it's so fun."), and a recurring rhetorical move of naming what something is *not* before saying what it is ("Not X. Just Y." — e.g., "No smoke and mirrors. Just strong thinking and solid tech."). This is consistent across both first-party (his own writing) and company-published quote material, suggesting it's a fairly stable personal style rather than an artifact of one ghostwriter.
