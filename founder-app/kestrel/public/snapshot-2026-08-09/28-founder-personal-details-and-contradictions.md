# Personal Details, Contradictions, and a Note on Search-Tool Reliability

## IMPORTANT: a caution about this capture session's search tool

Over the course of this research session, the web-search tool's AI-generated result summaries **repeatedly introduced specific, plausible-sounding claims that could not be corroborated — and in at least two cases were actively disproved — by direct page fetches.** Examples encountered:

1. A recurring claim that Watson authored a piece called "How to Build a Brand (**Without Losing the Plot**)," attributed to a page at leaderspress.com/our-published-authors/matt-watson/. That URL returned **HTTP 404** on direct fetch. The real, verified article (file 25) has no such subtitle.
2. quotefancy.com/matt-watson-quotes was initially surfaced as a plausible source of his quotes; direct fetch confirmed it is about an **unrelated** "Matt Watson" (comedic quotes referencing "Spy Kids 2").
3. A Forbes Business Council profile surfaced as a candidate press hit; the search tool's own summary correctly flagged it as belonging to a **different** Matt Watson (CEO of Origin).
4. A highly specific personal/hobbies paragraph (see below) that could not be traced to any real, fetchable page.

Given this pattern, every claim below that originates **only** from an AI-summarized search result (not a direct fetch of a primary page) is flagged accordingly, and none is asserted as confirmed fact. This caveat applies to this entire capture batch (files 24–28) and should be weighed by anyone consuming this snapshot.
