# Case Study: Craft3

**Client:** Craft3
**Project Title:** Financial Institution Rebrand: Craft3

## Challenge
Craft3, a financial institution providing accessible loans to underserved communities in the Pacific Northwest, needed a rebrand to reflect its evolving mission and identity as a force for systemic change.

## Solution
Watson Creative conducted immersive workshops engaging staff across all organizational levels — from frontline employees to executives — to understand core values and aspirations. This discovery process revealed that Craft3's essence centers on empowerment rather than transactional lending. This insight shaped the rebrand's messaging and design strategy.

## Services Provided
Strategy, Content, Digital, Experiential Design, Marketing, Consulting

## Results
The rebrand successfully transformed Craft3's community engagement and organizational presentation. The new visual identity and messaging framework rolled out across digital platforms, marketing materials, and community initiatives. Partners and clients responded positively, recognizing enhanced clarity and confidence in the organization's positioning as a dynamic force for social good.
