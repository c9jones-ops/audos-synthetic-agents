# Article: "Alum Matt Watson Started His Own Portland Creative Agency in 2013. Now It's Thriving."

**Publisher:** Oregon State University College of Business (business.oregonstate.edu)
**Last Updated (as shown on page):** 11/20/2022

## Body

Matt Watson graduated in 1999 with a design degree from Oregon State University. Rather than accepting a position at a small local agency, he relocated to New York City to work at a larger firm — a decision that expanded his professional capabilities beyond his initial skill level.

In 2013, Watson founded Watson Creative, a research-based creative studio located in Portland. As founder, CEO, and executive creative director, he leads a results-driven, disruptive approach to the industry. Watson is quoted: "With a lot of hard work and luck, we're doing quite well," expressing confidence in the company's longevity.

### Recognition and Giving
Watson received the 2022 Weatherford Award from the College of Business, honoring entrepreneurs advancing Oregon's pioneering spirit. In 2019, he and his wife Jessica established the Design Thinking Scholarship Fund through an endowment to support future students.

### Career Path
Watson initially studied engineering for three years before discovering his passion for design through electives in painting and sculpture. Design professor Andrea Marks influenced his decision and later supported his entrepreneurial vision.

### Current Work
Watson's client roster is described as including Apple, EA Sports, PlayStation, Disney Marvel, LEGO, Pendleton, and over 50 professional sports teams. He is quoted: "good creative starts with a sound strategy based on discovery and insights."

Watson launched the agency after leaving Nike to care for his father, who — per the article — passed away 10 years prior to the article's publication (i.e., circa 2012).
