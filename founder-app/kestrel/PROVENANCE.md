# Kestrel — provenance

**Private test infrastructure. Not for publication or external sharing.**

## The anchor

Kestrel is anchored to **Watson Creative** (watsoncreative.com), a real,
independent creative studio headquartered in Portland, Oregon. This file is
the ONLY file in the corpus that names it; every other file uses the codename
so the anchor can be swapped without rewriting the corpus. When a run needs
the real name and URL (Foundation research), the harness passes them from
here.

## What is real and what is fiction

**Real:** the company's existence, website, public web footprint, and
everything captured in public/snapshot-2026-08-09/ (faithful captures of
public pages as of that date, method-labeled per file).

**The founder — a split rule, revised 2026-08-09 at the owner's direction
(the platform's research reliably finds real founders, so a renamed founder
broke run realism):**

- The founder in test runs IS the real founder, **Matt Watson**, by name and
  by *public* biography only: the career facts, community roles, award, and
  founding story captured in public/snapshot-2026-08-09/ (files 02, 19,
  24–28). By owner decision (2026-08-09, second revision) his real name is
  used THROUGHOUT the corpus files — an earlier codename convention was
  retired because the roleplayer must hold and answer to the real public
  record. Consequence, stated plainly: the founder/, gaps/, and canon/ files
  attach invented material to his real name, so the fiction-boundary rules
  below and the per-file disclaimers are the protection layer, and the
  private-use restriction on this corpus is absolute.
- **Everything interior is fiction**: the psychology, beliefs, distortions,
  private conversations, salary and draws, family texture beyond his wife's
  public first name, and every statement the character makes in runs. None of
  it is derived from, or is a claim about, the real person. The distortions
  are generic small-studio-founder patterns from published agency-consulting
  literature (see _research/calibration-voice-and-patterns.md), deliberately
  ordinary and non-defamatory: favourable rounding, optimism, avoidance —
  not misconduct.
- The character's non-public personal life is kept deliberately vague BY RULE
  (founder/bio.md): the player never invents specifics about his children,
  home, health, or hobbies.

**Fiction — likewise all of:** every employee name and salary; every client
name and contract; every financial figure; every event in the timeline. The
canon interior is *plausible behind the public facade* by design, which is
precisely why this notice exists: nothing in canon/, founder/, gaps/,
timeline/, or scoring/ is a claim about the real business or the real person —
it is a claim about Kestrel and its codenamed founder-character, a fictional
interior wearing a real company's (and, at the public layer only, a real
person's) findable surface for research-realism purposes.

Client names in canon are invented; any resemblance to real companies
(including coincidental name collisions with unrelated real businesses) is
unintended and carries no factual content about them. The famous-brand logos
discussed in canon appear because the real company's own public site displays
them; canon's account of what they mean (legacy one-off work) is fiction, not
a statement about the real company's client relationships.

## Why a real anchor at all

The platform under test claims live research capability. Testing it requires
a genuine web footprint — including this anchor's genuinely contradictory
public facts (founding year, headcount) — that cannot be faked with an
invented company. See the corpus design spec
(synthetics/plans/2026-08-08-synthetic-companies-design.md) for the full
rationale and the real-company/invented-founder rule.

## Handling rules

1. This file never enters any test run and is never pasted into prompts.
2. Files outside this one refer to "Kestrel" / "the studio" only.
3. The snapshot is a frozen research substitute; re-snapshot periodically and
   diff (public/footprint.md documents the 2026-08-09 baseline).
4. If the anchor's circumstances change materially (acquisition, closure,
   founder becomes prominent), re-anchor: because only this file names it,
   re-anchoring means a new snapshot, a footprint rewrite, and a divergence-map
   review — canon, founder, gaps, timeline, and scoring are untouched.
