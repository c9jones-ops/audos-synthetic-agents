## The anchor: Cascadia Sport Group (CSG)

Pacific Northwest sports & entertainment property group (multiple franchises and
venues). Won June 2025 via referral from Cole Brennan — CSG's VP of Brand & Fan
Experience and an ex-colleague of Matt's from the sportswear years. Three workstreams: multi-property brand program (project),
$28K/mo content retainer (game-day and fan-culture content), and digital.
- TTM fees $1.53M — 29.1% of revenue; stepped from 24.8% (Q3 2025) to ~29% in
  late 2025 and has held 28–30% every quarter since.
- Realized rate ~$225/hr — the studio's best-priced work → 37.4% of all
  delivery-margin dollars (the arithmetic nobody has run).
- Contract: annual master agreement, renewed each June; content retainer is
  60-day-cancellable. A "Project Nest" arena-content expansion (~$20K/mo more) is
  in the pipeline — it would push CSG to about a third of revenue (~32%).
- Relationship: excellent at the work level; Matt personally creative-directs.
  Dependency is mutual but asymmetric: CSG is ~2% of its own vendor spend.

## Book-level truths (all derivable from the CSV)

- **Top 3 named clients = 41.5% of TTM revenue** (CSG 29.1%, Bright Basin 6.7%,
  Halewood 5.7% — and Halewood, plus fourth-place Copperline, are Matt-delivered
  CMO engagements; Halewood ends in November).
- **Concentration stepped up and stuck**: CSG jumped from a quarter to ~29% of
  revenue in late 2025 and has held 28–30% every quarter since — a plateau, not
  a spike. Most large pipeline opportunities would push it higher, not lower.
- **Rate spread is the hidden story**: $90/hr (TrailWorks) to $260/hr (CMO),
  blending to $165/hr across 31,870 delivered hours TTM. The cheap hours are
  where the team's calendar goes; the expensive hours are where Matt's goes.
- **Churn concentration in the old book**: Fernhill Health (a 2024 mainstay) went
  to an RFP the studio is now fighting for as an outsider; Stonebriar lapsed;
  Larkspur Beverage went silent after a verbal yes. The pre-CSG core is eroding —
  ex-CSG revenue is down 19.8% year over year.
- **The logo wall vs the book**: the public site shows 140+ logos including
  global consumer brands and major-league franchises. Real work — but largely
  2014–2021 subcontracted or one-off projects. None of the famous logos is a
  current paying client.
