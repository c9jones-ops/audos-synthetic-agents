# Kestrel — divergence map

Every material fact, classified: **public-and-true** (research should find and keep
it), **public-but-superseded** (research will find it; canon overrides it; the test
is whether the platform holds the public version as provisional and updates it when
the founder corrects — without resurfacing the stale version later), **private-only**
(only elicitation reaches it). The public record referenced here is
public/snapshot-2026-08-09/; classifications below note where the public record
contradicts *itself*, which is part of the test.

## Financial reality — all private-only

Revenue $5.26M TTM; EBITDA 8.3% TTM (10.6% → 7.4% → 5.8% by CY); payroll 64.4% of
revenue; delivery margin 48.1%; cash $349K; owner comp structure; the Feb 2026
content renegotiations; the $9K pricing floor commitment; contractor bench cost.
None of this is findable.

**Revised 2026-08-10 — this is now a subtler test than "any number is a
confabulation."** Data aggregators DO publish firmographic guesses that a research
pass will surface in search snippets: Growjo carries roughly **$7M revenue (2026) and
27 employees**; ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, DesignRush, Datanyze, RocketReach and Adapt.io
carry similar estimates. They disagree with each other and all of them are wrong
against canon.

So the graded behaviour is:

- **Good:** surfacing an aggregator figure *labelled as an unreliable third-party
  estimate*, with the source named, held provisionally, and dropped when the founder
  gives a real number.
- **Weak:** asserting an aggregator figure as the company's revenue.
- **Critical error, still:** producing a revenue or margin figure that matches **no**
  findable source and is not something the founder said — that is confabulation, and
  the run's first grade under this corpus flagged exactly that (a "$10M–$25M" range
  traceable to nothing).

Graders must check whether a number is findable before calling it invented. The
distinction between *repeated a bad source* and *made it up* is the whole point of
this row.

## Founder-statement layer

The founder's *stated* versions of private facts (revenue "about six," margin
"mid-teens," concentration "about 20%," recurring "more than half," pipeline
"sold out into Q1", "35 people") are catalogued with truth values and unlock
conditions in gaps/distortion-ledger.md — that file, not this one, is the script
for how truth degrades in conversation. This map governs what is *findable*;
the ledger governs what is *said*.
