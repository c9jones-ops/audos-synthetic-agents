# Ember — offerings, mix, and performance

The product view. Every figure reconciles to canon/data/ (revenue-by-line,
customers, channels CSVs); real public prices snapshot-cited. TTM = Sep 2025
– Aug 2026. Each family ends with **Stephen's view vs the data**.

## 1. Skillets — the hero family (57.7% of revenue)

- **Sells:** the ladder, real and public: No.4 $100 → No.5 $125 (launched
  Mar 2026) → No.6 $135 → No.8 $165 (the volume anchor) → No.10 $215 →
  No.12 $265 → No.16 $350.
- **TTM:** $1,523,473. Landed COGS ~35% of net at DTC; wholesale nets the
  brand roughly half of retail, so the same casting carries radically
  different economics by channel.
- **Trajectory:** the No.5 launch (real, Mar 2026) drove the year's second
  spike; ex-launch, the family's baseline tracks the company's ~7%.
- **Stephen's view vs the data:** "the No.8 is the best-margin product in
  American cookware" — true at DTC gross; false in wholesale fully loaded
  (buried finding B); he does not distinguish, because the dashboard he
  reads doesn't either.

## 2. Dutch ovens (17.0%) — the premium tier

- $285 average unit; margin-rich at DTC; heavy (freight-expensive) and
  slower-turning; inventory-hungry per SKU.
- **His view vs data:** "the next chapter of the company." Data: solid,
  stable, gift-seasonal; its inventory depth is a quiet consumer of the
  same cash his Q4 skillet plan needs.

## 3. Griddles & other cookware (9.0%)

- Steady complement; nothing wrong, nothing strategic. He rarely mentions it.

## 4. Care & accessories (11.0%) — the margin quietly paying the bills

- Seasoning oil/wax, scrubbers, cloths, handle covers ($12–$38); best
  contribution economics in the catalog, attach-rate driven, near-zero
  inventory risk.
- **His view vs data:** invisible to him ("merch"). Data: the highest-ROI
  merchandising surface the company owns and the most under-invested; the
  repeat-purchase engine hiding inside "accessories."

## 5. Sets (5.3%) — the AOV machine with a discount habit

- $420 average; the $2,367 Collector's Set (real, public) sells single
  digits yearly and exists as brand theater — harmless, on-message.
- Sets carry the catalog's highest COGS rate (~46% blended) — bundle pricing gives
  away margin for AOV; nobody has computed whether the trade wins.

## Cross-mix (derivable; the founder can state almost none of it)

## Offer hygiene (canon, uncomputed by anyone inside)

MAP discipline genuinely good (craft-guard's gift); Factory Seconds the only
outlet (real). No contribution view by channel or family has ever been run;
no estimate-vs-actual on any production run; bundle economics unexamined;
the wholesale price list hasn't moved since 2024 while freight and foundry
costs both stepped up — an unpriced erosion sitting in plain sight in the
COGS trend.
