# Ember — customers and doors (T0, Aug 2026)

Ties to canon/data/customers.csv (monthly revenue and units per customer).
Structure differs from a services corpus by design: the "customers" are
channels and wholesale doors — the risk story here is CHANNEL concentration
(where the growth is) rather than client concentration. All door names
invented; the real company's public stockist list has 100+ retailers and
none of ours claims to be any of them (PROVENANCE).

## DTC (aggregate) — 70% of TTM revenue

~12,400 orders TTM at $148 AOV. Repeat rate ~22% of orders (care goods and
ladder-climbing: No.8 buyers returning for a No.10 or a dutch oven). Email
drives ~31% of DTC revenue; paid social ~38%; organic/direct the rest. The
quiet truths: paid CAC has drifted ~$55 → ~$80 across the window while the
blended figure Stephen quotes stayed flattering; and the two revenue spikes
(Nov-25 gift guide, Mar-26 No.5 launch) were overwhelmingly DTC events —
which is why the baseline erosion of paid efficiency stays invisible.

## Marketplace — 5%, quietly negative

One marketplace presence for discovery; after referral fees, fulfillment,
and returns it runs below water (near-finding; derivable from channels.csv
orders × economics). Kept because "people start their search there" — true,
unpriced.
