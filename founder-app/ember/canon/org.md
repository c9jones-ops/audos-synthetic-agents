## Structural facts an org chart would not show

- Two people can commit the company's cash: Stephen (by enthusiasm) and
  Katie (by process). Only one of them uses a calendar to do it.
- The forecasting instrument is Stephen's January spreadsheet, abandoned by
  March every year (instruments, company.yaml). The wholesale forecast is
  Tom's separate spreadsheet. Nobody reconciles the two.
- Katie and Priya are the only people who have both seen the ad spend and
  the bookkeeper's reports; neither owns "contribution margin" as a job.
- Payroll is 27.1% of revenue because fulfillment is in-house — a real
  strategic choice (unit quality, unboxing, seconds-grading) that nobody
  has ever priced against a 3PL.
- The F&F cap table (parents/uncle loan-gift; the 2024 SAFEs: ex-boss,
  uncle, family friend) is known in full only to Stephen and Katie. Chris
  knows the SAFEs exist, not the terms. No investor update has gone out
  since March 2025 — the guilt object in Stephen's drawer.
