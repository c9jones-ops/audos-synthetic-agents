## Content summary
Origin story: vintage Griswold/Wagner skillets were lighter/smoother than modern cast iron; quality declined in the 1950s as manufacturers cut corners on labor-intensive finishing. The brothers developed a post-casting machining process to restore vintage-like weight/smoothness without hand-finishing labor costs.
