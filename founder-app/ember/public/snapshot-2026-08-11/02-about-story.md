## Origin story (verbatim quotes captured)

- "The company was started by two brothers, Stephen & Chris Muscarella, who named the company after their maternal grandmother—and it was from that side of the family that they inherited a number of vintage cast iron pans from the 1930s."
- "A question was born - why don't they make them like they used to?"
- "Initially, the Field Company was never meant to be a company. We started by wanting to understand all the secrets of cast iron."
- On technical development: the brothers consulted with a European material science professor and "were able to perform microscopic imaging to compare the graphite structure of vintage pans."
