## Content

In 2015, when founding Field Company with brother Chris, Stephen Muscarella explains they sought to honor both family heritage and American cast-iron tradition, citing that vintage cast-iron makers Griswold and Wagner were themselves family-named companies as inspiration for the approach.

**Note on founding year:** this essay states **2015** as the founding year in the summary captured — this is a THIRD distinct founding-year data point alongside the homepage's "2016" and the Iron Anniversary post's "2014 idea / March 2016 Kickstarter." See 00-manifest.md contradiction log.

**On the company's namesake:** the name comes from the brothers' maternal grandmother, **Betsy Field**, known as **"Dindi."** Stephen describes her as embodying the company's core values through her own lifestyle and character.

## Verbatim quotes
- On Dindi's kitchen approach: **"Dindi was eclectic and fearless. Her pantries overflowed with spices and other ingredients that she'd use with utter abandon."**
- The Field family toast, as related by Stephen: **"Here's to us, more like us, damn few."**
- On choosing the company name: **"the Field name really shared all of the qualities we wanted our products, and our company, to embody."**

The essay closes by tying the "Field" stamp on the pans to both the American cast-iron tradition and to Dindi's legacy as a matriarch who brought family together through food and gatherings.
