## Returns & Exchanges (fieldcompany.com/pages/returns)

**Return window (verbatim):** "If you aren't satisfied with your Field Cast Iron for any reason within 45 days of receiving it, you may return the item for a refund, less shipping costs."

**Process:** initiated via an external returns portal; help@fieldcompany.com for support.

**Conditions:**
- Cast iron cookware: returnable within 45 days.
- Accessories: "returned, unused and in new condition only, within 45 days of delivery."
- Exchanges are handled as refund + new order, not a direct swap.
- Factory Seconds: **final sale only**, no returns.
- International orders: **not eligible for return.**

**Shipping cost language:** refunds are issued "less shipping costs" — i.e., return shipping is the customer's expense, not covered by Field.

## Cast Iron FAQs (fieldcompany.com/pages/faqs)

- **Return policy:** same 45-day / less-return-shipping language as above.
- **Warranty:** "For the lifetime of your Field skillet, our team is available to provide guidance." Free product replacements limited to within 45 days of delivery only (i.e., "lifetime warranty" = ongoing support/guidance, not unconditional lifetime replacement).
- **International shipping:** ships to many locations except the EU. UK orders under £135 handled through "Passport Shipping" as the seller of record.
- **EU shipping:** currently **paused**, stated reason: new EU return regulations would require the company to cover full return shipping costs.
- **Retail availability:** select nationwide retailers, via the stockists page (see 04).
- **Manufacturing location (verbatim):** "All of our cast iron cookware is made in the USA" — with operations named in **Indiana, Illinois, and Wisconsin.**
  - Cross-reference: the informal.cc interview (see 22) describes casting in Wisconsin, CNC machining in Indiana, and grapeseed-oil seasoning at a second Indiana facility — Illinois is not mentioned in that piece. Minor discrepancy between the FAQ's three-state list and the informal.cc interview's two-state description — flagged, not resolved.
- **Seasoning:** skillets ship "pre-seasoned with two [coats of] grapeseed oil"; ongoing seasoning builds through cooking use — "The best way to build up seasoning is just by cooking with your skillet."
- **Sticky seasoning troubleshooting:** attributed to excess oil not fully polymerizing; "In most cases, continuing to cook will take care of this, as any oil residue becomes incorporated into new layers."
- **Cleaning:** "Cast iron can rust. That means no soaking, no drip-drying, and never put it in the dishwasher."
- **Induction/glass-top compatibility:** confirmed compatible — "At just 0.04" of clearance, it won't interfere with cooking performance or lose any power from electric or induction heat elements."
- **Outdoor/grill/campfire use:** confirmed supported — "Absolutely — we're huge fans of outdoor and live-fire cooking."
