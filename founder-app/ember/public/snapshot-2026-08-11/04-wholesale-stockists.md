## Stockists page (fieldcompany.com/pages/stockists)

**Inventory disclaimer (verbatim):** "In-store inventory changes frequently. We recommend checking in with stores before making a trip to ensure they have what you're looking for."

**Retailer count:** 100+ authorized retailers listed across the US and Canada, organized by state/province. Each listing includes store name, street address, and phone number.

**Sample retailers captured:**
- California: Bernal Cutlery (San Francisco), New West Knifeworks (St. Helena), Tartine Bianco (Los Angeles)
- Colorado: Blackbelly (Boulder), JAX Outdoor Gear (Fort Collins/Loveland, multiple locations)
- New York: Hudson & Charles (NYC, multiple locations), bluecashew Kitchen Homestead (Kingston), Warren Kitchen & Cutlery (Rhinebeck)
- Texas: Ball and Buck (Dallas), Bering's (Houston)
- British Columbia, Canada: Cook Culture (Vancouver, Victoria) — note this is the same "Cook Culture" brand that hosted Stephen Muscarella's 2020 podcast/Facebook Q&A (see 16, 18)

Links on-page to: Wholesale (for prospective retailers), Corporate Gifting (bulk orders) — both in footer navigation.

## Wholesale page (fieldcompany.com/pages/wholesale)

**Inquiry mechanism:** an "Expand" control reveals a form; applicants provide "your business name, address & which products you are interested in." Company responds with "pricing and more info."

**Program framing (verbatim/paraphrased):** "Get started selling our American-made, vintage-style cast iron" — described as bringing "cast iron back to its rightful place," actively seeking "new retail partners."

**Alternate channel noted on page:** "If you're in the trades of food service, hospitality, or corporate gifting," applicants are directed to a separate Google Form for that program (not itself captured — external form, not fetched).

**Not specified on this page:** minimum order quantity, wholesale pricing/margin, contract terms, lead times — these are stated to be provided after direct application contact, not published.

## Test-relevant read
Wholesale is a real, live, actively-recruiting channel (not just a legacy stockist list) — consistent with the March/April 2016-era origin as a DTC-first brand that added wholesale over time, and with the dossier's "emerging wholesale seam" read.
