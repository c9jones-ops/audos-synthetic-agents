## Verbatim quote (manufacturing philosophy, attributed to Stephen Muscarella)
**"Initially, it was about quality control and wanting to be hands-on in the development process, but over time, our manufacturing partners have become deeply invested collaborators."**

## Content summary
Describes how Field's vendor relationships evolved from transactional interactions into deeply engaged partnerships. The piece attributes improved product quality and better manufacturing-side morale to the domestic-manufacturing approach, and suggests overseas vendors wouldn't foster comparable personal investment in the work.

**Production process detail (new, specific facts not in prior dossier):** Field skillets are manufactured across **three U.S. facilities** — casting in **Wisconsin**, CNC machining in **Indiana**, and organic grapeseed-oil seasoning at a second **Indiana** location. This process reduces the No.8 skillet's weight from a traditional ~8.3 lbs down to ~4.3 lbs, while pursuing vintage-quality cooking-surface smoothness.

**Design philosophy detail:** post-casting machining lets Field control weight distribution independently — thicker bottoms for heat retention, thinner sidewalls for handling — while also controlling surface smoothness for faster seasoning adhesion.
