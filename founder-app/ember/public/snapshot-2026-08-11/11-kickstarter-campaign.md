## Resolves a prior-research flag
`founder-dossier-field.md` §6 flagged a "$475K–$1.6M" figure spread across sources as an unresolved discrepancy. This session resolves it: **the $475,000 figure is a mid-campaign snapshot** — the Yahoo Lifestyle piece quoting it was published March 25, 2016, while the campaign was still running (ended April 6). The **final** total was $1,633,361 / 12,553 backers. Not a contradiction — a snapshot-vs-final artifact.

## Campaign count
Only **one** Kickstarter campaign for Field Company itself was found this session (searched: general web, Kicktraq, BackerKit tracker pages, "second Kickstarter campaign Field Company" targeted query) — **verified: 2026-08-11**. This confirms the prior dossier's correction (one campaign, not two as an earlier research brief had assumed). Product-line extensions (No.10 skillet pour start April 2017, No.12 launch November 2018, etc. — see 05/09 and the Iron Anniversary post) are ordinary post-launch product additions, not separate crowdfunding raises.
