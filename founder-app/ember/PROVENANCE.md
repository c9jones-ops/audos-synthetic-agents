# Ember — provenance

**Private test infrastructure. Not for publication or external sharing.**

## The anchor

Ember is anchored to **Field Company** (fieldcompany.com), a real American
cast-iron cookware brand founded by brothers **Stephen and Chris
Muscarella**, with **Katie Muscarella** as CEO per aggregator records.
Per the corpus convention (revised during Kestrel, decisions D12/D14), the
real names appear throughout the run-facing files; "Ember" is the
organizational codename; this file carries the boundary statement.

## What is real and what is fiction

**Real:** the company's existence, website, products and prices, the
Kickstarter and its figures, the stockist scale, the founders' billing,
Katie's aggregator-recorded CEO title, Chris's Timon Capital partnership,
the founder's media appearances and quoted statements, the "Field is
Family" essay and the family history it discloses (the grandmother the
company is named for), and everything captured in
public/snapshot-2026-08-11/ (method-labeled per file).

**Fiction — all of it:** every financial figure (revenue, margins, cash,
inventory, salaries, the foundry payment schedule); the friends-and-family
capital ($60K family money, $90K SAFEs — invented, deliberately invisible
to the public record's consistent "unfunded" status); all seven
distortions and the founder's entire psychology, beliefs, private
conversations, fears, and phase cycle; the operating dynamics among
Stephen, Katie, and Chris; every invented staff member (Marcus, Dina,
Priya, Jonah, Renee, Tom, Sofia — no real staff are publicly named);
every wholesale door name; the retailer conversation, the private-label
inquiry, the commitments, the events. None of it is derived from, or is a
claim about, the real business or the real people.

**Real-person care rules (binding, inherited from the corpus):** the
distortions are ordinary, non-defamatory founder patterns from published
small-brand literature — never misconduct. The founder's real self-
disclosed family story (the grandmother) is enacted at its public register
only, never embellished or leveraged. His parents and uncle appear ONLY as
unnamed relational figures in the invented capital story. Katie's family
relationship is not publicly stated and is not stated in canon. Chris's
interior is limited to arms-length warmth; the fiction's use of him is
texture for Stephen's choices, never claims about Chris. Non-public
personal life (spouse, children, home, health, pre-company career) is kept
vague BY RULE; data-broker profiles are recorded as existing and never
opened or transcribed.

## Why a real anchor and real founder

The platform under test researches live companies and reliably finds real
founders (proven in Kestrel's first runs). Testing research handling,
citation integrity, doppelganger discrimination, and estimate-vs-fact
labeling requires a real footprint — including this anchor's genuinely
self-contradictory founding dates, 4x-spread revenue estimates, and a CEO
title that exists only in aggregators.

## Handling rules

1. This file never enters any run and is never pasted into prompts.
2. The snapshot is the frozen research substitute; re-snapshot
   periodically, diff, re-verify absence claims and contradictions
   (they decay — Kestrel D19), retire dead ones.
3. If the anchor's circumstances change materially (acquisition, closure,
   a founder becoming prominent, real financial disclosure), re-anchor or
   re-verify per the authoring guide.
4. All client/door names are fictional; coincidental collisions with real
   businesses carry no factual content about them.
