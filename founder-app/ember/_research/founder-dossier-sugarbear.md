  (emilyharpster@gmail.com) and ties her to the program but **does not state
  whether she took a loan, and if so how much** — the page only confirms program
  participation. This is inference-grade, not a sourced financing amount: treat as
  "she went through a microloan/mentoring nonprofit's entrepreneur pipeline,"

## 8. Persona-plausibility test

**Fictional interior to test against:** a hungry, growth-ambitious operator who
extrapolates from best signals, guards product integrity against cost compromise
(scratch-making, real ingredients) with values arguments, and oscillates between
growth euphoria and cash anxiety, funded by shoestring capital plus invented
friends-and-family money.

**Verdict: no material contradiction found. Fit is strong, with friction points
below — all minor and workable, none disqualifying.**

Friction points, however small:

1. **No public cash-anxiety or margin language at all.** Every directly-verified
   quote is emotional/physical ("It's everything," "hustle mode," "busier than
   expected") or values-based ("just milk, cream, eggs, and sugar"), never
   financial. This isn't a contradiction — it's simply an absence — but it means
   the corpus cannot lift real financial-anxiety phrasing from her; the oscillation
   mechanism will have to be built from the category-calibration material
   (calibration-founder-persona.md) rather than adapted from her own words. Worth
   flagging so the builder doesn't over-claim "grounded in her real quotes" for
   the cash-crisis material specifically.
2. **Prior career reads nonprofit-sector, not corporate — but it's real prior
   institutional experience**, which very mildly complicates a pure "she's never
   had a real job" bootstrapped-newcomer framing. A decade at United Way, with a
   stated award for "economic self-sufficiency" program work, means she likely
   *does* have real budget/program-management chops. This is not a contradiction
   of "shoestring capital, hungry operator" — if anything it supports the
   "extrapolates from best signals" trait (a program-builder background is
   exactly the kind of pattern-matching instinct the target interior needs) — but
   it's a step above a from-nothing-to-ice-cream-truck narrative and should be
   used deliberately, not ignored.
3. **The origin sequencing (wholesale-first in 2022, brick-and-mortar only in
