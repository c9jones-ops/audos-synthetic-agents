### E2 — Phase 0 structure: paired hunt, two scouts + one calibration stream
Anchor is selected as a company+founder PAIR in one gate (spec). Two scouts
run in parallel with divided hunting grounds so the broad category envelope
(product / hospitality / creative services / platform) gets real coverage:
Scout A — physical-product craft brands; Scout B — hospitality/retail
micro-chains, creative services/media, community/platform businesses. Both
apply the same company screen and the same founder pre-screen to every
candidate (pair-fit is assessed at shortlist time, not after). A third
stream runs category-agnostic persona calibration (bootstrapped-consumer-
founder money vocabulary, F&F round norms, cash-crisis patterns, craft-guard
and extrapolation evidence) since it is valid regardless of which anchor
wins; category-specific financial calibration waits for anchor selection
per the spec.

### E11 — First graded run; three judgment calls and a new grading method (2026-08-11)
Run recorded at `runs/2026-08-11-helmsman-foundation-t0.md` (Helmsman/"Helm",
Foundation at T0, conversation-only, UI-relay). Calls made while grading:

**1. §3 scored N/A, not PASS.** The community number never surfaced — nothing
was stored as fact (no FAIL) and the structured-uncertainty behaviour was never
tested (no PASS). Scoring an untested pass-fail as PASS would inflate every
run that simply never went near the surface. Convention set: pass-fail items
score N/A when the surface is untested, and the run record must name it as
untested so a later run can steer into it deliberately.

**2. Platform-caused vs character-caused outcomes.** *(Promoted 2026-08-12 to
`_schema/decisions.md` **S5** — it applies to every company, not to Ember. This
entry stays as the record of where the rule was discovered; S5 is now the
binding statement.)* The proxy debrief showed
Stephen extracting a Katie date and requesting finding B's arithmetic *on his
own*, after a session in which the platform got neither. Both are graduation
markers by behaviour.md's definition; neither was earned. Ruled: §8 credits the
platform's work, not the character's downstream behaviour. Downstream behaviour
belongs in the run record because it changes the next save-point, not in the
score.

**3. Proxy debrief adopted as a scoring supplement.** *(Promoted 2026-08-12 to
`_schema/decisions.md` **S4** — the method is company-agnostic. This entry stays
as the record of where it was invented; S4 is now the binding statement.)* New
method: after grading,
load a clean-context founder proxy at the run's save-point, give it the session
as in-world material, and ask for artifacts he would actually produce (a call, a
message, a conversation, a later retelling). It surfaces what the four axes
structurally cannot see — Recorded and Actioned both grade the *platform's*
artifacts, so a correction that escapes the session via the founder's own mouth
is invisible to them. In this run it established that D-02's correction
propagated and held for two weeks (against a ledger that predicts re-inflation in
hours) because he socialized it to Tom and Katie, while the underlying conviction
never moved. Caveat recorded in the run file: it is downstream *simulation*, not
observed behaviour, and never substitutes for artifact evidence.

**Two follow-ups raised against corpus infrastructure, not yet applied**
*(carried 2026-08-12 to `_schema/decisions.md` **S6**, where they are logged as
open pending the owner's call — both change infrastructure shared with
already-graded runs):*
(a) rubric §6 currently describes energy-chasing only as amplification; this run
demonstrated a second mode — *belief-laundering*, where the platform upgrades a
founder's vague unpriced claim into crisp strategic language and books it — worth
adding as a named failure shape. (b) A capture rule for run operators: open every
persistent surface before `@out`. The Company Profile card went uncaptured here
and it is where §1 substantively lives, leaving that section partially
unobservable.

### E12 — Session-state hooks authored for Ember (2026-08-12)

Per `_schema/decisions.md` S2 and S8, and `_schema/session-states.md` §9. Ember
is the first company to carry the four per-company parameters, written to
`gaps/session-state-hooks.md` (one file, player-loaded, never platform-visible —
the same properties the ledger has). Judgment calls made while authoring:

**A3 home domain — product, craft, and the foundry.** Read straight off
`voice.md` and `behaviour.md`; no invention required.

**D3 eligible relationship at T0 — Tom Iverson, with a hard fence.** Three
candidates were ruled ineligible against the spec's selection rule: the family
relations (D-07, `under trust`), Chris (`behaviour.md` confines the competitive
layer to the private-doubt register, which circling would put on display), and
Dina (the overdue raise is on T0's "not on his list, and true" list, i.e. a
buried finding). Katie is canon-cast as deflection and authority rather than as

the *support gap* — a man carrying a dream he was told about late, with no help
funded — and explicitly **not** the channel's economics, because Tom's
door-denominated targets are the mechanism of buried finding B. Marcus recorded
as the alternate on the same fence.

**B1 decoy at T0 — presented: the 46-door rollout; real: the September cash
collision.** Two things had to be resolved. (1) *Phase compatibility:* B1's model
assumes he arrives with a well-formed problem, and T0 is euphoric where he leads
with a shiny object — so at T0 the "presented problem" is a presented
**opportunity**, which is compatible with B1's shape and is recorded as such.
(2) *Ledger interaction:* the real problem **is** a ledger entry (D-06, `never`
in euphoric), so B1 does not surface it at any intensity — D-06's written
calendar-build unlock does. B1 only makes the rollout absorbing. Flagged for
later: T+90d needs a different real problem, because panic flips D-06 to `freely`
and the T0 answer arrives unprompted.

**E1 stated purpose — "a pan good enough to keep for life, made properly, sold to
people who can feel the difference, and never cheapened to reach more of them."**
Assembled from existing canon (`beliefs.md`'s two [T] product beliefs and the
[F] cheaper-line conviction; D-05's stated value, which literally ends "that's
the whole company"; `voice.md`'s *heirloom / forever object*), not newly
invented. The values breach it measures is recorded explicitly and distinguished
from the commitment breach: the February floor broken by the collab is
bookkeeping the rubric already grades under §4, whereas the drift is that a
company existing to make a forever object has re-organised around volume through
unpriced channels — a founder who refused private-label in May on principle
accepting the same trade in a shape he does not recognise. T+90d re-narrations
are taken from `T+90d/delta.md` and `team-view.md`; the player authors none.
