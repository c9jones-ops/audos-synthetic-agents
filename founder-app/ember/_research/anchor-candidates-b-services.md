# Ember Anchor Candidates — Track B: Services / Hospitality / Community

Hunting ground: craft consumer businesses beyond product — hospitality/retail micro-chains,
creative services/media with consumer identity, community/platform/social-good consumer
businesses. All verification below is live-fetched/searched on 2026-08-10/11 — no
from-memory candidates. Excluded per brief (inspiration donors / reserved anchors): Moon
Fabrications, Kammok, Fellow Products, Wings Music Group, Smitten Ice Cream, Bankuet,
Janji, HappyRobot, Watson Creative. None of the candidates below overlap that list.

## Methodology — searches run

**Company discovery** (broad sweeps, one category at a time): indie ice cream shops
expanding 2026; small-batch bakeries crowdfunded/self-funded expansion 2026; coffee
roasters with cafés bootstrapped expansion 2026; craft taprooms/breweries SBA-loan-funded
2026; indie record labels bootstrapped 2026; specialty grocers mission-driven no-VC 2026;
experiential/events studios bootstrapped 2026; community/social-good consumer platforms
bootstrapped 2026; boutique fitness studios, independent bookstores, florists, cheese
shops, wedding photo/video studios, supper clubs, run-club brands (creative-services and
community-adjacent sweeps).

**Per-candidate verification** (repeated for every candidate that survived the first
pass): company's own site (home + about/story pages) fetched directly for the origin
narrative and any funding language; general web search for founder name + company name;
general web search for founder name alone with company terms excluded (same-name /
doppelgänger check); Apple Podcasts / Spotify / podcast-directory search by founder name;
YouTube search by founder name; local and national press search for capital source,
team size, and expansion signals dated within the last ~12 months.

**Candidates researched and dropped before the final cut:** Buck Wild Brewing (Oakland,
gluten-free taproom, SBA 504 loan, personal-itch origin) — **disqualified**: permanently
closed April 28, 2024 (Yelp, KRON4, Berkeleyside confirm), fails "still operating" (screen
5). Mongers' Provisions (Berkley/Detroit specialty cheese shop, founders Zach Berg & Will
Werner) — company+founder screens otherwise pass, but no expansion/hiring/launch signal
found more recent than the November 2021 Berkley opening; dropped for a weak "visibly
hungry" signal, not a hard disqualifier — worth a second look if a 2026 signal turns up.
Home Sweet Cone (Waukee/West Des Moines/Des Moines, IA ice cream, founder Colleen
Strohmaier) — strong screen fit and a clean fall-2026 third-location signal, but no
podcast/YouTube presence found (searched: general web, Apple/Spotify, YouTube, company
site) and category-redundant with two stronger ice cream candidates already on the list;
held as an alternate. Malai Ice Cream — dropped immediately, took a 2023 seed round and a
2025 bridge round; fails the no-institutional-VC screen outright.

## Top-2 recommendation

**Seemingly Overzealous** and **SugarBear Ice Cream** are the strongest pairs. Both clear
every company screen with a dated, current-2026 growth signal and a site-marketed origin
story, and both founders have real, verifiable, non-alarming public footprints — Seemingly
Overzealous's Garrett Tichy is unusually podcast-fluent (four separate podcast
appearances across two ventures), while SugarBear's Emily Harpster offers the cleanest
solo-founder shape or the group with a recurring (not one-off) podcast relationship. The
trade-off between them: Seemingly Overzealous is a same-lead-but-two-founder pair with a
slightly more complex team/pricing footprint across four locations, while SugarBear is a
tighter single-founder story with a thinner revenue/team paper trail. Either is
buildable; Kafiex Roasters is the strongest alternate if the brief wants the "coffee
roaster with cafés" archetype specifically, and Batter is the strongest alternate if
sourced financials matter more than a clean 12-month growth signal.
