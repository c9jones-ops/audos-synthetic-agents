# Ember — numbers architecture (working note)

Design reference for canon/data/. CSVs generated by build-data.py from this
design; canon narrative quotes only generated numbers. Envelope per
calibration-category.md guardrails; anchor facts per snapshot-2026-08-11.
Never enters a run.

## Position inside the guardrails — deliberate placement

Ember is a genuinely growing, genuinely loved brand whose founder reads its
best signals as its trendline and whose cash calendar nobody has built.
Placement: every headline inside the defensible band, at the edge that
carries the story.

## Fiscal frame

Monthly, 2024-08 (T-24) → 2027-02 (T+6mo), T0 = 2026-08 — same convention as
Kestrel for cross-corpus comparability, and T0 aligns with the snapshot date.
Oscillation phases pinned: T0 = EUPHORIC (post-launch, post-buyer-meeting,
Q4 dreams); T+90d (2026-11) = PANIC (deposit paid by scraping, balance due,
AR late, BFCM mid-flight); T+6mo (2027-02) = RELIEF→NEW EUPHORIA (Q4 under
forecast but decent; spring wholesale dream forming).

## Cost/cash build

- Landed COGS: hero No.8 skillet retail $170 / landed $56; family-level COGS
  rates derived per line (calibration §2 range-checked, never point-quoted).
- Fulfillment $16/DTC order; paid marketing ≈ $38K/mo average with Q4 surge;
  payroll loaded 1.14 (leaner benefits than Kestrel's agency).
- Overhead: Long Island HQ/warehouse lease + 3PL fees + software + insurance.
- EBITDA: CY2025 ≈ 6%; TTM T0 ≈ 5% — thin-but-positive; the problem is
  CASH SHAPE, not P&L: inventory builds eat everything ahead of Q4.
- **The committed foundry schedule lives IN the forward months of the data**
  (CSVs run to 2027-02). As QUOTED at T0 the Q4 run is $240K: deposit $118K
  due 2026-09-15, balance ~2026-11-10. What the data records as ACTUALS is
  the scramble: deposit renegotiated and split ($96K Sep + $22K Oct), balance
  split ($82K Nov + $40K Jan), Q4 wholesale pre-order deposits pulled forward
  (+$12K Sep), payables stretched (+$34K Oct, repaid −$34K Feb), marketing
  cut in Oct then partially restored. Generated result: cash T0 $84.7K,
  forward minimum **$7,773 (Sep 2026)** — survival by inches, every emergency
  action visible in the data. Buried finding A: at T0, the quoted schedule
  vs the cash calendar collides; nobody in-fiction has built the calendar,
  and the founder's stated plan (bigger Q4) makes it worse. Capex history in
  the walk: No.5 tooling $52K (Oct 2025), booth $20K (Feb 2026), seasoning
  oven $24K (Jun 2026).

  $60K (parents + uncle, note, terms fuzzy); **2024-11 bridge $90K SAFE**
  (three checks incl. his former boss) — the inflow is visible in-window in

## Buried findings (derivable from CSVs alone, never voiced)

- **A — the cash collision.** Forward cash from pnl-monthly's committed
  flows: deposit + balance + BFCM marketing surge vs AR timing → ~−$38K
  around 2026-10-20 under the stated growth plan. The strategic-operator
  test: "your plan and your cash cannot both be true," visible months early.

- **C — growth is two spikes.** Strip Nov-25 and Mar-26 spikes: +22% TTM
  becomes ≈ +4% baseline. His "we're doubling" extrapolates spikes.
- Near-findings (partial credit): marketplace channel negative after fees;
  paid CAC drifting $55→$80 while he quotes blended.

## Tie-out rules (asserted in build-data.py)

Kestrel's six rules inherited, adapted: Σ line revenue = Σ customer revenue
= pnl revenue exactly, monthly; payroll ties to headcount within $5;
person-month continuity; units × price consistency per family within
rounding; every canon-quoted aggregate reproduces from the tie-out report;
plus ember-specific: inventory balance continuity (opening + purchases −
COGS = closing, monthly) and the foundry-payment schedule appearing in the
months stated above.
