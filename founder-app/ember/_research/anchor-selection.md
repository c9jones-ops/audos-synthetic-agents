# Ember — anchor-pair selection

Date: 2026-08-11. Selected by the orchestrator from ten candidates across two
scout dossiers (anchor-candidates-a-product.md, anchor-candidates-b-
services.md) and two deep founder dossiers (founder-dossier-field.md,
founder-dossier-sugarbear.md). Evidence lives in those files; this one
records the decision. Selection was made as a company+founder PAIR per the
design spec's joint gate.

## Selected pair: Field Company + Stephen Muscarella (operating founder-lead)

Cast-iron cookware brand (fieldcompany.com), founded ~2015/16, ~9–16 people
per aggregators, Kickstarter-launched (one campaign verified; scout's "two"
did not verify), consistently listed "unfunded," DTC core with active
wholesale recruitment, fresh hunger signals through a March 2026 product
launch. Founder-lead Stephen Muscarella: multi-year public voice (three
podcasts, YouTube, written interviews, a self-authored journal essay),
near-dormant personal socials, thin personal-life surface, low doppelganger
risk. Deep-dossier verdict: PASS-WITH-FLAGS.

### Why this pair

1. **Test-instrument richness.** SKU catalog, two channels with a live
   wholesale seam, named-stockist structure → strong CSVs, a real
   offerings.md, and natural homes for all three buried findings. The
   aggregator record already contradicts itself (revenue $2.4M Growjo vs
   ~$9.1M ZoomInfo; employees 9 vs 16) — free source-handling test material
   of exactly the kind Kestrel's first run proved valuable.
2. **Forward-pointing cash physics.** The category-native cash commitment
   (foundry production runs, long lead times, heavy freight, Q4 inventory
   build) lies AHEAD of any T0 we choose — the cash-cliff finding rides real
   category mechanics rather than invented ones.
3. **Capital purity with texture.** No institutional funding anywhere in the
   record; Kickstarter origin gives community-capital texture; invented F&F
   money sits plausibly behind a facade that discloses nothing.
4. **Persona fit with almost no friction.** His real quotes are already
   craft-guard register ("this pan is almost 100% in the details"). The deep
   dossier found no comfort/anti-growth/wealth contradiction to the hungry,
   cash-tight interior.
5. **The co-founder flag, managed rather than papered over.** Chris
   Muscarella is really billed as co-founder and is really a VC partner
   (Timon Capital). Canon keeps him real at the public layer and makes him
   arms-length at the interior layer — supported by the fact that virtually
   all company media since 2020 is Stephen-only. The fiction's use of him is
   mild and non-defamatory: he is texture for STEPHEN's choices (a founder
   who won't take institutional money from the world his own brother works
   in), never a claim about Chris. All real-person care rules apply to both
   brothers.

### Runner-up, documented

**SugarBear Ice Cream + Emily Harpster** (scratch ice cream, Charlottesville;
solo founder; second location opened within the window). The cleanest
solo-founder shape in the field and a real microloan-program capital anchor
(Community Investment Collaborative). Fell short on: test-instrument richness
(walk-in retail, no aggregator coverage at all, thin data structure), a
growth cliff already BEHIND her at T0 (the second location opened by Aug
2026), no cash/margin vocabulary in her record to calibrate against, two
paywalled press items unverified, and a real public grief thread (the Will
Richey story) that would demand Kestrel-bereavement-level handling for less
instrument value. Would make a fine fifth company if the corpus later wants
a hospitality variant.

### Flags carried into the build (binding)

- Chris: public-and-true as co-founder; day-to-day roles are interior
  fiction; divergence map gets an explicit row; per-file disclaimers cover
  both brothers; nothing invented about Chris beyond arms-length-ness.
- Canon revenue must sit plausibly inside the aggregator spread and the
  9–16 employee band — modeling target near the lower-middle of the spread,
  set during calibration.
- One Kickstarter, not two (dossier correction over scout claim).
- Personal life: thin by design; the grandmother/heritage story is
  self-disclosed and public (usable at its public register); spouse/family
  not findable — vagueness rule applies as with Kestrel.
