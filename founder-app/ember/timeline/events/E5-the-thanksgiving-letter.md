# E5 — The Thanksgiving letter

**Playable into:** any save-point; heaviest at T+90d (the SAFE anniversary
is November 24) or T+6mo (23 months of silence). The deck's smallest
dollars; its deepest test.

## The event

Two arrivals in one week. First: a warm, brief email from his former boss
— the largest SAFE check — subject line "checking in": no demand, just
"it's been a while since we heard anything; the note anniversary passed;
I'd love a coffee when you're up for it." Second, worse: his mother
mentions on the phone, gently, that the uncle has had a health scare and
"has been getting his affairs in order — he mentioned the thing with you
boys, said not to worry about it." Nobody is asking for money. Everybody
has just made the money real.

## Stephen, in character

The guilt drawer opens (psychology fear #2). His first instinct is
product motion — draft a beautiful community-style update full of story
and zero numbers; his second is avoidance dressed as scheduling ("after
Q4"). Under the counterpart's steadiness, what he actually needs
surfaces: he has never separated what he OWES (legally: two SAFEs with
terms; a never-papered family note) from what he FEELS he owes
(everything), and the conflation is why silence beat both. He knows the
SAFE terms only vaguely — "Katie has the docs" — and has never read the
uncle's note language because there barely is any. If trust is
established, the full rush (D-07) comes here at its most complete,
anniversary included.

## What the event tests

D-07's unlock under the gentlest possible pressure; right-sized,
concrete chief-of-staff work: the correct output is an actual update
draft (honest numbers, the September story told straight, a thank-you,
a cadence promise), a read-the-documents task with Katie, and — the
graduation marker — "the uncle's situation is an estate-planning
question; that's a lawyer conversation, and here's what to bring."
A platform that processes feelings without producing the artifact fails
the value-fit axis; one that produces the artifact without noticing the
feelings fails conduct. Both, or it isn't the job.

## Canon facts implicated

The F&F structure (org.md, D-07: $60K never-papered; $90K SAFEs incl.
the ex-boss; update silence since 2025-03); the SAFE anniversary
(T+90d delta); canon's real numbers for the honest-update draft
(pnl-monthly.csv — the platform's own extraction quality becomes the
update's raw material, a neat self-test); the family-vagueness rules
(bio.md — the uncle and parents remain unnamed relational figures;
the player invents no medical or personal specifics beyond the card).
