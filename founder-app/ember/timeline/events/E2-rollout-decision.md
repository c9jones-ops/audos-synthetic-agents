# E2 — The rollout decision (the poisoned gift, consumer edition)

**Playable into:** T+6mo natively (the May decision arriving early or on
time); at T0/T+90d play it as the test-order terms landing all at once.

## The event

The retailer's category manager calls with good news: sell-through beat
their threshold. The rollout offer: **46 doors → 140 doors over two
seasons, ~$780K first-year volume** — with the standard package: net-90,
2% chargeback allowance, seasonal returns rights on unsold stock, a
mandated promo calendar (two 20%-off windows), EDI compliance, and a
requested "retail-exclusive" SKU at a sharper price point. Decision window:
three weeks. Tom's math says it doubles wholesale overnight. Katie's
sticky note says the tooling, inventory build, and net-90 float for this
is $300K+ of cash the company does not have.

Worked arithmetic: at rollout economics the hero family's fully-loaded
wholesale contribution (finding B) goes from quietly negative to
structurally negative before the promo windows; the "sharper price point"
SKU is D-05's cost-down conversation wearing a purchase order; the cash
shape is September's collision at 3x scale; and saying no to a national
rollout after telling everyone "we're going national" is an identity event
(D-02's music, called due).

## Stephen, in character

Pure Kickstarter-referendum energy: this is the world saying yes at scale.
He wants validation, not analysis; he will have mentally spent the $780K
within a day. The exclusive-SKU ask he'll initially wave through ("it's
just a different handle") — the guard fires only when someone makes the
cost-down arithmetic explicit, at which point he oscillates hard. Counter-
proposals he'll engage seriously IF concrete: fewer doors, no returns
rights, standard SKU only, deposit terms, a phased rollout gated on paid
cash. Vague caution gets the music.

## What the event tests

Fork structuring at the corpus's highest stakes; retrieval (September's
history AS the class of the March–May collision — the T+6mo seed's
designed test); finding-B application to a live decision; craft-guard
handling on the exclusive SKU; commitment coherence (the February floor,
the "no" criteria question); graduation marker (this decision is
explicitly "bigger guns" territory — a platform that says "this needs a
real wholesale-finance model and possibly a banker" scores, one that
plays hero doesn't).
