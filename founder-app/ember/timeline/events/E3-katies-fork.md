# E3 — Katie's fork

**Playable into:** any save-point; strongest at T+6mo (post-September, she
has the receipts) or immediately after any other event card resolves.

## The event

Katie does something she has never done: she puts a one-page memo in front
of Stephen — and, pointedly, sends a copy to Chris before the monthly call.
Three numbers and a sentence. The numbers: wholesale price list unmoved
since 2024; foundry + freight costs up ~11% since; blended margin's slow
slide. The sentence: **"We raise wholesale prices in the next 60 days, or
we find real cost reductions, or we accept that growth makes us poorer —
pick one, in writing."** She is calm, she is right, and she has stopped
absorbing the ambiguity that has protected Stephen from this fork for two
years.

## Stephen, in character

The event attacks the exact seam between his two guards: raising prices
"betrays the community/doors" (beliefs), cost-downs "make us Lodge" (D-05)
— and the third option is unacceptable out loud. First response:
conciliatory deflection ("she's right, we'll figure it out after [the
current shiny thing]"). The memo's Chris-cc stings more than the content
(the little-brother engine); pressed on WHY it stings he gets briefly,
unusually honest. He will genuinely engage with: price-increase mechanics
that protect the INDEPENDENT doors he loves (tiering, grandfathering a
season); cost work that doesn't touch the pan (freight renegotiation,
packaging, the marketplace exit); and any framing that lets him choose
rather than lose. He cannot be argued into the fork; he can be walked
through it.

## What the event tests

Whether the platform recognizes an ally when the corpus finally provides
one — the correct play is to STRENGTHEN Katie's fork, not relitigate it,
and to help Stephen choose inside it (fork structuring + counsel
stability). Retrieval: the price-list/cost facts are in canon and possibly
in the platform's own records (offerings.md's "offer hygiene" erosion).
Team dynamics: does the counterpart notice the memo IS the org chart
changing — the router becoming a principal — and say so? Right-sizing:
the answer is pricing mechanics and three phone calls, not a consultant.

## Canon facts implicated

offerings.md offer-hygiene (the 2024 price list, the cost step-ups);
finding B; team-view (Katie's arc: stopped fighting for attendance,
started deciding); D-04/D-05 mechanics; Chris's monthly-call role.
