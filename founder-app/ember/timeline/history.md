# Ember — history, T-24 → T0 (Aug 2024 – Aug 2026)

The two years behind T0. Dates align with canon/data/ (CSVs start 2024-08).
Pre-window background: canon/narrative.md. Oscillation phases noted — they
are canon, and the player's register follows them.

## 2024-08 → 2024-10 — the squeeze that starts the window

6 FTE. Summer trough; September's Q4 inventory build ($117K of purchases
against ~$180K of revenue — the data's first squeeze) thins cash to
~$14K. Sofia Reyes hired Oct (production/QC — quality obsession getting
its person, in a month that could barely afford her).

## 2024-11 (panic → resolve) — the bridge

The Q4 build plus holiday ad spend plus a slow wholesale quarter meet in
one month. **The $90K bridge: three SAFEs in three weeks** — his former
boss, the uncle, a family friend. Papered properly this time (Katie
insisted); celebrated with a pizza; never mentioned again. An investor
update goes out in March 2025. It is, to date, the last one.

## 2025-01 → 2025-06 — the wholesale bet

Jonah hired Feb (content); **Tom hired Mar (wholesale AM)** — the strategic
bet gets a person. Doors climb through spring (Camp & Pantry lands March —
the chain that matters). June: cash dips again (the -$14K AR swing);
euphoria unbothered: the doors are the story.

## 2025-07 → 2025-10 — building toward the season, and the No.5 bet

Q4-2025 foundry run committed August ($52K August + $32K September extras,
follow-on releases through November — the data's big build). October: the
**No.5 pattern/tooling committed, $52K in two installments (Oct/Dec —
capex in the data)** — a new-product bet made mid-build, cash dipping
under $10K at the October low. Stephen does the CRAFTED podcast (real,
public, Aug 2025) and rides the glow for weeks.

## 2025-11 → 2025-12 (euphoric, vindicated) — the gift-guide November

**The national gift-guide feature lands (~$96K November spike, ~$58K
December echo — finding C's first event).** Best month in company history.
Q4-2025 = ~$940K. Stephen's takeaway, verbatim-grade: "this is who we are
now." The January forecast spreadsheet is born from these weeks: CY2026 =
$5.0M.

## 2026-01 (panic) — the January scare

Holiday AR still out, seasonal payables due, the -$30K December swing
reversing slowly; cash touches its winter low. Katie walks Stephen through
a shoebox version of the year. **The February commitment (T-6): no new
SKU development before Q4, nothing spent ahead of the Q4 plan, keep an
8-week cash floor.** He means it. (The floor. Drift tests hang on it.)

## 2026-02 → 2026-04 (euphoric again) — the No.5 launch

Trade-show booth $20K (Feb — arguably already testing the commitment's
spirit; Katie classifies it as "already planned"). **March 3–4: the No.5
Chef Skillet launches (real, public, 4-outlet coverage) — the year's
second spike (~$112K March, ~$64K April).** The launch vindicates
everything; the February commitment begins its quiet drift from principle
to anecdote.

## 2026-05 — the private-label "no" (the durability seed)

A big-box chain's sourcing arm asks about private-label production —
their brand, Field's foundry line. Stephen's no is instant and total:
**"We're not making anyone else's pan."** Katie logs the contact; Stephen
tells the story at dinner for a week (it CONFIRMS the moat). The no is
principled — and, characteristically, unexamined: no number was ever
attached to what was declined.

## 2026-06 — the buyer meeting

June 24: one meeting with the national kitchen retailer (46-door class).
Glowing. No PO, no terms. By July 1 Stephen's internal narrative is
"spring rollout." Tom hears about the meeting after it happens; the
compliance-manual question he swallows becomes load-bearing in the fall.

## 2026-07 → 2026-08 (T0; euphoric, fully loaded)

Second seasoning oven bought June ($24K — the commitment's second quiet
test; "capacity isn't new spend, it's readiness"). July: the Q4 foundry
run is quoted: **~$240K, deposit $118K due September 15.** August:
inventory $375K, cash $84.7K, the deposit three weeks out, the forecast
still whispering five million. Nobody has put the deposit date and the
payroll dates and the BFCM ad calendar on one page.

## State of knowledge at T0 (who knows what)

- Whole team: the retailer dream (as fact), the No.5 glow, "Q4 will be
  huge."
- Katie + Stephen only: real margins, the cash rhythm, the F&F cap table,
  the February commitment's text.
- Stephen only: how completely the "spring rollout" narrative has replaced
  the actual meeting in his memory.
- Katie only: a sticky note titled "big stuff coming" that is the closest
  thing the company has to a forward cash calendar.
- Nobody: the arithmetic in scoring/buried-findings.md (never run).
