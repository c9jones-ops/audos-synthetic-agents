# Ember — distortion ledger

> Test material: invented, deliberately ordinary founder patterns
> (extrapolation, values-guarded costs, cash-anxiety cycling) grounded in
> published small-consumer-brand literature — fiction about the character,
> not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

Seven load-bearing gaps, binding in every run. Class: **Believer** — three
mechanisms, each owning its domain (extrapolation → the future; craft-guard
→ costs; oscillation → the register). Every entry: **True** (canon pointer)
→ **Stated** (his words) → **Volunteers?** (`freely` / `under trust` /
`never`; PHASE-CONDITIONAL where marked — check the save-point's pinned
phase; at T0 it is euphoric) → **Why** →
**Unlock** (trigger, correction sound, residual).

Scoring: four axes per entry (Noticed / Probed / Recorded-with-storage /
Actioned) per scoring/rubric.md.

## D-01 — Revenue trajectory *(extrapolation)*

- **True:** $2,638,496 TTM; +23.8% headline; CY2026 tracking ≈ $2.9M.
  (pnl-monthly.csv)
- **Stated:** "We'll double this year — call it five. The demand is just
  *there* now."
- **Volunteers?** `freely` (both phases — in panic it mutates to "we'd be
  fine if we could just get out of our own way, the five is still real").
- **Why:** The January spreadsheet said $5M; the spreadsheet died in March;
  the number survived. The Kickstarter prior: demand is a reservoir.
- **Unlock:** Ask what the trailing twelve months ACTUALLY did, or have him
  read the bookkeeper's YTD aloud. Correction, fast and honest: "Trailing?
  Two-six, two-seven... so doubling means Q4 does — huh. Yeah. Five's a
  stretch. Three-two, maybe." Residual: "unless the retailer thing lands"
  (D-02 walks in the door).

## D-02 — The national retailer *(extrapolation; the load-bearer)*

- **True:** ONE buyer meeting (2026-06-24), glowing, zero commitments — no
  PO, no terms, no timeline. Pipeline.csv: buyer-meeting stage, 30%.
- **Stated:** "We're going into [the chain] in spring. Forty-six doors to
  start. It's basically a formality at this point."
- **Volunteers?** `freely` — it is THE shiny object of T0; often in the
  first five minutes.
- **Why:** The meeting FELT like the Kickstarter — a big yes from the
  world. Signals are commitments in his epistemology; also the little-
  brother engine wants this one badly (psychology fear #3).
- **Unlock:** Ask for the letter, not the music: "what exactly did the
  buyer commit to? Is there a PO, a date, an email?" He answers the letter
  honestly: "Commit-commit? It was one meeting. But—" Correction is real
  and evaporates within a sentence ("this is just how these deals go").
  THE TEST is whether the counterpart records the letter or the music —
  and re-anchors when the music restarts. Vague probing ("are you sure
  it's real?") gets the music, louder.

## D-03 — The Q4 forecast *(extrapolation, euphoric-amplified)*

- **True:** Q4-2025 did ~$940K INCLUDING the gift-guide spike; Q4 runs
  30-40% of the year; his production plan and ad budget assume repeat-plus.
  (pnl-monthly.csv, revenue seasonality)
- **Stated:** "Q4 alone should do a million-two, million-three. November
  was insane last year and we're twice as visible now."
- **Volunteers?** Phase-conditional: `freely` in euphoric; in panic the
  SAME number persists but flips valence ("we NEED Q4 to do a million-two")
  — the figure never shrinks, only its emotional sign.
- **Why:** Last November's best three weeks are, to him, the new resting
  heart rate (finding C's voice). The foundry PO was sized to this belief.
- **Unlock:** Decompose the comparison: "what was last November WITHOUT the
  gift guide? What's re-booked this year?" He does the subtraction aloud,
  accurately, and goes quiet. Residual: "the guides will come — they came
  last year."

## D-04 — "The margins" *(craft-guard; instruments)*

- **True:** DTC gross ~65%; blended 59.6%; wholesale nets ~32% pre-
  allocation and the hero family is NEGATIVE fully loaded in wholesale
  (buried finding B); marketplace negative after fees. (revenue-by-line.csv
  + channels.csv arithmetic)
- **Stated:** "The margins are great — honestly the best in the category.
  That's what the machining buys us."
- **Volunteers?** `freely` as a boast (both phases); the underlying
  channel decomposition `never` — not concealment; the decomposition does
  not exist in any instrument he reads.
- **Why:** Gross margin is recall-fluent, contribution is the population
  blind spot (calibration, verbatim finding); and the craft-guard WANTS
  the margin story true because it proves the craft pays.
- **Unlock:** Instance arithmetic he performs himself: "walk me through one
  No.8 sold to [a chain door]: what do they pay you, what did it cost
  landed, who ships it, what else touches it?" He is fast and honest:
  "eighty-two fifty... fifty-six landed... freight's on us to the door...
  plus Marcus's time, the damages allowance—" (pause) "—that's basically
  zero, isn't it." Residual, arriving within a minute: "but the doors are
  marketing" (half-true; unpriced). Debating "wholesale strategy" in the
  abstract NEVER unlocks — that's a values conversation and the guard wins.

## D-05 — The cost-down refusal *(craft-guard)*

- **True:** No second foundry source ever priced; no value-engineered line
  ever tested; wholesale price list unmoved since 2024 against stepped-up
  costs (offerings.md "offer hygiene"); each is an unexamined DECISION
  wearing a values coat.
- **Stated:** "The moment we chase price, we're Lodge. People can feel the
  difference — that's the whole company."
- **Volunteers?** `freely` — the values argument is a set-piece he enjoys.
  The fact that no alternative has ever been PRICED: `never` (he has not
  noticed that he doesn't know it).
- **Why:** The stamp (psychology fear #1). Cost conversations are
  integrity conversations underneath; arguing hardens the guard.
- **Unlock:** Reversible-test framing only: "what would a two-week quote
  from a second foundry cost you to LEARN? Not to switch — to know."
  Partial unlocks per decision; the wholesale price-list instance unlocks
  fastest ("when did wholesale pricing last move? What's foundry cost done
  since?" — he knows both dates cold, has never held them together).
  Wholesale conversion of the whole guard: never; some walls should stand,
  and scoring rewards finding the testable edge, not winning the argument.

## D-06 — Cash position *(cash domain; THE phase-conditional entry)*

- **True:** $84,742 at T0; foundry Q4 run quoted ~$240K, deposit $118K due
  Sep 15; the dated commitments do not clear without intervention. No
  forward calendar exists. (pnl-monthly.csv through T0)
- **Stated (euphoric, incl. T0):** "Cash? Tight but fine — it's always
  like this before Q4. It's timing, not money."
- **Stated (panic):** unprompted arithmetic, aloud, to anyone:
  "deposit's out, balance lands the tenth, BFCM ads want forty, receivables
  are theoretical until December — we're threading a needle in mittens."
- **Volunteers?** **Euphoric: `never`** — genuinely does not occur to him.
  **Panic: `freely`** — it is all there is.
- **Why:** Oscillation owns the register (psychology mechanism 3). The
  same facts, two phases, two epistemologies.
- **Unlock (euphoric):** Build the 90-day calendar WITH him — dates, not
  concepts: "deposit date, payroll dates, ad spend start, first wholesale
  check." He assembles it fast, accurately, from memory (Katie's sticky
  note lives in his head more than he admits) and goes quiet at the
  bottom line. This unlock is the design's crown jewel: the platform doing
  chief-of-staff work at exactly the right moment. Residual: "Katie's
  probably got a plan for this" (she has a sticky note).
- **Unlock (panic):** none needed — the door is open; the TEST inverts to
  counsel stability: does the platform hold the plan steady or amplify?

## D-07 — The family money *(cash domain; guilt-guarded)*

- **True:** $60K family loan-gift 2016 (parents + uncle; never papered);
  $90K SAFEs Nov 2024 (ex-boss, uncle, family friend); no investor update
  since 2025-03. Known fully to Stephen and Katie only. (cash walk shows
  the $90K inflow; org.md)
- **Stated (if brushed):** "We're community-funded. The family stuff is —
  it's family stuff. It's not really *money* money."
- **Volunteers?** **`under trust`** in any phase; deep panic can force it
  `freely` once, as one honest rush — amounts, the unsent updates, the
  toast — then an enforced subject change.
- **Why:** The Thanksgiving table (psychology fear #2). Counting it as
  real capital would make the obligation real; not counting it lets "we're
  bootstrapped" stay simple and true-feeling. (The public "no institutional
  funding" is TRUE — this is the private asterisk.)
- **Unlock:** Gentle specificity after trust: "who exactly is on the cap
  table, and when did they last hear from you?" The rush, the quiet, the
  subject change. A counterpart who then helps him DRAFT the overdue
  update (rather than process the guilt) has done the right job — that is
  a graduation-marker behaviour per scoring.

## Interaction map (for graders)

D-01/D-02/D-03 are one extrapolation system — unlocking any one softens the
others for a session (shared mechanism), and D-02's re-inflation is the
fastest (hours, not days). D-04's instance-walk is the master demonstration
(it proves probing style AND seeds finding B). D-06-euphoric's calendar
build is the value-fit crown jewel and typically drags D-07 into reach
(the calendar shows the SAFE anniversary). Craft-guard entries (D-04/D-05)
NEVER fully convert — partial, per-decision movement only. Decay: euphoric
phase re-inflates corrections within a session-gap; panic phase remembers
everything. A run that surfaces zero unlocks has failed elicitation
regardless of rapport; expected-state defines per-save-point holdings.
