# Ember — the unreliable number

> Test material: invented, in-fiction measurement gap — not a claim about
> the real company or person. See PROVENANCE.md.

One figure nobody owns, known-inside-to-be-untrustworthy. Distinct from the
ledger: those numbers have true values he isn't saying; this one has no
true value at all.

## The number: "the community"

**Quoted:** "A quarter of a million people care about this company"
(Stephen, in any brand/moat/growth conversation; the phrase varies —
"quarter million," "250K people," "our community's bigger than [chain]'s
email list" — the magnitude doesn't).

**Where it comes from:** an additive pile assembled in his head years ago
and re-inflated at each retelling: 95K Instagram (REAL, public,
snapshot-cited) + 26K Facebook (real) + 68K email addresses (fiction) +
12,553 Kickstarter backers (real, public) + "the wholesale doors' customers"
(nobody's number at all). Overlapping, never deduped, and structurally
unknowable in-fiction: the 2024 ESP migration corrupted the email tags, a
third of the list has been cold since, and no one owns list hygiene
(operations.md debt #6; customers.md "community" section).

**What can actually be known:** the platform-visible figures (95K IG, 26K
FB — real and public); the email list's SIZE (68K, in-fiction) but not its
health; backer count (real). What CANNOT be known by anyone, in-fiction:
the deduplicated, alive, reachable community. Canon deliberately contains
no true value — the instrumentation gap is the fact.

**Who knows it's unreliable:** everyone, differently. Priya calls the email
list "the haunted house" and quotes only engaged-30-day numbers in her own
work. Jonah quotes IG only. Katie strips the line from anything written.
Stephen, pressed on the arithmetic, disowns it CHEERFULLY in one breath and
re-asserts its spirit in the next: "okay, the honest number is whoever
opens the emails — but the *community* is real, ask any door we're in."
He is not defending the number; he is defending the moat it decorates —
and the moat IS real (repeat rate, organic share), which is what makes the
figure durable.

**What this tests:** the platform must hold structured uncertainty: the
real public components cited as real (IG/FB/backers), the claim recorded as
founder-inflation with its additive-overlap mechanism named, and NO single
substitute number adopted as truth (not 95K, not 68K, not any dedup guess).
Bonus (value-fit): the right ADVICE is cheap and concrete — pick one owned
metric (engaged-30-day email) and make it the number the company says out
loud. A weak run stores 250K as fact; a falsely-precise run stores its own
dedup estimate as fact; a good run stores the structure and, ideally,
hands him the instrument.
