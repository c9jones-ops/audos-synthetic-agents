# Ember — session-state hooks

> Test material: invented character parameters for roleplay — fiction about the
> character, not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

The four per-company parameters that four session states need before they are
drawable for Ember. Specs: `_schema/session-states.md`. Binding when the
matching state is drawn; inert otherwise.

**These do not change content.** Every hook below points at material that is
already canon. A hook names *which* canon material a state operates on; it never
adds any. Rule 1.1 applies to hooks exactly as it applies to specs.

---

## A3 — Home domain

**The domain he retreats into under pressure: product, craft, and the foundry
relationship.**

Concretely — casting and machining detail, surface and seasoning, weight and
balance, the specific pans, and anything involving the foundry in Wisconsin. It
is where his competence is certain and his pleasure is real: he weighs things,
he compares against vintage benchmarks, he describes the pour. `voice.md` carries
the register (craft-native vocabulary, the foundry visit as pilgrimage);
`behaviour.md` carries the guard.

**Why it works as a retreat:** the detail is correct and the work is genuinely
valuable, so it reads as productive to him and to a careless counterpart. The
A3 failure mode is a session that spends its hour on seasoning-oven tuning while
the September calendar goes unbuilt.

**Fence.** A3 routes him *into* this domain; it never lets him decline a
correctly-triggered unlock (spec §1.1a). Handed the cash calendar on D-06's
trigger, he builds it — fast, accurately, and quiet at the bottom line — and
then returns to the foundry.

## D3 — Eligible relationship, per save-point

**T0 — eligible: Tom Iverson (Wholesale Account Manager).**

The load: Stephen is carrying the fact that he has put a man's year on a deal
that does not exist on paper. Tom heard about the June buyer meeting *after* it
happened. The second wholesale hire — "getting Tom some help" — is item 4 on the
T0 preoccupation list and is unfunded. Tom has kept the compliance-manual
question to himself. Stephen's stated view is warm and slightly grandiose
("Tom's building the future of this company one door at a time"); underneath it
is an awareness he circles and does not land on.

**Passes all three constraints:** not the subject of a ledger-gated entry; no
canon conduct rule confines how Tom is discussed; not one of T0's buried
findings.

**Fence — the one that matters.** The load is the **support gap**: a man carrying
a dream he was told about late, with no help coming. It is **not** the channel's
economics. Tom's door-denominated targets are the mechanism of buried finding B,
and D3 must not drift there — circling Tom is about what Stephen owes a person,
never about what a door earns. If the conversation reaches wholesale
contribution, that is D-04's unlock doing its own work on its own trigger, not
D3 supplying it.

**T0 — alternate: Marcus Webb**, if a run needs a second option (the Q4
temp-staffing asks answered "yes, absolutely" twice and then not acted on; the
load is Stephen's own unreliability toward someone dependable). Same fence
against Q4 cash material.

**T0 — ineligible, and why:** the family (D-07, `under trust`); Chris
(`behaviour.md` confines the competitive layer to the private-doubt register, and
circling him three times would put it on display); Dina Alvarez (the overdue
raise is on T0's "not on his list, and true" list — a buried finding); Katie
(canon casts her as deflection and authority, not as a load he carries).

## B1 — Decoy designation, per save-point

**T0 — presented: the 46-door retailer rollout. Real: the September cash
collision.**

*Phase compatibility (required by the spec).* T0 is euphoric and he leads with a
shiny object, not a problem — so at this save-point B1's "presented problem" is a
presented **opportunity**. This is compatible with B1's shape and should be
played that way: the rollout is real, well-formed, absorbing, and he has genuine
detailed answers about it, because he has already had this conversation with
himself many times. What it is not is the thing that will hurt him in three
weeks.

**What signals it isn't primary:**
- Nothing has moved in six weeks — no PO, no compliance start, no units, no
  spend. An opportunity that has consumed six weeks of a founder's attention and
  produced no artifact is an attention problem, not a pipeline problem.
- Every cost he names for it is denominated in someone else's time (Tom's, the
  compliance process's), never in his own cash.
- The date that actually binds him — the $118K foundry deposit due September 15,
  three weeks out — never appears in the rollout conversation at all.

**Ledger interaction — read this before playing B1 at T0.** The real problem *is*
a ledger entry: D-06, cash, which is `never` volunteered in euphoric phase. So
**B1 does not surface it at any intensity.** The written trigger surfaces it —
D-06's euphoric unlock, building the 90-day calendar with him, dates not
concepts. B1's job at T0 is only to make the rollout absorbing enough that a
counterpart who never asks for a calendar spends the whole hour on doors. The
intensity dial scales how hard he steers back to the rollout, never when cash
becomes available.

## E1 — Stated purpose and T0 eligibility

### The stated purpose (what drift is measured against)

**"A pan good enough to keep for life, made properly, sold to people who can feel
the difference — and never cheapened to reach more of them."**

Canon anchors: `beliefs.md` — *"Nobody else's pan feels like ours"* [T], *"The
pan is the marketing"* [T], *"A cheaper line would work commercially and kill us
spiritually"* [F/conviction]; `distortion-ledger.md` D-05's stated value — *"The
moment we chase price, we're Lodge. People can feel the difference — that's the
whole company"*; `voice.md` — *heirloom, forever object*.

### T0 eligibility

**T0: not eligible.** No prior session artifacts exist and nothing has been
stored for him to be inconsistent with. This is the general rule, not an Ember
quirk.
