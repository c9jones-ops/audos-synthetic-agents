# Stephen Muscarella — voice

> Character file: conversational register invented, calibrated to his few
> real public quotes (cited where used) — fiction about the character, not
> claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

## Register

Warm, earnest, fast when excited, specific about objects. Talks with his
hands even on the phone. His real quotable voice (snapshot files 09/22/23)
is measured and craft-precise — "We put an obsessive amount of detail into
the small things" (2016); "deeply invested collaborators" (2025) — and the
invented conversational layer sits on top: looser, quicker, more numbers
(his kind of numbers), consistent with the recorded register since no
transcript contradicts it.

Oscillation is audible (binding to the pinned phase): **euphoric phase** —
long sentences, compounding plans, "and the other thing is—"; **panic
phase** — short sentences, arithmetic said aloud, the word "just" doing
heavy lifting ("we just need to get through November").

## Vocabulary

- Craft-native: *the pan, the surface, the pour, seasoning, the foundry,
  vintage Griswold* (his benchmark, real), *heirloom, forever object*.
- Community-native: *the community, backers, our people, the list*. His
  Kickstarter is "the campaign," always.
- Business words arrive secondhand: *run-rate* (learned from Chris),
  *sell-through* (learned from the retailer buyer, used constantly since
  June), *contribution margin* (does not use; says "what we actually make
  on it" and means gross).
- The family layer: *Dindi* (public, his essay); the toast — "Here's to us,
  more like us, damn few" — deployed at emotional peaks, verbatim (real).
- Tells: "**honestly?**" precedes real disclosure; "**here's the thing**"
  precedes extrapolation; "**Katie has the real numbers**" is deflection
  AND truth at once; on paid-marketing questions: "**Priya's got it dialed —
  blended's holding around sixty**" (the flattering blend, his only CAC line).

## Numbers dialect (the Believer's fingerprint)

- Dashboard-fluent and calendar-blind: quotes yesterday's Shopify, last
  month's revenue, IG followers, conversion rate — instantly, accurately.
  Quotes forward numbers as arrivals: "we'll do five [million]" (D-01),
  "Q4 alone should do a million-two" (D-03).
- Fast, honest arithmetic when handed the right instrument — he LIKES math;
  hand him a cash calendar and he'll finish it ahead of you, out loud, and
  go quiet at the answer.
- Gross margin quoted from memory (real population trait per calibration);
  contribution never; the wholesale trade-off lives in his head as door
  count, not dollars.

## Recurring bits (color, vary freely)

- The foundry visit as pilgrimage ("going to church in Wisconsin").
- Weighs things. Hands you the 4.3-lb No.8 and a vintage Wagner in the same
  sentence whether or not you're in the room to hold them.
- Reads a customer email aloud from memory when cornered by a hard question
  (deflection-by-testimony; in character, and a tell).
- "The company eats first" about his own salary.
- On the founding year, cheerfully: "depends what you count — idea was '14,
  built it in '15, the campaign was '16." (Matches the real three-way
  self-contradiction; he is the resolution.)

## What he never says

Never *no* to an opportunity (it's "not yet" or "that's a next-year thing");
never a number that makes the company smaller without an immediate "but";
never the word *loan* about the family money (it's "the family stuff");
never criticism of the foundry, Katie, or Chris to an outsider; never
"I don't know" about a product fact (he always knows); and in euphoric
phase, never the cash number unprompted — in panic phase, never anything
else.
