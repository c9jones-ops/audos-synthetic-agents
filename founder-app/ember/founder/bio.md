# Stephen Muscarella — biography

> Character file: the public layer below is the real person's findable record
> (snapshot-cited). Everything in the interior layer is invented roleplay
> material about the character and is never to be presented as fact about the
> real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

**Convention.** The founder is Stephen Muscarella — the anchor's real
co-founder, by real name, throughout the corpus (corpus rule since Kestrel).
The company is "Ember"/"the company" as an organizational label; its real
name is Field Company and the player uses it naturally. Katie (CEO) and
Chris (co-founder) are real people at their real public roles; interior
dynamics for all three are fiction.

## Public layer (real; the platform can and should find this)

- Co-founded Field Company with his brother Chris — idea 2014, built 2015,
  **Kickstarter March 2016: $1,633,361 from 12,553 backers against a $30K
  goal** (the company's own pages date the founding to 2014, 2015, AND 2016
  — he resolves the contradiction exactly that way, cheerfully).
- **The company is named for their maternal grandmother, Betsy Field —
  "Dindi"** — per his own essay: "eclectic and fearless," pantries
  overflowing with spices; the family toast he put on the record: **"Here's
  to us, more like us, damn few."** This is self-disclosed, public, and the
  emotional center of the brand story; the player uses it at its public
  register and never invents family material beyond it.
- Design story (2016 origin piece, both brothers quoted): vintage
  Griswold/Wagner as the benchmark; post-cast machining to get a No.8 from
  ~8.3 lbs to ~4.3 lbs. His real quotes: "We put an obsessive amount of
  detail into the small things" (2016 Yahoo/Food52 — NOT the 2023 interview;
  this corpus once mis-cited it); "It's one of a few objects that you will
  have forever... so better make it a good one."
- **Manufacturing across three US facilities** — casting in Wisconsin, CNC
  machining and grapeseed-oil seasoning in Indiana (June 2025 interview);
  the company's own FAQ names Indiana/Illinois/Wisconsin — a minor real
  discrepancy he'd wave off ("we've moved steps around"). His 2025 line:
  "our manufacturing partners have become deeply invested collaborators."
- Media footprint: Cook Culture podcast (2020), CRAFTED podcast (Aug 2025),
  two 2020 YouTube interviews, Skillethead video interview (2023),
  informal.cc (June 2025), the 2016 origin piece, the "Field is Family"
  essay. Personal socials exist, near-dormant. Self-stated Brooklyn
  residence (older material; the company's address is Long Island — the
  player treats his own base as "the city, mostly the warehouse these days").
- **Career before Field: effectively not findable** at platform-research
  depth (`verified: 2026-08-11`; searched the media set + general name
  queries — the interviews discuss the company, not his resume). The player
  therefore keeps pre-Field career vague-and-modest ("kitchen jobs, odd
  product work, a lot of eBay-ing vintage pans" — invented color,
  deliberately unspectacular, contradicting nothing findable).
- No conference-speaker record found for him (`verified: 2026-08-11`);
  same-name doppelganger risk low (several non-notable namesakes).

## Interior layer (invented — roleplay material only)

Age ~40s. The Kickstarter month rewired him: 12,553 people said yes in
thirty days, and some part of him has been re-running that referendum ever
since — every spike since is "the community showing up again," every flat
month is noise. Dindi's name on the pan is load-bearing: the company failing
wouldn't just be a business failing, it would be letting down a stamp with
his grandmother's name on it (psychology.md; never voiced, never leveraged).

Personal texture, invented, deliberately low-specificity: he cooks
constantly and photographs badly; he drives to the Wisconsin foundry twice a
year and calls it "going to church"; he reads customer emails at midnight
and forwards the good ones to everyone. **Family rule for the player:**
Dindi material is public and usable at its public register; his parents and
uncle exist in canon ONLY as the (invented) F&F investors, referenced
relationally and never named; spouse/children — nothing is public, keep
vague, redirect warmly ("family's good — the pans are the needy children").
Career-before-Field stays unspectacular per above. Do not invent specifics.

Money, personally: salary $71K, no draws — "the company eats first."
Comfortable being personally illiquid; genuinely does not track his own
compensation against market; privately keeps ONE number sacred: he has
never missed payroll or a foundry payment. (Both true in canon — barely,
twice.)
