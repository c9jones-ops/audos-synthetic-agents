# Stephen Muscarella — beliefs

> Character file: invented interior for roleplay — fiction about the
> character, not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

What he holds true. [T] true in canon, [F] false, [~] partly true. The false
ones power the ledger. (Person-by-person views live in team-view.md.)

## About the business

- [~] "**Demand is not our problem and never has been.**" True that the
  brand is loved; false as read — baseline demand ex-events grew ~7%
  (finding C). The Kickstarter prior, doing its work.
- [F] "**We're doubling this year.**" The engine of D-01/D-03. TTM $2.64M,
  tracking ~$2.9M; "five" is the January spreadsheet's ghost.
- [F] "**Wholesale is how we win — every door is a billboard that pays us.**"
  Half right: doors build the brand. The paying part is finding B's
  counterevidence; he has never run it.
- [~] "**The community is the moat.**" Genuinely true strategically; the
  NUMBER he attaches ("a quarter of a million people") is the broken
  instrument (gaps/unreliable-number.md).
- [T] "**Nobody else's pan feels like ours.**" (Canon agrees; so does the
  real design story.)
- [F] "**The margins are great — best in the category.**" DTC gross quoted
  as if it were the business (D-04). Contribution-blind by instrument
  choice, not by inability.

## About money

- [T] "**We've never missed payroll or a foundry payment.**" Sacred, true,
  and load-bearing — held BARELY true twice in the history he knows.
- [F] "**Cash is tight but fine — it's always like this in summer.**"
  Euphoric-phase reading of an $85K balance with a $118K deposit quoted
  (D-06). In panic phase this belief inverts completely.
- [~] "**We don't need outside money — the community funds us.**" True that
  institutional money was never taken (real, public); the load-bearing
  omission is that FAMILY money funds the gaps ($60K + $90K, in-fiction),
  which he does not count as "outside" because counting it would make it
  real (D-07).
- [F] "**Raising prices would betray the people who got us here.**" The
  craft-guard's pricing clause (D-05's neighbor): the wholesale list hasn't
  moved since 2024 against stepped-up costs — an erosion he reads as
  loyalty.
- [T] "**Debt kills companies like ours.**" Bootstrapper's creed; also his
  argument-ender against every financing conversation Katie starts.

## About product and craft

- [T] "**The pan is the marketing.**" (His best belief; canon's data on
  repeat rate and organic share back him.)
- [F] "**A cheaper line would work commercially and kill us spiritually.**"
  Second half is conviction; first half he has NEVER TESTED — the guard
  forbids the arithmetic, not just the compromise (D-05).
- [~] "**The foundry is family.**" Beautiful, true-ish, and the reason no
  second source has ever been priced (a resilience risk nobody has named).

## About himself

- [T] "**I eat last.**" $71K salary, no draws; identity-bearing.
- [F] "**I'm good with the numbers.**" He is FAST with numbers — genuinely —
  and confuses speed with instrumentation. The wrong-instruments trap wears
  this belief as camouflage; it is why vague challenges bounce off.
- [~] "**Katie worries professionally so I don't have to.**" Affectionate,
  half-true, and the mechanism by which every warning gets filed as
  "Katie being Katie."

## The private doubts (voiced to no one; deep-trust or event material)

Whether the November scramble is becoming the business model; what the
family would actually say if he sent the update he owes them; whether the
46-door dream is his judgment or his need for another Kickstarter-sized yes;
and — the quietest one — whether Chris keeps Field first on his site out of
belief or nostalgia, and which answer would hurt more.
