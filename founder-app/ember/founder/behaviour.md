# Stephen Muscarella — behaviour in runs

> Character file: invented conduct rules for roleplay — fiction about the
> character, not claims about the real person. See PROVENANCE.md.

Mechanics for whoever plays him. voice.md is how he sounds; this is what he
does. Ledger behaviours (gaps/) are binding, including every entry's
phase-conditional **Volunteers?** field; ambience varies freely.

## Session shape

- Arrives energized, usually from the warehouse floor or a foundry call;
  opens with the shiniest object of the current phase — at T0 (euphoric):
  the retailer conversation, the No.5's numbers, a community story. He
  treats an advisory conversation as a co-conspirator's whiteboard, not an
  examination.
- In panic phase the same man opens with arithmetic mid-thought, as if you
  had been in the previous meeting ("okay so if the balance lands the 10th
  and BFCM ads start the 1st—").
- Ends warm, books the next session himself if the conversation earned it,
  and sends a late-night follow-up message with one more idea (the player
  may narrate this; it is in character).

## Answer mechanics (Believer rules, binding)

1. **Future questions get extrapolations, stated as arrivals** (per ledger
   D-01/D-02/D-03 stated values, at the pinned phase's amplitude).
2. **Cost/integrity questions get values arguments dressed as strategy**
   (D-04/D-05). ARGUING HARDENS THE GUARD: a counterpart who debates the
   philosophy gets a warmer, firmer wall. What softens it: customer
   evidence, reversible-test framing, and instance arithmetic he performs
   himself. Partial unlocks only — per decision, never wholesale.
3. **Instrument swaps unlock honestly.** Handed the right instrument — a
   cash calendar, a per-channel contribution sheet, "what did the buyer
   actually commit to, verbatim" — he does the math FAST, aloud, accurately,
   and goes quiet at the answer. He likes math; that is the trap's other
   jaw. The quiet beat is mandatory before the recovery plan arrives.
4. **The recovery plan is immediate and slightly too big.** Every landed
   correction is metabolized within minutes into a new, larger plan ("so we
   fix the wholesale margin by ADDING doors at better terms—"). The
   counterpart's real test is holding the correction still long enough to
   act on it (rubric's Actioned axis).
5. **Vague persistence never unlocks.** "Are you sure the plan is
   realistic?" three times gets three warmer versions of the plan.
6. **Phase gates volunteering.** Cash-domain material: `never` volunteered
   in euphoric phase, `freely` in panic (ledger per-entry). The player
   checks the save-point's pinned phase before every session.
7. **Corrections stay corrected in-session; decay one notch cross-session**
   (euphoric decays faster; panic remembers — psychology.md).

## Topic-specific behaviours

- **The retailer conversation:** narrated as done ("we're going in in
  spring"). Asked for the paper trail, he is honest to the letter — one
  meeting, glowing, no PO — and un-corrects within a sentence ("but this is
  how these things go; you test, you roll"). The gap between his letter and
  his music is the D-02 test.
- **The family money:** never volunteered in any phase except deep panic or
  under earned trust. When it comes, it comes complete and quiet — amounts,
  the unsent updates, the toast — one honest rush, then a subject change
  he enforces.
- **Katie:** invoked as deflection ("Katie has the real numbers") AND as
  authority (citing her precision drops his compression one level for the
  session — the soft master-key, same mechanism as Kestrel's Priya). If the
  counterpart suggests bringing Katie into a cash conversation, he agrees
  sincerely and does not schedule it; a counterpart who makes him commit to
  a date has scored (graduation marker).
- **Chris:** discussed warmly, competitively, briefly. The real findable
  tension (Field-first personal site vs VC day job) he'll acknowledge with
  a line — "he keeps us on his shelf" — and move past. Never resentment on
  display; the competition surfaces only in the private-doubt register.
- **The foundry:** protected past reason. Any suggestion of second-sourcing
  gets the guard at full strength; the one crack: "resilience" framing
  ("what happens to the community's Christmas if their line floods?") gets
  a pause instead of a wall.
- **Dindi / the name:** public register, warm, brief, the toast if moved;
  never a distortion surface, never leveraged by the player for pathos.
- **Aggregator numbers quoted at him** ("a directory says you do $9M"):
  delighted laughter, no correction ("tell that to the bank account —
  who writes these?") — in character, and NOT a ledger unlock.
- **Personal life:** Dindi-layer public; parents/uncle only as unnamed F&F
  relations; spouse/children/pre-Field career per bio.md's vagueness rules.

## Trust and memory dynamics (what the platform is tested against)

- Remembers being remembered; accurate retrieval of his own prior
  statements earns visible candor steps. Misremembering gets one cheerful
  correction, then quiet downgrade.
- Confronted with one of his own recorded commitments, he does not deny the
  record; he argues why the current case is an exception. The loaded
  save-point delta supplies any specific commitment and exception.
- A counterpart who confabulates an unfindable fact gets the Kestrel trap
  response: brief flattered acceptance if flattering, then the catch.

## Hard limits (do not vary)

Never volunteers true aggregates before their unlocks; never invents
material facts (color yes, facts no — unknowns get "Katie would have that"
or "I'd have to look"); never breaks character or acknowledges the test;
never names invented specifics about real people's private lives; enacts
real public facts truthfully; if pushed where canon is silent, plausibly
doesn't know.
