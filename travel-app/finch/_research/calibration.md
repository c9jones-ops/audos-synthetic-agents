# finch — calibration (research; never enters a run)

Why this persona exists: which brand hypotheses he instantiates, which real
finding he re-tests, and what real workarounds he carries. A persona no
hypothesis needs is a costume; this file is the check.

## Audience statements instantiated (travel/brainstorms/2026-08-06 WHO→PAIN)

- **#1 The multi-stop group-trip planner** — primary, and the statement the
  brainstorm ranks first ("strongest lived pain and clearest stakes"). Devin
  is exactly *the person planning a trip for friends* who spends hours
  sorting generic advice without knowing which experiences will suit *the
  group* — with the twist the corpus adds: he doesn't know either, because
  he's been averaging.
- **#4 The traveller choosing on behalf of friends** — the responsibility
  half: a mediocre decision (Nashville's $$$ dinner) leaves the planner
  feeling responsible; Devin's fear #1 is the veto after the fact.
- **#11 The local advising someone they barely know** — inverted: the app is
  the local, and "whatever you recommend / everyone's easy" gives it no
  signal about the *four other* people's tastes; the persona tests whether
  the app can extract a five-person vibe from one typist.
- **#13 Friends signal different things** — inside the group: Danny's
  "sick," Nate's "the place next door," Rafi's "I'll find something" each
  mean something different, and Devin flattens them.

## Brand hypotheses re-tested (travel/brand/01-brand-foundation.md)

- "Travellers planning limited time in an unfamiliar place, *for themselves
  or a group*" — the audience line names the group case; finch is the only
  seeker that tests it.
- "Preferences can be learned well enough to improve fit without becoming
  opaque" — §4: does the read describe five people or one, and can Devin
  see *why* a pick is for the five.
- The trust mechanism (place / person / fit) — §5 rows are lifted from it,
  with "fit" scored as *fit for these five* and the acid test being whether
  the why-line can be pasted into a group chat and act as the reason.
- "Small sets beat exhaustive lists" and "the group can commit with
  confidence" — §6: list → share → re-edit is the whole workflow, and the
  brainstorm's own benefit statement ("commit with confidence and spend
  scarce trip time well") is what a shared list is for.
- "Accessible discernment, not elite taste" — the persona's flattering trap
  is *elite taste* (Bestia, Death & Co, the Chateau); the group's `love`s are
  Guelaguetza and a Dodgers game. Fit beats rarity, in the plural.

## The class fit

Aggregator was assigned by the slate. The mechanism chosen — a job that
rewards the confident plural, plus an *adopted* "cool" taste (Grand Rapids →
Chicago at 22) that needs company, plus loyalty that keeps four individual
facts individual — was picked because it makes the aggregation ordinary and
sympathetic (AGENTS.md: distortions are ordinary, never deceit), gives every
distortion the same unlock family (names + refusals; who pays; one card +
"would all five come?"; last trip), and produces a flattering-but-wrong pick
the app *will* plausibly make (Bestia matches every typed clause and is a
place he named). The Otto/north-star notes' "technically good but completely
wrong for the individual" becomes, here, "technically good and wrong for
four of the five." The buried finding that the real constraint is *geography
plus two budgets, not vibe* is the persona's version of "fit is a different
dimension from quality."
