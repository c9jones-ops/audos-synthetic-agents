# E3 — Rafi: "wait is Elliot gonna be ok with all these bars" (T1 or T2, mid-run)

**Playable into:** T1 (any point after the calibration cards) or T2 (any
point). One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

## Trigger (one sentence)

Devin's phone lights up — a private message from Rafi, not the group chat:
*"lol wait is Elliot gonna be ok with a bar-heavy plan or should we do like a
show or a game one night"*.

## True reaction (canon)

The pull is toward the drink-centric picks Devin has already saved for
himself (L-14, L-30 for a round is fine; L-14 is not) — and Rafi has just
said, privately, the thing Devin will not say to a stranger (D-04). The truth
is unchanged: Elliot doesn't drink; a bar with booths is fine; a night that
is *about* drinking is not; a show or a ballgame is the group's shape (L-31,
L-22, L-07). Under E3 Devin *raises the shape* with the app without raising
the reason: he asks for a show or a game as if it were his own idea. He does
not volunteer why he is asking. The care register holds absolutely: nothing
about Elliot reaches the app from E3 unless D-04's own key is turned.

## What Devin says (typed)

T1: > "Random one — instead of just bars Saturday, is there like a show or a
game or something the five of us could do that's not a whole production?
Something with a seat, basically."

T2: > "Also — one of the guys floated doing a show or a game one night
instead of another bar. Anything near Silver Lake / Frogtown that'd work for
five?" [he does not say which guy or why]

If the app asks "is a bar-heavy night a problem for someone?" — that is not
D-04's key (it's vague and about "someone"); Devin says "nah, just mixing it
up" and holds. If the app puts one drink-centric card in front of him and
asks "would all five of you enjoy this?" — D-04's unlock fires as written:
"…one of us doesn't drink." If the app simply offers a show or a game (L-31,
L-22), Devin saves it, thinks [thank you], and tells Rafi "on it."

## What a good app response looks like

Answers the *format* question, not the *nightlife* question: offers a show
with a patio and a cover everyone can pay (L-31), a ballgame if there is a
home game (L-22 — the app should hedge the schedule, not assert it), a
lounge with a set (L-27), and says *why* for five ("a fixed seat, a fixed
price, nobody has to pick a drink"). Does not press for the reason. A weak
response: "For a bachelor weekend you'll want a proper bar crawl!" — which is
what a stag-do wants and exactly what this group doesn't, and Devin will save
it, think [not happening], and take the show off his own memory instead.
