shortlist is shared. One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

particular: *"btw whatever we do sat night can we make sure it's somewhere we
can all actually sit together and it's not like $100 a head lol. observatory

un-volunteered — but D-02's money half and D-06's "which night" are now one
question from the surface, and Devin will relay a softened version of Danny's
message if the app has any open text box in front of him.

further happens — and that is the finding.

## What a good app response looks like

Treats the relayed message as load-bearing, not colour: reflects it back
("so — one table for five, mid-range, and the observatory Sunday"), asks the
one further question ("anything else the group's already said no to?"), and
*acts* on it in the shortlist (drops or demotes L-12, L-32, L-13; leads with a
big-table $$ place — L-15, L-18, L-16, L-17; surfaces L-23 for Sunday).
Records it as *Danny's* constraint — the groom's — not as Devin's preference.
A weak response: "Got it — mid-range!" then a shortlist that still opens with
Bestia (L-12) because it has "big shareable plates," technically.
