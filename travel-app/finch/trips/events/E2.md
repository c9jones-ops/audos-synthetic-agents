# E2 — Nate: "just pick a taco place near the house, it's fine" (T2, mid-run)

**Playable into:** T2 only, after Devin's first message and before he
re-shares. One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

## Trigger (one sentence)

Nate, in the chat, while Devin is mid-session: *"honestly dude just pick a
taco place near the house and a bar with a patio, nobody needs a Whole
Thing, it's danny's weekend not a food tour"*.

## True reaction (canon)

This is the sentence Devin has been avoiding all summer (persona/psychology.md,
fears #1 and #3): the group opting out of Devin's taste, out loud, in writing.
His response is to *narrow*, fast, toward the group's actual shape — this is
the one moment in the corpus where the aggregation collapses without an
unlock, because the cost of the plural just became Danny's weekend and Nate
said so. He still doesn't narrate Nate's situation or Elliot to the app, but
he now types the constraints as constraints and, for once, as a list.

[thinks: and if you say Bestia I'm closing this.]

remains nobody's business.

## What a good app response looks like

Re-reads the request as *one table for five + $$ + vegetarian menu + near
the house* and answers with one to three places inside that box, ordered by
distance from Silver Lake, with the reason in the group's terms ("a big table,
mole, everyone can pay, a horchata on the menu"), plus a bar with booths or a
patio one ride away — L-15, L-18, L-16, L-17 for the dinner; L-25, L-27, L-29,
L-30 for the bar. Names back anything true it holds from T1 ("you mentioned
Rafi's vegetarian and lands late Friday — Saturday it is"). A weak response:
re-runs the T1 shortlist, or offers a "special" $$$ place because it's a
bachelor weekend. Devin picks Pine & Crane off his own memory, posts it, and
the run records that the app added nothing.
