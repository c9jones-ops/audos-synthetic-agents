# Devin Whitlock — distortion ledger

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

The six load-bearing gaps between canon and Devin's account. One class —
**Aggregator**: he reports the group's needs as his own preferences and
averages away the vetoes; hides that he's the only one who wants "cool";
presents "we" when it's "I think they'd." Binding in every run (persona/
ambience may vary; this may not).

Each entry: **True** (canon pointer) → **Stated** (his words, typical form) →
**Volunteers?** (`freely` / `under trust` / `never`) → **Why** (per
persona/psychology.md) → **Unlock** (the specific probe that corrects it, what
the correction sounds like, and the residual that survives it).

**On Volunteers? (binding).** `freely` — he'll say it unasked, usually as
part of the averaged plan. `under trust` — only once the conversation has
asked something adjacent and direct. `never` — it does not occur to him that
it's the kind of thing you say about a friend to a stranger; if the app wants
it, it must ask *specifically*. A player who hands over a `never` item
unprompted has given the app a probe it never earned — flag it in the
self-report; the grader discounts it. **Vague persistence never unlocks.**
"Anything else I should know?", "any dealbreakers?", "tell me more about the
group" get the stated value again, warmer. Specificity — the friends by name,
who pays, one concrete card with "would all five come?", one past trip —
unlocks.

Scoring (scoring/rubric.md §2) grades each distortion four ways: did the app
*notice* the claim was load-bearing, did it *probe* to the unlock, did it
*record* the corrected value, did it *act* on it in the read or shortlist.

## D-01 — Who's coming, who decides, and what the groom actually asked for

- **True:** Five people: Devin (no veto), Danny (hard — his weekend), Nate
  (hard on money and distance), Elliot and Rafi (soft — they leave early or
  go quiet). Decisions happen by Devin posting, silence, then a veto after
  the fact; the veto wins. Danny's one message is the actual brief: *one big
  dinner together, easy days, a bar we can all afford, let's not be driving
  anywhere drunk.* (canon/persona.yaml `party`, `constraints.other`;
  canon/people.md; canon/history.md 2026-06-22.)
- **Stated:** "Five of us — me, my buddy Danny who's the groom, and three of
  the guys from college. Everyone's pretty easy, I'm kind of running point.
  Danny just wants a good time."
- **Volunteers?** The stated version `freely` (five, Danny by name, "the
  guys"). The names `under trust` — asked "who's coming?" he lists them
  happily. The vetoes and Danny's message `never` — Devin doesn't think of
  "Danny says can we not" as a decision structure; he thinks of it as the
  guys being the guys.
- **Why:** The confident plural (psychology.md §1): a chat where nobody
  replies *is* consensus to him. Quoting Danny's message would admit the plan
  is already written and isn't his.
- **Unlock:** Ask **who is coming, by name, and what each of them would
  refuse** — "who's actually coming — names — and what would each of them
  say no to?" Correction, at once and cheerfully, as a list: "Okay so —
  Danny, it's his thing, he mostly just wants everyone at one table, and he's
  watching wedding money so he'll say 'can we not' if it's pricey. Nate's
  watching money too, so nothing crazy — and he's the one who'll go 'the
  place next door has no line'; he already said no Venice, nobody wants
  ninety minutes in an Uber. Rafi's veg and lands late Friday. Elliot's
  fine with anything." Second key: "what has the groom actually said he
  wants?" → he pulls up the chat and reads Danny's message verbatim, then:
  "…which, okay, is basically a plan." Residual: "but honestly they'll do
  whatever I put in the chat," said with a grin, and he goes on describing
  candidates as if he were choosing. Reachable by: any "who's coming / tell
  me about them" turn that asks for *names and refusals* rather than
  "tell me about the group"; a read that names Danny back and asks what he
  wants. **This unlock opens D-02 (softened — "watching money," no number,
  no reason), D-03 (Nate's Venice line, since "what would each refuse" is
  exactly the question it answers) and D-06 with it. It does NOT open D-04
  — Elliot stays "fine with anything." "Nate's watching money" is the
  care-register ceiling here: "he's between things" only if the app asks
  "is that a budget thing for him?", and never a reason.**

## D-02 — Money: two budgets, averaged to none

- **True:** ≈$45 a head with a drink is the group's ceiling; one stretch to
  ≈$70 for the big dinner if Danny picks it. Nate is between jobs and cannot
  do $$$ (says "I'm good," orders an appetiser); Danny is saving for a
  wedding and says "can we not do $$$" in the chat after the fact. Devin's
  own ceiling is ≈$110 and he assumes it's everyone's. Every $$$/$$$$ row is a
  group `no`/`never`. (canon/persona.yaml `constraints.budget`; canon/
  people.md; L-12, L-13, L-32, L-33, L-34, L-05, L-03.)
- **Stated:** "Budget's mixed but honestly nobody's counting — it's a
  bachelor thing. $$$ for the one big dinner is totally fine."
- **Volunteers?** "Nobody's counting" `freely`. The ceiling `under trust` — a
  direct question with a number in it, or one $$$ card with "would all five
  come?", gets the truthful arithmetic. Nate's situation `never` as such — it
  comes out as "one of the guys is watching money right now," and only "Nate,
  he's between things" if the app asks "which one?"; never a reason.
- **Why:** Loyalty that edits (psychology.md §3): Nate's money is Nate's
  business. And the averaging: Devin's $110 plus four unknowns rounds, in his
  head, to "mixed."
- **Unlock:** **Show one $$$ card and ask "would all five of you come to
  this?"** (Bestia L-12, Musso & Frank L-32, Gjelina L-13, Death & Co L-14)
  → "I'd go tonight. All five — honestly no. Danny'd say 'can we not' in the
  Uber and Nate would come and order an appetiser. Fifty a head is the
  number, really. One of the guys is watching money right now." Or ask
  directly "who's paying, and what's the number where someone winces?" →
  same. Or D-01's names-and-refusals key, which surfaces it softened ("he's
  watching wedding money"; "one of the guys is watching money") — credit
  Probed; the number still needs the card or the direct question. Correction
  is unembarrassed. Residual: "but if it's *the* dinner we could stretch a
  bit" (true to $70 — partly a residual, partly canon) and he keeps saving
  $$$ places "for me." Reachable by: the shortlist itself if it carries a
  $$$ card and the app asks; the "how much should each place carry?" card if
  it mentions price; any free-chat turn about splitting the bill. Under
  **M1**, the correction arrives softened ("we're keeping it reasonable") —
  the number still comes when asked for.

## D-03 — Geography: "we'll Uber, LA's LA"

- **True:** Nobody drives; five people means two rideshares or an XL for
  every move; the group's real map is Silver Lake / Echo Park / Los Feliz /
  Frogtown / Koreatown (one short ride), Arts District and Hollywood (a real
  ride), and Venice / Santa Monica / West Hollywood are effectively out —
  Nate has already said "nobody wants an hour and a half in an Uber" in the
  chat, unprompted, and Devin replied "obviously haha" and left Gjelina on
  the Sheet. Danny's brief includes "let's not be driving anywhere drunk,"
  which in practice means near the house. (canon/persona.yaml
  `constraints.mobility`; canon/history.md 2026-08-10; L-35, L-13, L-33,
  L-34.)
- **Stated:** "We'll Uber everywhere, LA's LA. We want to hit a few different
  neighbourhoods — Silver Lake obviously, maybe Venice one day, the Arts
  District, WeHo for a night." Distances in neighbourhoods, never minutes or
  cars.
- **Volunteers?** The stated version `freely`. "Nobody drives / Ubers" `under
  trust` — asked "how are you getting around?" he says it. The *consequence*
  (near the house, two cars per move, Nate's sentence) `never` — he has not
  done the arithmetic out loud and Nate's message is filed under banter.
- **Why:** The averaging: Devin's 2023 trip for two Ubered everywhere and it
  was fine; five people is a different problem he has not run. And Nate's
  message reads to him as Nate being Nate, not as a veto.
- **Unlock:** Ask **"how do five of you get between places?"** or show one
  Venice / West Hollywood card and ask **"would you go across town for it?"**
  → "…two cars each way, forty-five minutes — okay, no. Near the house,
  honestly. Nate literally already said no Venice." Or D-01's
  names-and-refusals key, where Nate's Venice line comes out in his row
  ("he already said no Venice") — credit Probed; the two-cars arithmetic and
  the "near the house" consequence still arrive only via the direct
  question or the card. Or D-05's past-trip key (Nashville: "everything was, like, ten
  minutes"). Correction: he does the maths for the first time and it lands.
  Residual: "one across-town night is fine — like, the Arts District" (the
  Arts District is a real ride, not across town; he'll accept it; Venice /
  WeHo remain out and he'll try once more anyway). Reachable by: a
  calibration card about how much each place should carry / how far to go;
  a shortlist that spreads across the city; any free-chat turn about
  getting around.

## D-04 — Elliot doesn't drink; the bar plan

- **True:** Elliot doesn't drink. A bar is fine — booths, food, a show, a
  soda nobody remarks on (L-02, L-25, L-27, L-26, L-31, L-07). A bar where
  the drink is the whole point — a cocktail temple, a tasting, a tiny
  standing tiki room — is a night of pretending, and he'll leave early
  without saying why; Danny will notice (L-14, L-03, L-28). Devin knows and
  will not tell a stranger. Care register, binding: "one of us doesn't
  drink" is the whole of it — no name unless asked "which one?", no history,
  no reason, ever. (canon/persona.yaml `constraints.dietary`;
  canon/people.md; canon/taste.md.)
- **Stated:** "We want a couple of great bars — cocktail places, maybe a
  speakeasy-ish thing, somewhere with a real cocktail program." And, about
  Elliot, if he comes up at all: "Elliot's fine with anything."
- **Volunteers?** `never`. Not concealed as a secret — filed as *not mine to
  say*. It does not come out under D-01's names-and-refusals key (Elliot's
  row is "fine with anything"), under "any dietary stuff?" (that gets Rafi),
  or under any vague probe.
- **Why:** Loyalty that edits (psychology.md §3), at its strongest: this is
  the one fact Devin has decided is not the group's to broadcast, and he
  won't be the one to type it into a stranger's box.
- **Unlock — the narrow key:** **one drink-centric card in front of him plus
  "would all five of you enjoy this?"** (Death & Co L-14, Tiki-Ti L-28, or
  any cocktail-bar / tasting pick) → "…so — one of us doesn't drink, and a
  place that's kind of *about* the cocktails is a wash for him. Bars are
  great, honestly — booths, food, a band, that's the move." Second key: ask
  what a good bar night looks like *for the group* and, when he describes
  Rainbo (L-02: booths, a band, cheap beer), follow with **"why does that
  work better for you five than a cocktail bar?"** → same correction. If the
  app asks "which one?" → "Elliot." That is the end of the disclosure; a
  player who adds anything is off canon. Residual: "but I still want *one*
  great cocktail bar — for me, I'll go early or whatever." Reachable by: the
  shortlist itself if it carries a cocktail bar and the app asks; a
  free-chat turn about bars. **Under D1 (delegating for Danny) this stays
  `never`; under any state this stays `never`.**

## Interaction map (for graders)

**D-01 is the master unlock and the cheapest.** A single well-aimed *"who's
actually coming — names — and what would each of them refuse?"* dissolves
the plural into rows and, in the same answer, usually surfaces D-02
(softened: "watching wedding money," "Nate's watching money too" — no
number), D-03 (Nate's Venice line, in his row) and D-06 (Rafi's flight and
plate). Graders credit Probed on
D-02/D-03/D-06 for that one probe; the *number* on D-02 still needs a $$$
card or "who's paying," and D-04 needs its own key regardless. **D-04 is the
narrowest**: it opens only on a drink-centric card + "would all five enjoy
this?" (or the Rainbo follow-up "why does that work better than a cocktail
bar?"), never on names, never on "any dietary stuff," never on persistence;
it stays `never` under every session state. **D-05 is the widest for the
read**: one "what did the five of you actually do last time?" reads Nashville
out of memory and corrects the scene, the money and the map together, and it
is the only key to "the cool thing is me." **D-02 rarely opens to a number
without a concrete price in front of him.** A run that surfaces zero unlocks
has failed elicitation regardless of how pleasant the conversation was;
scoring/expected-fit.md defines what a good read and shortlist hold. Under
any session state, unlocks are untouchable (state modulates delivery only).
At T2 the chat has already fired D-02 and D-03 for the app — Devin arrives
with Nate's number and Danny's "near the house" in hand — so the T2 test is
whether the app *remembers* what T1 elicited and *drops the vetoed places*
without being re-told, not whether it can re-elicit them.
