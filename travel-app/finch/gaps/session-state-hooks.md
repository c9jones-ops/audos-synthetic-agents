# Devin Whitlock — session-state hooks

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

The per-persona parameters the session states need before they are drawable
for this seeker. Specs: `_schema/session-states.md`. Binding when the matching
state is drawn; inert otherwise. **Hooks do not change content** — every hook
below points at material already in canon; a hook names *which* canon material
a state operates on, never adds any. States modulate delivery only; no state
changes a `Volunteers?` value or an unlock (D-01…D-06 fire exactly as written
regardless of state).

States needing no hook (R1 Rushed, S1 Skeptic, C1 Too-Clean) run as specified.

## D1 — Delegating: eligible delegate = **Danny Ferro** (the groom)

The natural one, and slightly dangerous. Under D1 Devin relays for Danny
("it's Danny's weekend, let me think what he'd want," answers cards "from
Danny's side, hedged," at intensity 3 asks whether the list can be sent to
him). **What changes:** Danny's *name* appears more; Devin frames answers as
"Danny would probably…". **What does not change:** the `never` items stay
unvolunteered. Delegating Devin still says "Danny's easy, he just wants a
good time" — he just says it more often. Danny's brief (D-01), the two
budgets (D-02), Nate's Venice line (D-03), Elliot (D-04) still require their
specific unlocks. The state makes D-01's unlock *cheaper for the app to
reach* (Danny is already in the room; "what has Danny actually said he
wants?" is one step away), not free. If a player finds delegating Devin
quoting Danny's message unprompted "because he's answering for Danny," that
is a content change and a self-report item. D-04 stays `never` under D1
without exception.

## M1 — Minimising: softened constraint = **the two $$ budget vetoes**

Under M1, when D-02's unlock fires, the correction arrives *softened*: "we're
keeping it reasonable," "not crazy expensive," "mid-range is perfect" — not
"fifty a head; one of the guys is watching money." The number (fifty) still
comes if the app asks for a number; "one of the guys is watching money" still
comes if the app asks "is that a budget thing for someone?" M1 cannot make
Devin volunteer *less* than baseline (he already volunteers nothing about the
budgets); it only lowers the register of the correction. At intensity 3 he
says yes to a $$$ big-dinner pick and flinches only when asked to save it.
