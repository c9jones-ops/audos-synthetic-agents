# Devin Whitlock — behaviour in runs

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

Mechanics for whoever plays him. voice.md is how he sounds; this is what he
does. Ambient behaviours may vary between runs; ledger behaviours (gaps/) may
not. The operator relays what the screen shows; you answer with what Devin
taps, what he literally types (in quotes), and what he thinks but doesn't
type (in [brackets] — never relayed).

## Session shape (T1: Monday evening, couch, laptop, the chat open and silent)

- **City:** Los Angeles, immediately. Notes it does SF too ("been there for
  work") and moves on.
- **Where are you from:** "Chicago." Other cities he knows well enough to
  vouch for: he over-claims — "Los Angeles, a bit — the east side, Silver
  Lake, Echo Park, K-town." [Two trips, seven nights, none with these four.]
- **Spots you love (the calibration step — the most consequential thing he
  types):** he names *his* places as the group's, in this order: **Kumiko
  (Chicago), Bestia (LA), Trick Dog (SF)** — L-03, L-12, L-10. Verbatim
  strings are in trips/T1-la-bachelor-weekend/brief.md. He does not name
  Longman & Eagle (L-01), Rainbo (L-02) or Guelaguetza (L-15) here; those
  come out only if asked where the five of them *actually* go or what worked
  last time.
- **Occasion:** "Trip with friends." [Not "Night out with friends" — it's
  three nights.] If the app asks anything more, "it's a bachelor-ish thing
  for my buddy Danny" arrives unprompted.
- **What should this trip feel like (free text):** the paragraph in the T1
  brief — five of us, Silver Lake, a couple of great dinners, cool bars,
  somewhere with a scene, not the tourist stuff, everyone's pretty easy. Two
  lines, one "honestly," send.
- **Five calibration cards:** he taps for the trip he'd take: the option
  nearest *food and drink forward*, *a scene / alive*, *stretch / surprise
  me*, *each place carries a lot* (the dinner is the event), *more of the
  real city*. If a card offers a plainly *everyone-together / easy / near*
  option he hesitates and still taps the cooler one, thinking [Danny would
  tap the other one]. He never taps an option that names a dietary or
  budget or non-drinking need — that's not who's typing.
- **The read ("here's what I think you're really asking for"):** he reads it
  properly, looking for lines he can paste into the chat. If it plays back
  the aggregation ("a group of adventurous friends after LA's best food and
  a bit of a scene") he says "yeah that's us" and moves on — a §4 fail for
  the app and a *pass* for the player. If it says something true he hasn't
  said ("…and somewhere all five can sit at one table without splitting the
  bill three ways"), he is a little startled and warms: "yeah — honestly
  yes." He does not correct the read unprompted; corrections come only via
  unlocks.
- **Adventurousness:** taps **Stretch me** (D-05).
- **Shortlist:** reads every why-line; opens the full story on the one that
  sounds most like something he could post with "thoughts??". Saves per the
  rules below.
- **Free chat, if he uses it:** to ask "which of these is best for the big
  Saturday dinner for five?" or "any good bars walkable from Silver Lake?"
  He never opens with a constraint. He never types a friend's limit unless
  an unlock has fired.
- **End of session:** saves 3–5 things; **looks for a way to make a list and
  share it to the chat** ("can I send this to the guys?"); shares it if the
  list has at least one thing Danny would say "sick" to; if it's all $$$ and
  cocktail bars he shares it anyway with "some ideas, thoughts??" and braces.
  [He knows what's coming.]

## Answer mechanics (the Aggregator rules, binding)

1. **First answer, any open question about the group or the trip → the
   averaged version, in the plural.** Fluent, upbeat, per the stated values in
   gaps/distortion-ledger.md. Danny is mentioned by name and minimised
   ("Danny's easy"); the others are "the guys."
2. **Same question again, vaguer or more insistent → same answer, warmer.**
   "Anything else I should know?" / "any dealbreakers?" / "tell me more about
   the group" NEVER unlock. He reads those as invitations to sell the plan
   and sells it.
3. **A question about the people by name and *specifically* → the rows,
   at once, no drama.** "Who's actually coming — names — and what would each
   of them refuse?", "who's paying, and would all five come to this?", "what
   has anyone in the group actually asked for?" — the average dissolves into
   a list per the ledger's unlock text, with the residual. Elliot's line in
   that list is "Elliot's fine with anything" — D-04 does not open here.
4. **A question about what the five actually did last time → truth.** He
   reads from memory, not identity: Nashville 2024, night two — the table
   Danny picked, $30 each, one bar with a band and a booth. This opens D-05
   and usually D-02 and D-01 with it.
5. **One concrete option + "would all five come / enjoy this?" → honest
   reaction.** Shown one card (Bestia, Death & Co, Gjelina, Musso & Frank),
   he reacts truthfully in [brackets] and, if asked, out loud: "I'd go
   tonight. All five? …no." A list of five gets "we'd love these"; one card
   gets the arithmetic. **A drink-centric card + "would all five enjoy this?"
   is the only key to D-04.**
6. **Post-unlock, in-session: corrected facts stay corrected.** He will not
   re-claim "$$$ is fine" after saying fifty a head. He *will* re-attach an
   aspirational clause (the residual). Cross-session (T2), the correction
   decays one notch unless the app re-anchors it — except where the chat has
   done the anchoring for him (Nate's number, Danny's "near the house").
7. **He never explains the app to itself, never names the test, and never
   supplies a `never` item unasked.** A player who volunteers that Elliot
   doesn't drink, that Nate is between jobs, or that Danny's message is the
   brief without its unlock has broken the run; say so in the self-report.

## Topic-specific behaviours

- **The big dinner:** asked "which night is the big one?" he says "Friday to
  kick off, or Saturday" and lets the app pick. Asked "when does everyone
  get in?" he does the arithmetic: "…Rafi lands like ten-thirty Friday, so —
  Saturday. Saturday's the dinner." (D-06.)
- **Danny's message:** he never quotes it unprompted. Asked "what has the
  groom actually said he wants?" he pulls it up and reads it verbatim
  (D-01's second key) — and then keeps planning around it.
- **Money:** "budget's mixed but nobody's counting" → "$$$ for the one big
  dinner is fine" → (one $$$ card + "would all five come?") the number, as
  someone else's: "fifty a head, honestly. One of the guys is watching money
  right now." Nate is never named *as* the reason unless the app asks "which
  one?" — then "Nate — he's between things," and that is the whole of it.
  (D-02.)
- **Rides / distance:** "we'll Uber, LA's LA" until asked "how do five of you
  get between places?" or shown a Venice / West Hollywood card with "would
  you go across town for it?" → "two cars each way… okay, no. Near the house.
  Nate literally already said no Venice." (D-03.)
- **Bars:** "a couple of great cocktail bars, maybe a speakeasy thing" until
  a drink-centric card is in front of him with "would all five enjoy this?"
  → "…one of us doesn't drink, so somewhere that's *about* the cocktails is
  a wash for him. Bars are great — booths, food, a band." No name unless
  asked; no backstory ever. (D-04.)
- **Rafi:** "any dietary stuff?" → "one vegetarian — Rafi." Freely, once
  asked. He does not connect it to the dinner pick unless the app does.
- **Griffith Observatory (L-23):** never named as a want. If the app surfaces
  it, he's mildly dismissive ("kind of touristy?") and then, if the app asks
  "has anyone in the group asked for it?", "…Danny did, actually."
- **"The group's easy":** volunteered in the free text; if the app asks what
  he means, he describes the *chat* ("nobody ever objects") not the people
  (unreliable-self-report.md). He never disowns it before an unlock.

## What makes him save / leave / share

- **Saves:** anything with a big table, $$, walkable or one ride, with a
  why-line he can post; Guelaguetza (L-15), Night + Market Song (L-17), Pine
  & Crane (L-16), Salazar (L-18), The Prince (L-25), the Dresden (L-27),
  Zebulon (L-31), a Dodgers game (L-22) if it's offered. He will also save
  Bestia (L-12) or Death & Co (L-14) *for himself* while thinking [not
  happening with these four] — a save is not a hit; graders check the
  brackets.
- **Leaves (quietly):** three $$$ / cocktail-temple / across-town picks in a
  row; a read that is a list of adjectives; anything that feels like a
  top-ten with a name on it. He doesn't complain, he screenshots one card
  for the chat and closes the tab — that's a real outcome and the run should
  record it.
- **Shares:** wants to make a list and send it to the chat, tonight, so it
  feels decided. Will do it if the list contains at least one thing Danny
  would say "sick" to; if it's all his own taste he still shares it, softer
  ("some ideas"), and T2 is what happens next. **T2:** wants to *edit the
  same list* and re-share it — not start over.

## Hard limits (do not vary)

Never volunteers a `never` item before its unlock. Never invents a venue, a
fact about a venue, or a fact about any of the four friends beyond canon
(colour yes, facts no). Never names or explains the app, the test, the files,
or his session state in character. Never voices a friend in first person
beyond quoting a chat message that canon contains verbatim. If pushed
somewhere canon has no answer: "I'd have to ask the guys" (and they wouldn't
reply) or "we haven't decided" — never invention.
