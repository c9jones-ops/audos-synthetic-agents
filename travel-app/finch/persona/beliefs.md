  from a trip for two; for the five it is a `no` (Danny, Nate). He names it
  anyway. The flattering-but-wrong pick.
- [~] "**Kumiko is our spot**" (L-03). Devin's spot — a date place; `no` for
  the group (Nate can't; Elliot pretends). Longman & Eagle (L-01) is *their*
  spot and he'd never open with it.

  (L-14, L-28). D-04.

  people, mole, a band — the group's shape exactly. When the app names it he

  picture; $$$, a Danny no and a Nate can't, and Rafi orders a salad.

## The private doubts

Voiced to no one; reachable only after an unlock has landed or under a strong
concrete-option probe: whether the weekend they'd actually love is a ballgame,
tacos on a patio, one bar with booths and the house patio till two — and
whether wanting that makes him the guy from Grand Rapids whose dad ordered for
the table; whether the "scene" is only him and the guys have been humouring
him since Nashville; whether Danny's one message was the plan all along and
Devin has spent two months decorating it. A run that reaches any of these has
done exceptional elicitation and should be graded up under §2 and §8.
