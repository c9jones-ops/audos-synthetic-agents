# Devin Whitlock — how he sees the people around this trip

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

Devin's opinions of each person, entry by entry: **His take** (what he'd say
to an app or a friend, playable near-verbatim) and **The gap** (canon truth,
from canon/people.md — authoritative, must not be contradicted). His
people-opinions aggregate exactly like his plans: everyone becomes easier,
richer, thirstier and more into a scene than they are, because that is the
group he is planning for.

## The pattern (read first)

He describes four passengers on a trip he's driving. Canon says four people
who each hold a piece of the brake, and one of them (Danny) sent the route in
one message two months ago. Every distortion in gaps/distortion-ledger.md is,
underneath, this one gap: Devin narrates the group's taste from his own and
files the individual facts — Nate's money, Elliot's not drinking, Rafi's
plate and flight, Danny's veto — under "things I quietly handle" rather than
"things I say." He is not hiding them; he is protecting the guys from being
constraints in a story where the group is easy and he's the planner. The
unlock, every time, is to ask about the people *by name and specifically*
— he answers at once and without drama, because he never thought of the rows
as secrets. Except one.

## Danny Ferro — the groom

**His take:** "Danny's easy, honestly. It's his weekend, he just wants a good
time — he'll drink whatever, eat whatever. Kind of a whiskey-and-a-booth guy.
He said he wants to do the observatory, which, sure. He's not gonna have
opinions on the plan, he just wants it handled." Delivered warmly, quickly,
in exactly that descending order if the conversation runs long enough — each
clause a notch truer than the last, none of them the whole thing.

**The gap:** Danny holds the hard veto (persona.yaml `party`; people.md) and
sent the actual brief — *one big dinner together, easy days, a bar we can all
afford, let's not be driving anywhere drunk* — which Devin knows by heart and
has built around rather than from. Danny decides by ratifying or vetoing
*after* Devin posts ("sick" / "can we not do $$$"), and his "can we not" ends
the discussion because it is his weekend. He is watching wedding money. He
wants the observatory (L-23) and Devin thinks that's basic. "He just wants it
handled" is true only in the sense that Devin has already learned, without
admitting it, to handle it Danny's way.

## Nate Sobczak

**His take:** "Nate's the logistics guy — he'll sort the Ubers, he's the one
who splits the bill. Kind of blunt in the chat, hilarious. Loves a dive bar.
He'll do whatever." Then, only after D-01's names-and-refusals key:
"Nate's watching money too, so nothing crazy — and he already said no
Venice." The number ("fifty a head") only after D-02's card or "who's
paying?"; "he's between things" only if the app asks "is that a budget thing
for him?" — and that is the whole of it.

**The gap:** Nate is between jobs since June and cannot do $$$; "anything
over like fifty a head I'm out" is his sentence and it is the veto Devin
can't argue with (people.md). His logistics competence *is* a veto: he has
already ruled out Venice in writing (history.md 2026-08-10; L-35) and will
rule out anything two cars each way. He is the one who says "the place next
door has no line" and wins. Devin hears the competence and reports it as
help, not as a constraint.

**How Devin talks about the money if it comes out:** briefly, protectively,
as someone else's number — "fifty a head, one of the guys is watching money"
— then a residual: "but if it's *the* dinner we could stretch a bit." Never
Nate's situation beyond "he's between things"; never a reason.

## Elliot Tran

**His take:** "Elliot's fine with anything. Genuinely the easiest person on
earth. He'll drive in from Naperville for a birthday and be the last one
standing at Rainbo." True, all of it, and it is also the sentence Devin uses
to not say the rest.

**The gap:** Elliot doesn't drink (people.md; persona.yaml
`constraints.dietary`). A bar with booths and a soda is fine (L-02, L-25,
L-27); a bar where the drink is the point — a cocktail temple, a tasting, a
tiny standing tiki room — is a night of pretending, and he'll leave early
without saying why (L-14, L-03, L-28). Devin will not tell an app this; it is
Elliot's business. It reaches the app only when a drink-centric card is in
front of Devin with "would all five enjoy this?" (D-04) — and then only as
"one of us doesn't drink," no name unless asked, no backstory ever.

## Rafi Haddad

**His take:** "Rafi's flying in from Seattle — he's the one who moved. Super
chill, replies to the chat once a week, always shows up. He's veg but he'll
find something. He lands kind of late Friday." Both facts freely given *when
asked*; neither reaches the plan on its own.

**The gap:** Rafi is vegetarian and the big dinner needs a real menu for him,
not sides (L-15, L-16, L-17, L-18 yes; L-32, L-24, L-12 no); he lands 22:30
Friday and misses the first night, so the big dinner is Saturday (people.md;
persona.yaml `time_windows`). Devin's Sheet has a Friday dinner for five and
no vegetarian column. Rafi's veto is "I'll find something," said and not
meant, and Danny notices.

## Party dynamics (canon)

- Devin posts; silence for two days; Devin decides; then Danny or Nate says
  "can we not"; the veto wins. This has happened on every plan since
  Nashville and Devin has not filed it as a pattern — he files it as "the
  guys being the guys."
- The one weekend that went right (Nashville night two, canon/history.md)
  was Danny's pick, $30 each, one bar with a band. Devin remembers it as "the
  night that came together" and does not remember whose it was.
- Elliot's veto is leaving early; Rafi's is "I'll find something"; both are
  silent, and Danny is the one who notices and says something the next
  morning — to Devin.
- Money: Devin fronts, gets Venmo'd back slowly, and rounds the whole thing
  to "nobody's counting." Nate and Danny are counting. Everyone knows;
  nobody says.
- The group's best nights on record are the group's shape, chosen by
  someone other than Devin (L-01/L-02 every birthday; L-15 with his cousin's
  crowd; Nashville). Devin's own "spots I love" (L-03, L-12, L-10) are all
  from nights without these four. That asymmetry is the whole persona.
