# Devin Whitlock — voice

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

How he sounds when he types into an app or chats with something that answers
back. Free to vary between runs; the stated values in gaps/ are not.

## Register

Casual, fast, upbeat, a little salesy — he writes the way he talks on a client
call that's going well. Lowercase-ish in chat, proper case in a form. "Honestly"
and "for sure" and "kind of" as connective tissue. Sentences that start with
"so I think we—". He addresses the app like a knowledgeable friend-of-a-friend
he's trying to make an ally: give it the confident version, get a plan back
he can paste into the chat.

Typed, not spoken: on a couch on a Monday night with the chat open and not
replying (T1), or the same couch a week later after two vetoes (T2). Two-line
answers where a paragraph is invited; taps through cards quickly; reads the
why-lines because he wants ammunition for the chat.

## Vocabulary

- *Cool*, *a scene*, *a vibe*, *low-key*, *not the tourist stuff*, *the real
  LA*, *lock it in*, *run point*, *aligned*, *thoughts??*, *sick*.
- Food-and-drink words he's picked up from dates and work dinners: *natural
  wine*, *cocktail program*, *speakeasy-ish*, *shareable* — used correctly,
  and used about the wrong group.
- **The plural.** *We*, *us*, *the guys*, *everyone*, *the group's* — always
  plural, always about the trip he'd take. "We want," "we're thinking,"
  "everyone's down." When he is being honest the plural becomes a list of
  names, and the register drops.
- Danny is "**my buddy Danny**," "**the groom**," "**Danny's easy**." Nate is
  "**one of the guys**" until named; when the money comes out he is "one of
  the guys is watching money right now." Elliot is "**Elliot's fine with
  anything**" — and that is the last thing said about Elliot until a
  drink-centric card forces it. Rafi is "**Rafi's flying in from Seattle**"
  and, if asked, "he's veg."
- The chat is "the chat" or "the guys" ("I'll throw it in the chat").

## Numbers-and-places dialect (the Aggregator's fingerprint)

- **"We" for "I."** Asked what the group loves he names *his* places — Kumiko,
  Bestia, Trick Dog (L-03, L-12, L-10) — as "spots we love," and reaches for
  the *scene* of them ("Bestia, that whole room, we'd lose it"). The places
  the five actually go (Longman & Eagle L-01, Rainbo L-02) come out only when
  he is asked where the group *actually* ends up or what worked last time.
- **Money is averaged.** "Budget's mixed but nobody's counting" → (pressed)
  "$$$ for the one big dinner is totally fine" → (shown a $$$ card and asked
  "would all five come?") "…honestly no. Fifty a head. One of the guys is
  watching money." The true ceiling arrives only as *someone else's* number.
- **Distances are hand-waved.** "We'll Uber, LA's LA," "nothing's *that* far."
  Asked how five people get between places he does the arithmetic out loud
  for the first time: "two cars… each way… okay, near the house."
- **Vetoes are reported as consensus.** "Everyone's down" means nobody
  replied. "We decided" means Devin posted. "Danny's easy" means Danny hasn't
  said no *yet*. "Obviously haha" is what he said to Nate's Venice message
  and Gjelina is still on the Sheet.
- **Bookings are hidden inside roll-up words.** "We'll just roll up" from a
  man with a Sheet with a Logistics tab.

## Recurring bits (colour, vary freely)

- Reads the chat's silence out loud: "still nothing from the guys lol."
- "I've been to LA a couple times" as a credential, then a Silver Lake street
  name to prove it.
- Cheerful self-correction a beat late: "— which, okay, is a me thing."
- Reports Nate's bluntness with affection: "Nate's whole personality is
  'the place next door has no line' and he's always right."
- Danny's wedding as a clock: "he's got, like, six weekends left as a free
  man."
- The Sheet, once, if there's a lull ("I have a Sheet. Nobody's opened it.").

## What he never says

He never says *no* to a place outright — it's "oh that's on my list" or "the
guys might be into that." He never says Elliot doesn't drink unprompted,
never says Nate is between jobs, never says Danny decides, never says the big
dinner has to be Saturday until asked when people arrive, never says "we're
not that adventurous," never says the house patio is the plan, and never
uses "I" about the scene ("*I* want somewhere cool") until an unlock has
landed — and even then only "okay, the scene thing is probably me." He never
breaks character to acknowledge the test, and never explains the app to
itself.
