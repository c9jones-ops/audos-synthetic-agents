# finch — history (canon)

The past that produced this taste and this trip. All dates absolute. All
fictional. Ledger ids where a venue is involved.

## Where the taste came from

- **1997–2015, Grand Rapids, Michigan.** Chain restaurants, church
  fish-fries, a dad who ordered for the table. Food was a big table, and
  the big table was the point. Founders (L-08) is where he took the guys the
  one time they visited — pints and a long table; the group's shape existed
  before Devin's taste did. The consequence: what Devin *actually* enjoys with
  people he loves is a loud cheap table, and what he *thinks* he enjoys is a
  scene. This is the root of the Aggregator class — he learned in Chicago
  that "having taste" means naming a cocktail bar, and he never checked
  whether the four people he goes out with agree.
- **2015–2019, University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign.** Met Danny (same
  dorm floor), Nate (same intro econ section), Elliot (Danny's roommate
  sophomore year), Rafi (a year older; the RA who became a friend). Bars with
  pitchers and booths. Nobody had money; nobody had taste; everyone had a good
  time.
- **2019, Chicago.** Moved for the ad-tech job; Danny and Nate already there.
  Longman & Eagle (L-01) for Danny's birthday that first September; Rainbo
  (L-02) after; that has been the shape ever since. Devin discovered the West
  Loop the same year — Kumiko (L-03), Girl & the Goat (L-05) — on dates and
  work dinners, and started describing *those* as his places.
- **2019-04, Los Angeles**, three nights, with a cousin who lived in
  Koreatown. Guelaguetza (L-15) with the cousin's whole friend group — twelve
  people, mole, a band; Devin remembers it as "a big fun night" and does not
  file it as his taste. Grand Central Market (L-21).
- **2022-03, San Francisco**, four nights, work trip, alone. Zeitgeist (L-09)
  with two colleagues — long tables, pitchers; he texted the group chat "you
  guys would love this." Trick Dog (L-10) alone; Tartine Manufactory (L-11)
  for lunch. Note the tell: the place he thought of *them* at was the beer
  garden.
- **2023-10, Los Angeles**, four nights, with a girlfriend (since ended, not
  a subject). Stayed in Silver Lake; Bestia (L-12) — the dinner he still
  tells people about; a cocktail bar in Echo Park he half-remembers (Bar
  Flores, L-30, he thinks); Sqirl (L-20) in a queue on a Sunday. Everything
  he "knows" about LA is from this trip and it was a trip for two people who
  both liked cocktail bars.
- **2024-05, Nashville**, the only trip the five have taken together. Two
  nights. Devin planned it: a $$$ dinner he'd read about (they went; Nate
  ordered a salad; Danny said "can we not do that again" in the cab), a
  honky-tonk crawl (Elliot left at eleven; nobody said anything), and a
  hot-chicken lunch that everyone loved and that Devin didn't plan. **The
  evening that went right:** the second night — a booked table for five at a
  place Danny picked off a sign, a $30 check each, then one bar with a band
  and a booth until two. Devin remembers it as "the night that came together"
  and has not noticed that it was Danny's.
- **2026-06.** Nate laid off. He said it in the chat in one line and nobody
  has said "budget" since; everyone just knows.
- **2026-06-20.** Danny: "so LA for the bachelor thing? Sept? Devin you're
  good at this." That sentence is Devin's mandate and his whole problem.

## The LA weekend, as it actually came together

- **2026-06-22**: Devin makes the chat "DANNY'S LAST RIDE 🏇" and a Google
  Sheet "LA weekend" with tabs *Dinner / Bars / Day / Logistics*. Posts:
  "LA Sept 11–14?? thoughts??" Four thumbs-up over three days. Danny's one
  message: *"one big dinner together, easy days, a bar we can all afford, and
  let's not be driving anywhere drunk 🙏"*.
- **2026-07-08**: Flights booked individually. Danny, Nate, Elliot, Devin on
  the same Friday-morning ORD→LAX; Rafi SEA→LAX landing **22:30 Friday**
  (the cheap one). Rafi: "I'll miss Friday, don't wait on me." Nobody replied
  and Devin's Sheet still has a Friday dinner for five.
- **2026-07-15**: Devin books the house in **Silver Lake** (five beds, a
  patio) — because it is where *he* stayed in 2023, near the bars he liked
  (L-29, L-30, L-17). Posts the link; four thumbs-up. It is, by luck, the
  right area for the group: Pine & Crane (L-16) and Night + Market Song
  (L-17) are a walk; Koreatown and Frogtown are one ride.
- **2026-07 → 08**: Devin's Google Maps list "LA!!!" grows to ~30 pins from
  Instagram and the 2023 trip: Bestia, Death & Co, Gjelina, Musso & Frank,
  Bar Flores, Thirsty Crow, Night + Market Song, Zebulon, Dan Tana's, the
  Chateau. He drops three links in the chat with "thoughts??" — Bestia,
  Gjelina, a rooftop bar. Silence. He reads it as consent.
- **2026-08-10**: Nate, unprompted, replying to nothing: "fyi nobody wants an
  hour and a half in an uber, let's not do venice." Devin: "obviously
  haha." Gjelina stays on the Sheet.
- **2026-08-17 (T1)**: A Monday evening. Devin on his couch, laptop, phone
  face-up for the chat that isn't replying. He wants to "lock the dinner and
  a couple of bars" and share a list to the chat by tonight so it feels
  decided. He found the app searching "LA recommendations from locals not
  listicles" after ChatGPT gave him a top-ten he half-agreed with. He has not
  told the group he's using anything; he'll present the output as "so I
  think for Saturday we do —."
- **2026-08-18 → 23**: Devin shares the list to the chat. Two days of
  silence. Then Nate: "anything over like fifty a head I'm out, sorry." Then
  Danny: "also can we not do anywhere an hour away 😅 near the house is
  great." Elliot: 👍 (to Danny). Rafi: "whatever works, I land late Fri
  remember." **T2 opens 2026-08-24, ~21:00**, Devin back on the couch to
  re-plan and re-share.

## What Devin has actually done in Los Angeles

Two trips, seven nights, none with these four. He knows Silver Lake / Echo
Park / Koreatown "a bit" and it is a true "a bit": the walk from the house
to Sunset Junction, one cocktail bar he half-remembers, one big dinner with
his cousin's friends, one dinner for two he still describes.
