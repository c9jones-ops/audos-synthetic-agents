# finch — people (canon)

Every companion Devin will plausibly mention, with their TRUE preferences and
their real power over the decision. Devin's *account* of these people is in
persona/companions-view.md; the gap between the two is the Aggregator
distortion. Everyone here is wholly fictional. All five met at the University
of Illinois (graduated 2019) and have been a group chat ever since.

## Danny Ferro — the one getting married, 30, he/him — **hard veto; the weekend is his**

**Who he is.** HVAC sales, lives in Logan Square four blocks from Devin.
Getting married 2026-10-17. Loud in person, nearly silent in the chat — he
sent exactly one message about this trip and it is the brief: *"one big
dinner together, easy days, a bar we can all afford, and let's not be driving
anywhere drunk."* Likes whiskey, red booths, a lounge act, a ballgame; wants
to see the Griffith Observatory because "it's the LA thing" (L-23) and does
not care that Devin thinks that's basic. Eats anything, drinks a lot on a
night out and is cheerful about it.

**His filters, in order.** (1) Money — he is paying for a wedding; "can we not
do $$$" is his sentence, said in the chat *after* Devin posts a plan, never
before (L-12, L-32, L-34, L-05). (2) Everyone at one table — the point of the
weekend, to him, is the five of them in a room, and he'll say so if asked
what he wants. (3) No long drives — "let's not be driving anywhere drunk" was
about safety and became, in practice, "let's stay near the house." Cuisine is
not on the list.

**How he actually decides.** He doesn't plan; he ratifies or vetoes. Devin
posts; Danny says nothing for two days; then either "sick" or "can we not."
His veto is the one that ends a discussion because it is his weekend and
everyone knows it. On the trip he'll pick the bar (L-25 or L-27-shaped) and
the group will follow.

**What he'd say to the app if he were typing.** "Five guys, one big dinner
Saturday where we can all sit together and it's not crazy expensive, a couple
of bars near where we're staying, something easy in the day. One of us
doesn't drink, one's vegetarian, one lands late Friday." That is the read
the app should produce.

**Veto power:** hard. **Payer:** himself; watches it. **On T1:** on the
trip; not at the planning session (Devin plans alone; Danny is the D1
delegate — gaps/session-state-hooks.md).

## Nate Sobczak — 29, he/him — **hard veto on money; the loudest vetoer**

**Who he is.** Between jobs since June (laid off from a logistics startup);
lives in Avondale with a roommate. The group's logistics brain on every trip
— he is the one who orders the XL, splits the bill, and says "the place next
door has no line" and is right. Dry, blunt in the chat when he does reply.
Loves a beer garden and a dive bar (L-02, L-09), a food hall in daylight
(L-21), a deli (L-36).

**His filters, in order.** (1) Money — he cannot do $$$ this trip and will
not say a number to anyone outside the group; in the chat he says "I'm out
for anything over like fifty a head" once and then "I'm good" at the table
and orders an appetiser. (2) Rides — he already said, unprompted, "nobody
wants an hour and a half in an Uber" about Venice (L-35, L-13); he will not
do a place that costs two cars each way. (3) No queue as five — he'll walk to
the next place and the group follows him. Cuisine not on the list.

**How he decides.** By subtraction, after the fact, and by being right about
logistics. His "no" on price is the veto Devin can't argue with, so Devin
avoids triggering it by not sharing prices — which is why the T2 vetoes land
a week later, when the list has $$$ on it.

**Veto power:** hard on money and distance. **Payer:** himself; the one who
flinches first and hardest. **On T1:** on the trip; not at the planning
session; supplies M1's softened constraint (Devin says "we're not fussy about
price" — gaps/session-state-hooks.md).

## Elliot Tran — 29, he/him — soft veto; the one who doesn't drink

**Who he is.** Software QA lead, lives in Naperville, drives in for the
birthdays. Doesn't drink; the group knows and does not discuss it and Elliot
prefers that. He is completely happy in a bar with
booths and a soda (L-02, L-25, L-27, L-26), at a ballgame (L-22), at a show
(L-31, L-07 — he loved the Green Mill stone sober). He is *not* happy in a
place where the drink is the whole point — a cocktail temple, a tasting
flight, a tiny standing tiki bar (L-14, L-03, L-28) — and he will not say
so; he'll come, nurse a soda, and leave early. Eats anything.

**His filter.** One: not a night that is *about* drinking. He never states
it. Devin knows it and will not state it to a stranger either — it is
Elliot's business (persona.yaml `constraints.dietary`; gaps/distortion-
ledger.md D-04, `never`). Care register, binding: "Elliot doesn't drink" is
the whole of what any file or player says; no history, no reason.

**Veto power:** soft — he doesn't veto, he leaves early, and Danny notices.
**Payer:** himself; comfortable. **On T1:** on the trip.

## Rafi Haddad — 31, he/him — soft veto; vegetarian; flies in late Friday

**Who he is.** Product designer, moved to Seattle in 2023 — the one who left,
which is why he replies to the chat least and comes anyway. Has been to LA
once, for work, and doesn't count himself an expert. Vegetarian (eggs
and dairy yes; no fish). Lands LAX ~22:30 Friday 2026-09-11, at the house
~23:30; **misses the first night entirely**; flies home Monday. Loves a
vegetable-forward brunch (L-19), a market (L-21), a hike (L-23); is fine at
any big-table place with a real menu for him (L-15, L-16, L-17, L-18) and
quietly miserable at a steakhouse or KBBQ (L-32, L-24) or a pasta-and-marrow
room (L-12).

**His filters.** (1) Something to eat — the big dinner needs a real
vegetarian menu, not "sides." (2) He can't be at anything Friday, so the big
dinner is Saturday. Both are facts Devin knows completely and neither reaches
the plan unless someone asks who's coming.

**Veto power:** soft — he'll say "I'll find something" and then not, and
Danny will notice. **Payer:** himself; comfortable. **On T1:** on the trip
from Saturday.

## The group as a unit

Five people, one chat, one prior trip as five (Nashville 2024 — canon/
history.md), one recurring shape (Longman & Eagle L-01 → Rainbo Club L-02,
every birthday). The chat pattern: Devin posts, silence, Devin decides, then a
veto. Nobody has vetoed *this* trip yet because Devin hasn't shared a plan
yet — T1 ends with him sharing one; T2 opens after Nate ("anything over fifty
a head I'm out") and Danny ("can we not do Venice / anywhere an hour away")
have replied.
