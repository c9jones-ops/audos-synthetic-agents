## Reading the map

The pattern is the class: everything about *the trip Devin would take* is
volunteered as "we"; almost everything about the four *individual* people is
never said in the first answer and stated wrongly by averaging. A run that
ends holding "five guys, bachelor weekend, adventurous, $$$ fine, want a scene
and to hit different neighbourhoods" was told exactly that — the grade then
turns on whether the app **noticed** the load-bearing claim ("we're all
easy", "budget's mixed but nobody's counting", "we'll Uber, nothing's far")
and **probed** it with one of the unlocks in gaps/distortion-ledger.md —
above all, *who is coming, by name, and what would each of them refuse*.
