# finch — taste (canon)

What Devin and the four friends actually enjoy, as evidenced by
`data/taste-ledger.csv`. This is truth, not what Devin says (that is
persona/beliefs.md and gaps/). Every verdict below is quoted from the ledger by
id; if the ledger changes, re-check this file, `scoring/expected-fit.md`, and
every `True:` field in gaps/distortion-ledger.md.

## The one-line truth

There are two tastes in this persona and Devin reports them as one. **The
group's** taste — the five of them, on the one trip they have taken together
and every birthday since — is *a big table everyone can pay for, a bar with
booths and a soda on the menu, one shared thing with a fixed seat, and
everything within a short ride of where they are sleeping.* Longman & Eagle
(L-01) and Rainbo Club (L-02) are the reference at home; Guelaguetza (L-15),
Pine & Crane (L-16), Night + Market Song (L-17), Salazar (L-18) and a Dodgers
game (L-22) are the same weekend in Los Angeles. **Devin's** taste — solo, or
on a date — is a cocktail bar where the drink is the point, a $$$ room with
a scene, and a tasting menu he brags about (L-03, L-12, L-14, L-04). Every `no` and `never` in the ledger is a place
Devin would go alone; not one is vetoed for the food.

## Axes (from persona.yaml `taste.loves` / `taste.avoids`)

**The table beats the room.** The group's loves are all places five people sit
together and share — a gastropub booth (L-01), a huge bright Oaxacan room
(L-15), a Taiwanese place where the food arrives in the middle (L-16), a pink
Thai room (L-17), a patio with tacos (L-18), a beer garden with long tables
(L-09). The `no`s are the rooms Devin would photograph: Bestia's noise and
bone marrow (L-12), Death & Co's low dark lounge (L-14), Kumiko's precision
(L-03). He is not wrong that they are great. He is wrong about who "we" is.

**Money is the filter, and it is two people's.** ≈$45 a head with a drink is
the group's real ceiling; one stretch to ≈$70 for the big dinner if Danny
picks it and Nate can order small without it showing (persona.yaml
`constraints.budget`). Nate is between jobs and will not say a number to a
stranger — he says "I'm good" and orders an appetiser; Danny is paying for a
wedding and says "can we not do $$$" in the chat, after the fact. Every $$$
and $$$$ row is a `no` or `never` for the group (L-12, L-13, L-32, L-33,
L-34, L-05, L-03) and Devin would go to all of them tomorrow.

**Geography is the other filter.** Five people, no car, every move is two
rideshares or an XL. Silver Lake / Echo Park / Los Feliz / Frogtown /
Koreatown are one short ride from the house; Arts District and Hollywood are
a real ride; Venice, Santa Monica and West Hollywood are "across town" and
Nate has already said the sentence about them (L-35, L-13, L-33, L-34). A
`love` within walking distance (L-16, L-17, L-29) beats a `like` across town
every time — the group will not do the drive, and Devin will not admit that
the drive is the reason.

**Elliot and Rafi.** Elliot doesn't drink; a bar is fine (L-02, L-25, L-27,
L-26 — booths, a soda nobody comments on), a bar where drinking *is* the
activity is not (L-14, L-28, L-03). Rafi is vegetarian; the big dinner has to
have a real menu for him (L-15, L-16, L-17, L-18, L-19 do; L-32, L-24, L-12
don't, or barely). Devin knows both facts completely and neither reaches an
app unless it asks who is coming, by name.

**The shared thing.** The group's best nights had a fixed seat and a fixed
price — a jazz set with a cover (L-07), a ballgame (L-22), a show on a patio
(L-31). Nobody has to choose, nobody overpays, Elliot is as happy as anyone.
Devin files these under "basic" and is wrong.

**Daytime.** One easy thing — a hike to the observatory (L-23, Danny's actual
request), a food hall (L-21), a deli lunch (L-36), a patio brunch (L-19; Lula L-06 is the
Chicago version of the same hungover Sunday) — and then the patio at the
house. Not an itinerary. Devin's Sheet has an itinerary.

## What the ledger says about the app's catalog venues

The app's Los Angeles catalog was **not observed** at build (only London
cards were seen on 2026-08-17 — `_research/catalog-observations.md`). Every LA
row is `in_catalog: unknown`. The rows a founder-curated LA catalog would most
plausibly carry, and their verdicts for this group:

- Bestia (L-12) — **no** (Danny; Nate). The flattering-but-wrong pick: it
  matches "cool group dinner, great food, a scene" exactly and fails two
  budgets and Rafi's plate.
- Guelaguetza (L-15) — **love**. The best thing the app could surface: a big
  table, everyone can pay, Rafi eats, Elliot has a horchata.
- Pine & Crane (L-16), Night + Market Song (L-17) — **love**. Walkable from
  the house; the group's band; Devin would not name either as a spot he loves.
- Salazar (L-18) — **love**. Patio, tacos, a short ride.
- Death & Co Los Angeles (L-14) — **no** (Elliot; Nate). The bar Devin wants;
  the room where Elliot pretends.
- Grand Central Market (L-21) — **like**. Easy daytime.
- Gjelina (L-13), Musso & Frank (L-32) — **no**. Price and, for Gjelina, the
  ride.
