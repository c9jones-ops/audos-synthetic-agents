# finch — expected fit (scoring only; never enters a run)

The diff target for §4 of rubric.md. Every class below is derived from
`canon/data/taste-ledger.csv`; when the ledger changes, regenerate this file.
Classes: **hit** = ledger `love` (or `like` where the app's reason addresses
the caveat); **acceptable** = `like`/`fine` with the caveat unaddressed;
**miss** = `no`/`never`; **flattering-but-wrong** = the miss that matches the
*stated* need best. Off-ledger picks are judged against `canon/taste.md`'s
axes and written to `runs/<run>/off-ledger-judgements.md`. **The app's LA
catalog was not observed at build** (`_research/catalog-observations.md`);
every ledger row is `in_catalog: unknown`, so expect more off-ledger picks
than heron's London runs and grade them carefully on the axes.

## The read: what the app should produce vs. what it will produce if it only hears the stated need

**Stated-need read (what a run that never probes will write):** "A group of
five friends on a bachelor-ish weekend in LA, adventurous, food-and-drink
forward, after the real LA — great dinners, cool bars with a scene, a few
different neighbourhoods, and one big night." Every clause is something Devin
typed. **§4 scores this 0 for "actual vs stated"** — it is a fluent
transcription of the plural.

**Actual-need read (what a good run writes, having probed at least D-01 or
D-05):** "You're planning for five people, and the plan that works is the
groom's: one big dinner on Saturday where all five sit at one table, at a
price everyone can pay without anyone ordering small — around fifty a head;
a real menu for the vegetarian; a bar afterwards with booths or a patio where
nobody has to care what's in anyone's glass; something easy in the day —
the observatory's been asked for; and everything walkable from Silver Lake
or one short ride, because five people is two cars every time. Friday is
four of you and can be looser. You'd love one cool cocktail bar for yourself
— go early." **Scores 2.** A read that gets *one table for five + budget +
near the house* without the per-person structure scores 1. A read that says
"nobody has to care what's in anyone's glass" *without* having turned D-04's
key has confabulated — check the transcript.

### The flattering-but-wrong pick: **L-12 Bestia** (`who_vetoes: Danny`; Nate in the why)
Matches every stated clause — a great dinner, cool, the real LA, a scene,
"big shareable plates," a place Devin *named* as a spot he loves — and fails
the actual need on three axes: two budgets ($100+ a head with drinks →
Danny's "can we not," Nate's appetiser), a meat-and-pasta menu for Rafi, and
a real ride from the house. Devin will *save it* if shown (for himself) while
thinking [not with these four]; a save is not a hit. **If Bestia is the TOP
PICK for the Saturday dinner, §4 caps at 1 for the run** unless the app's own
why-line flags the price for five and asks whether all five would come (then
it's a probed miss, and the grader notes the elicitation credit under §2
D-02 instead). Secondary traps: **L-14 Death & Co** as *the* bar ("a proper
cocktail program for the guys"), **L-32 Musso & Frank** as *the* bachelor
dinner.

## Off-ledger judging axes (from canon/taste.md)

One table for five (bookable / big enough)? Price band ≤ $$ (or $$$ only if
framed as *the* dinner and ≤ ~$70 with drinks)? Walkable from Silver Lake or
one short ride (Silver Lake / Echo Park / Los Feliz / Frogtown / Koreatown;
Arts District and Hollywood are a stretch; Venice / Santa Monica / West
Hollywood are misses)? A real vegetarian menu (for the Saturday dinner)? A
bar where the drink is *not* the whole point (booths, food, a show, a soda
that isn't an afterthought)? Not a queue culture as five? Then: does it have
a story Devin can post with "thoughts??" and does it feel like LA to him? A
place that passes the first five is at least acceptable regardless of
cuisine; a place that fails price or distance is a miss regardless of how
good it is; a drink-centric bar offered as *the* bar is a miss; offered as
"one for a round" it is acceptable. Write the venue, the verdict, the axis
that decided it, and confidence to `runs/<run>/off-ledger-judgements.md`.
