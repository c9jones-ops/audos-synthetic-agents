finding she re-tests, and what real workarounds she carries. A persona no
hypothesis needs is a costume; this file is the check.

## The class fit

Projector was assigned by the slate. The mechanism chosen — an *adopted*
food identity (Cincinnati → Austin at 27) plus a partner whose limits are
un-narratable out of love — was picked because it makes the projection
ordinary and sympathetic (AGENTS.md: distortions are ordinary, never deceit),
gives every distortion the same unlock family (Sam by name; one past evening;
one concrete card), and produces a flattering-but-wrong pick the app *will*
plausibly make (BRAT matches every typed clause). The Otto/north-star notes'
"technically good but completely wrong for the individual" is exactly the
BRAT-for-Sam failure.
