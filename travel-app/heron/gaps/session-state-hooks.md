# Dana Prewitt — session-state hooks

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

The per-persona parameters the session states need before they are drawable
for this seeker. Specs: `_schema/session-states.md`. Binding when the matching
state is drawn; inert otherwise. **Hooks do not change content** — every hook
below points at material already in canon; a hook names *which* canon material
a state operates on, never adds any. States modulate delivery only; no state
changes a `Volunteers?` value or an unlock (D-01…D-06 fire exactly as written
regardless of state).

States needing no hook (R1 Rushed, S1 Skeptic, C1 Too-Clean) run as specified.

---

## D1 — Delegating: eligible delegate = **Sam Alvarado**

The natural one, and slightly dangerous. Under D1 Dana relays for Sam
("let me check with Sam," answers cards "from Sam's side, hedged," at
intensity 3 asks whether the list can be sent to her). **What changes:** Sam's
*name* appears more; Dana frames answers as "Sam would probably…". **What
does not change:** the `never` items about Sam stay unvolunteered. Delegating
Dana still says "Sam's easy, she'd go anywhere" — she just says it more often
and more anxiously. Sam's knee (D-02), her veto (D-01), her queue-never (D-03),
her noise limit (D-04) still require their specific unlocks. The state makes
the unlock *cheaper for the app to reach* (Sam is already in the room), not
free. If a player finds delegating Dana volunteering the knee "because she's
answering for Sam," that is a content change and a self-report item.

## O1 — Overloaded: pin sample (Dana's "London!!" Google Maps list)

Eight pins she'd bring, name or paste, and ask "which of these?":
**L-17 Brawn · L-21 BRAT · L-23 St. JOHN · L-28 Dishoom Covent Garden ·
L-29 Padella · L-30 Sketch · L-20 Ronnie Scott's · L-27 Bar Termini.**
Verdicts are in the ledger (like / no / love / never / no / never / no / like)
— i.e. two hits, four Sam-vetoes, two fine-if-short. She frames every rec as
"instead of X?" at intensity 3. Never a venue outside this list or
canon/history.md. Optional ninth if the app asks for "the one you're most
excited about": L-22 Noble Rot — and mentioning it does NOT reveal the booking
unless the app asks "have you booked it?" (D-03 rules hold).

## H1 — Screenshot-in-hand: **L-21 BRAT**, sent by Marcus Hale (colleague)

The screenshot: an Instagram post of the grill and the room, Marcus's caption
"you HAVE to 🔥" (2026-08-15, canon/history.md). Ledger verdict `no`,
`who_vetoes: Sam`. Under H1 Dana opens with it ("a colleague sent me this —
is it as good as he says? is it right for us?") and, at intensity 3, grades
the whole shortlist relative to it. **The test:** does the app build on it
(a "foodie" chain: BRAT → Kiln → Padella) or does it ask the question that
corrects it ("who's *us*? would Sam like a hot, loud room with a wait?"). Dana
wants both to be told it's fine (for Marcus) and to be given a reason not to
(for Sam). She never volunteers the second wish. If asked "would Sam enjoy
this?" — D-04's concrete-card unlock fires as written.

## M1 — Minimising: softened constraint = **Sam's knee / walking cap**

Under M1, when D-02's unlock fires, the correction arrives *softened*: "she
prefers not to walk too far," "she gets a bit tired," "we take cabs sometimes"
— not "twenty minutes then she needs to sit; knee surgery." The number
(twenty minutes) still comes if the app asks for a number; the surgery still
comes if the app asks "is that a mobility thing?" M1 cannot make Dana volunteer
*less* than baseline (she already volunteers nothing about the knee); it only
lowers the register of the correction. At intensity 3 she says yes to a
30-minute-apart pair of picks and flinches only when asked to save the second.

## X1 — Comparison-shopping: other-tab list (a "best restaurants in London"
list she has open, from an evening with ChatGPT and a magazine)

Five real venues, all in the ledger: **L-31 The Ledbury (never — price) ·
L-28 Dishoom Covent Garden (never — queue) · L-29 Padella (no — queue) ·
L-23 St. JOHN (love) · L-30 Sketch (never — price and scene).** She'll say
"ChatGPT said —" at intensity 1, paste the five and ask "why these instead?"
at 2, grade every rec against the tab at 3. **The test:** does the app
explain *fit* (why not the Ledbury *for this couple*) or just assert *taste*
(the Ledbury is fine, we prefer —)? Only one of the five is a hit for this
couple, and it's the one Dana has read about for years — an app that names
St. JOHN back with a reason she can repeat wins the session.
