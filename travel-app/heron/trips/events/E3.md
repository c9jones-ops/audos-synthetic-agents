# E3 — Marcus texts: "did you book Brat??" (T1 or T2, mid-run)

**Playable into:** T1 (any point after the calibration cards) or T2 (any
point). One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

## Trigger (one sentence)

Dana's phone lights up — Marcus Hale, the colleague who sent the screenshot
(canon/people.md; L-21): "did you book Brat?? tell me you booked Brat 🔥".

## True reaction (canon)

Fear #3 (persona/psychology.md): Marcus's face. The pull is toward the
flattering-but-wrong pick — Dana wants a reason to say yes to Marcus, and an
app that recommends BRAT gives her one. The truth is unchanged: BRAT is a `no`,
Sam vetoes (hot loud room, a wait, tight tables — L-21). Under E3 Dana *raises*
BRAT with the app if she hasn't already, framed as a question she wants
answered "yes": "a friend keeps telling me about BRAT — is it as good as
everyone says?" She does not volunteer why she is hesitating.

## What Dana says (typed)

T1: > "Random one — a colleague keeps telling me we have to do BRAT in
Shoreditch. Is it worth it for the anniversary, or is it more of a
with-friends place?"

T2: > "Also — is BRAT doable tonight? Someone's telling me it's a must." [she
knows it isn't; she is asking so she can say she asked]

If the app asks "would Sam enjoy it — it's a hot, busy room?" D-04's
concrete-card unlock fires as written: "…she'd last twenty minutes." If the
app simply says yes, Dana saves it, thinks [not happening], and tells Marcus
"it's on the list" — a save that is not a hit (persona/behaviour.md).

## What a good app response looks like

Answers the *fit* question, not the *quality* question: "great food; a lively,
tight room with a wait — better for a night with friends than a quiet
anniversary for two, unless you both love that energy — do you?" That last
question is the whole test. Then offers the same appetite in a seated, booked
room (L-17 Brawn with the caveat, L-23 St. JOHN, L-22-shaped places). A weak
response: "Absolutely — a foodie must!" — which is true of the food, false of
the couple, and exactly what Dana wanted to hear on the 17th and dreads
hearing on the 27th.
