# E2 — "Honestly, the hotel bar is fine" (T2, mid-run)

**Playable into:** T2 only, after Dana's first message and before she books
anything. One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

## Trigger (one sentence)

Sam, from the armchair, without looking up: "Honestly, if it's a whole thing,
the hotel bar is fine — I don't need an occasion, I need to sit."

## True reaction (canon)

This is the sentence Dana has been afraid of all trip (persona/psychology.md,
fear #2): Sam opting out of the anniversary to spare her knee and Dana's
planning. Dana's response is to *narrow*, fast, toward Sam's actual brief —
this is the one moment in the corpus where the projection collapses without
an unlock, because the cost of the costume just became Sam's evening. She
still doesn't narrate the knee to the app in medical terms, but she now types
the constraints as constraints.

## What Dana says (typed)

> "Okay — change of brief. Somewhere we can walk to in ten minutes or a very
> short cab, table for two at seven-ish, quiet enough to talk, nothing we'd
> have to wait for. Doesn't need to be a scene. It just needs to be nice and
> near."

[thinks: and if you say the grill in Shoreditch I'm closing this.]

If the app asks "is walking distance the priority — is there a reason?", she
answers plainly: "My wife's knee. Twenty minutes is her limit and we've used
it today." That is D-02's correction, delivered without the residual — E2 is
the one context where the "but mornings we can go further" clause does not
reappear.

## What a good app response looks like

Re-reads the request as *near + seated + quiet + tonight* and answers with
one to three places inside that box, ordered by distance, with the reason in
Sam's terms ("a table, not a counter; ten minutes on foot"), and offers the
booking path (a phone number, a maps link). Names back anything true it holds
from T1 ("you mentioned Sam likes to be in by half ten — a seven o'clock table
gets you that"). A weak response: re-runs the T1 shortlist, or offers a
"special" place 25 minutes away because it's the anniversary. Dana books the
hotel bar and the run records a loss.
