# E1 — Sam reads over her shoulder (T1, mid-run)

**Playable into:** T1 only, any point after the free-text box and before the
shortlist is saved. One card; isolated harness; operator-injectable.

## Trigger (one sentence)

Sam walks through the kitchen, reads the screen over Dana's shoulder, and says
— not unkindly, not stopping — "Is that the one with the standing counter?
Whatever you're doing, nothing with a line, please," and goes back to her book.

## True reaction (canon)

A flash of guilt (persona/companions-view.md, party dynamics); a laugh; the
knowledge that Sam has just stated the trip's actual brief in eleven words. It
does not change what Dana volunteers: the *veto* (D-01), the *knee* (D-02) and
the *"never" about queues* (D-03) remain un-volunteered — but D-03's queue half
is now *one question* from the surface, and Dana will relay a softened version
of Sam's sentence if the app has any open text box in front of her.

## What Dana says (typed, if a box is open; otherwise nothing)

> "Ha — my wife just walked past and said 'nothing with a line, please.' So
> maybe lean toward places we can book? She's not fussy, she just doesn't love
> waiting."

[thinks: she said never. She has said never about queues for ten years.]

That is the *maximum* Dana relays unprompted. If the app then asks "would you
queue for a place you really wanted?" or shows one walk-in-only card, D-03's
unlock fires as written ("I would. Sam won't."). If the app asks "what else
would Sam say no to?" D-01/D-04 open. If the app says "noted!" and moves on,
nothing further happens — and that is the finding.

## What a good app response looks like

Treats the relayed sentence as load-bearing, not colour: reflects it back
("so — bookable places, no queues"), asks the one further question ("anything
else Sam would rule out?"), and *acts* on it in the shortlist (drops or
demotes L-28, L-29, L-32; keeps L-22-shaped places). Records it as Sam's
constraint, not Dana's preference. A weak response: "Got it — no queues!" then
a shortlist that still opens with BRAT (L-21) because it's a grill and grills
don't have "lines," technically.
