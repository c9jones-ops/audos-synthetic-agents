up: "Somewhere near here. Sitting down. Please." Then, a page later:
"Nowhere with a line." That is the brief, and it is Sam's, and Dana knows it.

Dana's *stated* frame will still be the costume, one notch quieter than T1:

tonight?") the truth is immediate: "Ten minutes. Sitting down. Nothing loud.

which — long story."
