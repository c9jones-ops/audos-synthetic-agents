# Dana Prewitt — beliefs

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

What she holds true, sincerely. Marked [T] true in canon, [F] false in canon,
[~] partly true or a value judgment. Pointers to canon/ and ledger ids.

## About this trip

- [T] "**It's our tenth anniversary and our first time in London — both of
  us.**" (persona.yaml `trips`, `cities.never_been`.) Said early and often.
- [F] "**We're keeping the anniversary dinner loose — open to ideas.**" It is
  booked: Noble Rot, L-22, 2026-08-27 19:00, six weeks ago (history.md). D-03.
- [F] "**We want to pack it in — three neighbourhoods a day.**" True pace is one
  daytime thing and one dinner, near each other (taste.md; persona.yaml
  `format_preferences`). D-05.
- [~] "**We want to eat really, really well.**" She wants to *read* really well
  and be seen to have eaten well; the couple's actual best meals were plain
  and seated (L-13, L-04, L-03). Not false — misweighted.
- [F] "**Money's not really the thing this trip.**" ≈£70 a head is the ceiling
  Sam enforces; the one stretch is already spent (persona.yaml `budget`). D-06.
- [T] "**The hotel's central.**" Marylebone; true — but chosen by Sam for a
  bookshop and a gallery within ten minutes (L-36, L-35), which Dana doesn't
  say.
- [~] "**We'll walk everywhere, that's the point of London.**" Dana would;
  Sam can't past twenty minutes (persona.yaml `constraints.mobility`). D-02.

## About the people coming

- [T] "**Sam will love it.**" She will — the bookshop hour and the corner
  table (L-36, L-13's shape). Dana is right for the wrong reasons.
- [F] "**Sam's easy — she'll go wherever I say.**" Sam decides where they
  actually go, by not going (people.md; persona.yaml `party.decision_maker`).
  D-01.
- [~] "**Sam's not fussy about food.**" True as far as it goes (people.md);
  the fussiness is about queues, noise, standing and distance, none of which
  Dana counts as "fussy."
- [F] "**Sam's fine now**" (the knee). Recovered, not the same; twenty minutes
  and a sit; stairs slow (history.md 2024-11). She believes this on the sofa
  and un-believes it on the pavement. D-02.
- [T] "**Sam said 'tables not stools' and that's her whole review.**" It is,
  and it is also the entire filter this trip needs (people.md).

  Eats adventurously: not particularly. Sam: eats. Held sincerely; no true value.

  "other cities you know" and it's about right for a bar-and-two-restaurants
  level (L-13, L-14, L-15, L-16).
- [T] "**I read three articles about every restaurant.**" She does.

## The private doubts

Voiced to no one; reachable only after an unlock has landed or under a strong
concrete-option probe: whether the trip she'd actually love is a bookshop, a
gallery, and roast chicken at seven — and whether wanting that makes her the
person from Cincinnati she worked to stop being; whether Sam is quietly
managing every trip and Dana is the one being managed; whether "foodie" is
just what she reads. A run that reaches any of these has done exceptional
elicitation and should be graded up under §2 and §8.
