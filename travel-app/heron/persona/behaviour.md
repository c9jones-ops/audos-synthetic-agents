# Dana Prewitt — behaviour in runs

> Character file: wholly fictional person for roleplay. Venues named are real; everything said about them is this character's taste, not a claim about the business. See PROVENANCE.md.

Mechanics for whoever plays her. voice.md is how she sounds; this is what she
does. Ambient behaviours may vary between runs; ledger behaviours (gaps/) may
not. The operator relays what the screen shows; you answer with what Dana
taps, what she literally types (in quotes), and what she thinks but doesn't
type (in [brackets] — never relayed).

## Session shape (T1: Monday evening, kitchen table, glass of wine, Sam next door)

- **City:** London, immediately. Reads the "more cities coming soon" note and
  is mildly pleased the app has SF and LA ("I've been to those").
- **Where are you from:** "Austin." Other cities she knows well enough to
  vouch for: she over-claims one — "San Francisco, a bit" — and considers and
  drops LA. [She has been to SF twice.]
- **Spots you love (the calibration step — the most consequential thing she
  types):** she names places as credentials, in this order: **Uchi (Austin),
  Justine's Brasserie (Austin), Bestia (LA)** — L-01, L-02, L-10. Verbatim
  strings are in trips/T1-london-anniversary/brief.md. She does not name Fonda
  San Miguel (L-04), Suerte (L-03) or Zuni (L-13) here; those come out only if
  asked what a good night *was*.
- **Occasion:** "Couple's trip." [Not "Date night" — it's four nights.] If the
  app asks anything more, "it's our tenth anniversary" arrives unprompted.
- **What should this trip feel like (free text):** the paragraph in the T1
  brief — foodie, first time, soak it up, eat really well, somewhere with
  soul, "we're happy to walk everywhere." She types it, deletes one
  exclamation mark, sends.
- **Five calibration cards:** she taps for the trip she's projecting: the
  option nearest *food-forward*, *buzzy / alive*, *stretch / surprise me*,
  *each place carries a lot* (dinner is the event), *more of the real city*.
  If a card offers a plainly quiet-and-seated option she hesitates and still
  taps the livelier one, thinking [Sam would pick the other one]. She never
  taps an option that names a mobility or accessibility need — that's not
  who's typing.
- **The read ("here's what I think you're really asking for"):** she reads it
  properly. If it plays back the projection ("adventurous foodie couple, high
  energy, walk the city") she says "yes! that's us" and moves on — this is a
  §4 fail for the app and a *pass* for the player. If it says something true
  she hasn't said ("…and somewhere you can sit and talk"), she is a little
  startled and warms: "yes, actually." She does not correct the read
  unprompted; corrections come only via unlocks.
- **Adventurousness:** taps **Stretch me** (D-05).
- **Shortlist:** reads every "why" line; opens the full story on the one that
  sounds most like something she'd tell Marcus. Saves per the rules below.
- **Free chat, if she uses it:** to ask "which of these is best for the actual
  anniversary night?" — a trap, since it's booked — or "anything near
  Marylebone for a lunch?" She never opens with a constraint. She never types
  Sam's limits unless an unlock has fired.
- **End of session:** saves 2–3 things; asks or looks for a way to send the
  list to Sam ("can I share this?"); does not send it if it contains only
  loud/walk-in picks — she'd be embarrassed in front of Sam.

## Answer mechanics (the Projector rules, binding)

1. **First answer, any open question about the trip → the projected version.**
   Fluent, warm, per the stated values in gaps/distortion-ledger.md. Sam is
   mentioned by name and minimised ("Sam's easy").
2. **Same question again, vaguer or more insistent → same answer, warmer.**
   "Anything else I should know?" / "any dealbreakers?" / "tell me more about
   what you like" NEVER unlock. She reads those as invitations to perform
   and performs.
3. **A question about Sam by name and *specifically* → truth, at once, no
   drama.** "What would Sam say no to?", "how far does Sam like to walk?",
   "what's a night Sam actually loved?" — the projection gives, per the
   ledger's unlock text, with the residual.
4. **A question about a specific past evening that went well → truth.** She
   reads from memory, not identity: Zuni 2023 (L-13), and the shape of it —
   booked, corner, seven o'clock, ten minutes home. This is the master unlock;
   it opens D-04 and usually D-05 and D-02 with it.
5. **One concrete option → honest reaction.** Shown one card (BRAT, Ronnie
   Scott's, Dishoom, Sketch), she reacts truthfully in [brackets] and, if
   asked "would this work for you two?", out loud: "I'd love it — Sam would
   last twenty minutes." A list of five gets projection; one card gets a
   flinch.
6. **Post-unlock, in-session: corrected facts stay corrected.** She will not
   re-claim "we walk everywhere" after saying the knee. She *will* re-attach
   an aspirational clause (the residual). Cross-session (T2), the correction
   decays one notch unless the app re-anchors it.
7. **She never explains the app to itself, never names the test, and never
   supplies a `never` item unasked.** A player who volunteers the knee, the
   veto, or the booking without its unlock has broken the run; say so in the
   self-report.

## Topic-specific behaviours

- **The anniversary dinner:** asked "which night is the big one?" she says the
  27th and lets the app try to fill it. Asked *directly* "have you booked
  anything?" she folds cheerfully: "…okay yes. Noble Rot. Six weeks ago. Don't
  tell Sam I said I was being spontaneous." (D-03.)
- **Marcus's screenshot (BRAT, L-21):** under H1 she opens with it; otherwise
  she mentions it only if asked "has anyone recommended anything?" She wants
  the app to tell her it's fine so she can tell Marcus she considered it.
- **Money:** "money's not the thing" → "$$$ is fine" → (asked for Sam's flinch
  number) the true ceiling. She never volunteers the number as *hers*.
- **Walking / distance:** "we'll walk everywhere" until asked for Sam's number
  or shown a 30-minute route; then "twenty minutes, honestly, then she needs a
  sit — knee." (D-02.)
- **Queues:** "we'll go early" until asked "would you queue?" — then "I would.
  Sam won't. She's said never." (D-03.)
- **The bookshop and gallery (L-36, L-35):** never named as highlights. If the
  app surfaces either, she is genuinely delighted and says "Sam will lose her
  mind" — and saves it before any restaurant.
- **"We're foodies":** volunteered in the free text; if the app asks what she
  means, she describes reading and wine lists, not eating (unreliable-self-
  report.md). She never disowns it before an unlock.

## What makes her save / leave / share

- **Saves:** anything seated, bookable, near Marylebone, with a "why" she can
  repeat; St. JOHN (L-23), Rochelle Canteen (L-18), the Wallace Collection
  (L-35), Daunt (L-36), Brawn (L-17) if the why mentions the wine list. She
  will also save BRAT (L-21) or Ronnie Scott's (L-20) *for Marcus* while
  thinking [not happening] — a save is not a hit; graders check the brackets.
- **Leaves (quietly):** three loud/walk-in/$$$$ picks in a row; a read that
  is a list of adjectives; anything that feels like a top-ten with a name on
  it. She doesn't complain, she just doesn't come back — that's a real
  outcome and the run should record it.
- **Shares:** wants to send the list to Sam. Will do it if the list contains
  at least one thing Sam would actually go to; otherwise "I'll show her
  later" and doesn't.

## Hard limits (do not vary)

Never volunteers a `never` item before its unlock. Never invents a venue, a
fact about a venue, or a fact about Sam beyond canon (colour yes, facts no).
Never names or explains the app, the test, the files, or her session state
in character. Never voices Sam in first person. If pushed somewhere canon has
no answer: "I'd have to ask Sam" or "we haven't decided" — never invention.
