## The one-line truth

The evenings this couple has actually loved share a shape, and the shape is
not a cuisine: **a booked table for two, seated, in a calm corner, one great
dish, and ten minutes back to where they are sleeping.** Zuni Café (L-13) is
the reference; Suerte (L-03), Fonda San Miguel (L-04), and — on paper — Noble
Rot (L-22) and St. JOHN (L-23) are the same evening in different rooms. Every
`no` and `never` in the ledger is vetoed by Sam, and not one of them is vetoed
for the food.

## Axes (from persona.yaml `taste.loves` / `taste.avoids`)

**Format beats cuisine.** Sit-down over standing, every time; a reservation
over a wait, every time (L-05, L-06, L-28, L-29 are all places Dana likes the
food at and the couple will not go to). Small plates are fine seated at a table
with chair-backs (L-08, L-17); counter stools for a whole dinner are not
(L-32, L-16).

**Rooms.** Old-fashioned rooms with a story — plain white (L-23), tiled (L-24),
panelled — over designed rooms; a courtyard for lunch (L-18, L-14, L-35). Loud
is the disqualifier: a room where you lean in to talk is a room Sam goes quiet
in and then leaves (L-20, L-21, L-10, L-32). Dana half-likes buzz and half
knows better; her real preference, when the evening is going well, is quiet
enough to hear the wine list read aloud.

**Food.** Straight Italian (L-25, L-26), Mexican done properly (L-03, L-04),
French-brasserie mood (L-02, L-11), British "plain and good" (L-23, L-22).
Roast chicken for two is the platonic dish (L-13). Dana reads menus and wine
lists for pleasure and likes a list she can spend time in (L-22, L-17). Sam
eats what is in front of her, dislikes very spicy food but eats around it, and
would rather the food be *good and quick to decide* than remarkable.

**Pace and distance.** One daytime thing and one dinner a day, close to each
other or close to the hotel; back by ~22:30. About 20 minutes on foot between
stops (persona.yaml `constraints.mobility`); benches matter (L-37, L-35). A cab
is chosen over a walk without discussion (L-26). Stairs count against a place
(L-07, L-33).

**Money.** $$$ is "a proper dinner" and Dana is drawn to it; $$$$ is Sam's
flinch and, if Dana is honest, her own (L-30, L-31). ≈$90/£70 a head with a
drink is the normal ceiling; one stretch to ≈$160/£120 for the anniversary,
already spent on L-22.

**The real highlight surface.** Sam is a librarian. A bookshop (L-36) and a
quiet gallery with benches (L-35) within ten minutes of the hotel are the parts
of this trip Sam will talk about for a year — not the dinners. Dana would not
put it that way but she knows it, which is why the hotel is in Marylebone.
