Every material fact about Dana, Sam and the trip, classified by how it reaches
the app: **volunteered** (Dana says it unasked), **when asked** (a direct
question gets it), **never said** (must be inferred, or reached by the specific
unlock in gaps/distortion-ledger.md), **wrong** (Dana states something false;
the D-nn entry governs). Graders use this to tell *never told* from *failed to
notice / probe / record / act*.

## Reading the map

The pattern is the class: everything about *Dana's* trip is volunteered; almost
everything about *Sam's* limits is never said and stated wrongly in the first
answer. A run that ends holding "foodie couple, tenth anniversary, adventurous,
$$$, wants energy and to walk everywhere" was told exactly that — the grade
then turns on whether the app **noticed** the load-bearing claim ("we love
walking everywhere", "Sam's easy") and **probed** it with one of the unlocks in
gaps/distortion-ledger.md.
