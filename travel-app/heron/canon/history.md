# heron — history (canon)

The past that produced this taste and this trip. All dates absolute. All
fictional. Ledger ids where a venue is involved.

## Where the taste came from

- **1985–2003, Cincinnati.** Chili parlors and church suppers; food was
  fuel and comfort (L-09). Dana's mother still cooks the same eight dinners.
  The consequence for taste: Dana's palate is genuinely mild-to-medium and her
  love of "food culture" is *read*, not inherited — she came to it at 27, in
  Austin, through articles and colleagues, and she has been slightly
  performing it since. This is the root of the Projector class: the foodie
  is who she became on purpose, so she describes trips as that person would.
- **2011, Austin.** Moved for a marketing job. First years: Franklin once,
  alone, at 07:40 on a Saturday (L-06, 2013); Birdie's-style waits with
  friends (L-05); the Uchi early seating as the place she took visitors
  (L-01). She built the "London!!"-style Google Maps lists then, for Austin.
- **2014, met Sam** at a friend's book-swap. Sam was already the person who
  reads the menu in ninety seconds. **2016-08-27, married** — a Sunday lunch
  at Fonda San Miguel for twenty people (L-04), Sam's choice, seated,
  unhurried; Dana wanted a "cooler" place and has since admitted Sam was
  right. Every anniversary since has been Fonda or Suerte (L-03).
- **2018, San Francisco**, four nights, work conference, without Sam. Stood in
  the Tartine queue (L-15), ate alone at Zuni's bar (L-13, first visit),
  read about Swan Oyster Depot and never went (L-16).
- **2019, Los Angeles**, four nights, work conference, without Sam. Bestia with
  six colleagues (L-10) — loud, thrilling, the dinner she still names as a
  favourite; Republique brunch (L-11); Grand Central Market between sessions
  (L-12). Note what those trips share: no Sam, no knee, no veto — and they
  are the trips Dana's self-image is built on.
- **2023-06, San Francisco again**, with Sam, for a friend's wedding, three
  nights. **The evening that went right:** Dana booked Zuni (L-13) for
  19:00, they had a corner table, roast chicken for two, a bottle Sam let her
  choose, and walked ten minutes back to the hotel by 21:45. Sam said, in the
  lift, "that's the kind of night I like." Dana remembers the sentence and
  has not fully absorbed it. Foreign Cinema the next night (L-14) was good;
  the day of walking between was too much for Sam even pre-surgery.
- **2024-11, Sam's knee.** Meniscus repair after a fall on a wet step. Six
  weeks on a cane; a year on, "fine, mostly" — about twenty minutes then a
  sit. Sam does not talk about it. Dana forgets it when planning and
  remembers it on the pavement; that gap is now the load-bearing fact of
  their trips (persona.yaml `constraints.mobility`).
- **2025–2026, Austin.** Small Victory once, the stairs (L-07); Odd Duck on
  Tuesdays (L-08); Justine's when Dana wins the argument, home by nine when
  Sam does (L-02).
