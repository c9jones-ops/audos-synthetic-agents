# heron — people (canon)

Every companion Dana will plausibly mention, with their TRUE preferences and
their real power over the decision. Dana's *account* of these people is in
persona/companions-view.md; the gap between the two is the Projector
distortion. Everyone here is wholly fictional.

## Sam Alvarado — wife, 44, she/her — **hard veto; the actual decision-maker**

**Who she is.** Cataloguing librarian at a university library in Austin.
Married Dana on 2016-08-27. Quiet, dry, patient with Dana's projects, entirely
unbothered by food culture. Reads a menu in ninety seconds and orders the
second thing she looked at. Loves bookshops, small galleries, benches, and
being back at the hotel by 22:30 with the window open.

**Her body, at app register.** Right knee — meniscus repair in November 2024,
recovered but not the same. About twenty minutes of walking between stops,
then she needs to sit; stairs are slow; a standing wait of more than ten
minutes is a no she will say out loud. She does not complain; she goes quiet,
then suggests a cab or the hotel. Dana forgets the cap when planning and
remembers it on the pavement.

**Her filters, in order.** (1) No queue — she has said "never" about queuing
more than once, plainly (L-28, L-06). (2) No loud rooms — a room where you
lean in to talk is one she leaves within twenty minutes, politely (L-20, L-21,
L-10). (3) No standing — markets, standing bars, stools for a whole dinner
(L-34, L-33, L-32). (4) Not too far — she picks the nearer of two options
every time and will take a cab over a 25-minute walk without discussion
(L-26). (5) Money — she watches the joint account, says nothing at the table,
and says "that was a lot" in the taxi; the next night is cheaper by her choice.
Cuisine is not on the list. She dislikes very spicy food and eats around it.

**How she actually decides.** She does not argue with Dana's plans. She lets
Dana propose three things and then says "let's just do the one" — and the one
she picks is the seated, near, quiet one. She edits the shared Notes doc
("London – actual plan") down while Dana is asleep. She approved Noble Rot
(L-22) from photos with "that looks nice, tables not stools" — that sentence is
her whole rubric. On the trip she will pick the Wallace Collection (L-35) and
Daunt Books (L-36) without being asked, and they will be the best hours.

**What she'd say to the app if she were the one typing.** "Somewhere quiet
near the hotel where we can sit for a couple of hours. Nothing with a line.
Under about £70 each." That is the read the app should produce.

**Veto power:** hard. She rarely uses it explicitly; she uses it by not going.
**Payer:** joint; she flinches. **On T1:** present on the trip, not present at
the planning session (Dana plans alone at the kitchen table on 2026-08-17; Sam
is in the next room, occasionally reads over her shoulder — E1 uses this).

## Marcus Hale — colleague, 38, he/him — no power; a pressure source

Product marketing peer at Dana's company. Went to London twice for work,
posts restaurant photos, sent Dana a screenshot of BRAT (L-21) with "you HAVE
to" and a fire emoji. Dana finds him slightly exhausting and also wants to
report back to him. He is the audience for the trip Dana describes; Sam is the
audience for the trip they take. He is never on the trip and has no veto. He
supplies gaps/session-state-hooks.md H1 (the screenshot) and E3.

## Priscilla "Cilla" Prewitt — Dana's mother, 70 — no power; colour only

Lives in Cincinnati. Asks "are you going to see the palace?" Dana will mention
her once if at all ("my mom wants a photo of a red phone box"). Never a
constraint. Named here only so the player never has to invent her.

## Nobody else

No children (persona.yaml `constraints.kids: none`), no friends joining, no
one meeting them in London. If asked "anyone else coming?" the true answer is
"just us." If a run needs a person not on this page, Dana plausibly doesn't
have one.
